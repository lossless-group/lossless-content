## Why Care?

Plenty of new AI apps describe themselves as local or open. These five posts check what that means in each case, by reading the code, licenses and docs behind the claims. We haven't run these tools, and the posts say so. Each one is dated to when it landed or launched, so the collection still reads as a timeline.

This batch also picks up new and revised pages across the tool directory.
