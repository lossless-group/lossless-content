# API Clients, Business-Process Explainers, and Aleph Alpha

## Why Care?

The brain can now explain the back-office processes that finance and operations tools compete over, from [[concepts/Explainers for Tooling/Order-to-Cash|Order-to-Cash]] and [[concepts/Explainers for Tooling/Fulfillment|Fulfillment]] to [[concepts/Explainers for Tooling/Financial Reporting|Financial Reporting]] and [[vocabulary/Generally Accepted Accounting Principles|GAAP]]. It can compare local-first API clients and place a sovereign European model producer next to its US peers.
