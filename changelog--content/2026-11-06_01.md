## Why Care?

A firm page is only useful if you can find it next to its peers. Fourteen venture capital, corporate venture and private-equity firms had ended up in the general `organizations/` folder or in `tooling/Portfolio/`. They now sit with the other investors in [[vertical-toolkits/Alternatives/Venture-Capital-Firms/DCVC|Venture-Capital-Firms]], and every link that pointed at the old path was rewritten, including links inside the `client-content` and `content-areas` submodules.

The same batch adds 95 new pages, ranging from agentic commerce and AI security to atmospheric water generation, plus a new Talent Networks area that gathers Andela, Toptal and similar marketplaces.

## What's New?

**The investor sweep.** These files moved to `vertical-toolkits/Alternatives/Venture-Capital-Firms/`:

- From `organizations/`: 1517 Fund, DCVC, M31 Capital, Gigascale Capital, Matter Venture Partners, Overmatch VC, Unilever Ventures, In-Q-Tel, Olivon Advisors, BlackRock, Blackstone, Rock Health
- From `tooling/Portfolio/`: Avalanche VC, Learn Capital

Corporate venture arms follow the folder's existing practice (Samsung Ventures and Qualcomm Ventures already live there), as do asset managers like Hamilton Lane and Blue Owl. Some candidates stayed put on purpose:

- **VC Labs** is a program for fund managers, not an investor.
- **Novel Capital** is a revenue-based lender, so it stays in FinTech.
- **Ambition Capital** and **Fountain Partners** are only stubs from the Deep Tech Week event map, without enough information yet to classify them.

**Submodules bumped.** `content-areas` gained five private-markets explainers and Robotic Intelligence. `client-content` gained 35 Link Freedom Group cloud and app-builder records, along with Humain notes.
