## Why Care?

The brain can now size and map four markets it couldn't before: video search, agent-native CRMs, custodial robots and AI for biology, each with a sourced market estimate up front. It can explain [[sources/Standards-and-Specs/OAuth 2.0|OAuth 2.0]] from its 2010 origins to agent-to-tool access, and it has a new shelf on how teams actually work, from [[concepts/Team Topologies|Team Topologies]] to [[sources/Books/The Five Dysfunctions of a Team|The Five Dysfunctions of a Team]]. Fourteen more venture firms and a run of FinTech and payments networks fill out the investor and fintech maps.
