# Summary

`content-areas` gains its first new area since August 2025: **AI-Factories-Datacenters**. Separately, the main collections pick up a large batch of organizations, explainers, market categories, events, and tools.

## Why Care?

AI factories and datacenters have become a supply chain of their own. Power, cooling, grid interconnects, construction, colocation, and financing now each have their own operators and their own constraints. A dedicated content area lets us map that stack the way we already map Blue Economy and Finance, instead of scattering it across `concepts/` and `organizations/`.

# Content Updates

## content-areas (submodule)

- **New area `AI-Factories-Datacenters/`** with a README and the standard `Organizations/`, `Concepts/`, `Topics/`, `Issues/`, `Vocabulary/`, and `Sources/` folders.
  - 73 organization profiles covering operators, builders, power and grid companies, and investors (e.g. Equinix, Cologix, NTT Global Data Centers, Eaton, ABB Group, FuelCell Energy, Phaidra, MGX).
  - Concepts: AI Factories, Building Energy Management Systems, Data Center Operators, Datacenter Builders, Smart Grids.
- Blue-Economy: Environmental Management Systems.
- Finance/Private-Markets: Forge Global, Sacra.
- `README.md` now lists the new area.

## content (main collections)

- **Organizations:** 43 new, many from Deep Tech Week Austin and defense/hardware circles (DCVC, Saronic, ICON, Base Power, Neurophos, Nominal, Fleetzero, 1517 Fund, Overmatch VC, and others).
- **Concepts:** new AI explainers (Agent Native, AI Doomerism, Compositional Generalization, Enterprise Automation, High-Density Compute, Image Generators, Surveillance Wages) and market categories (Colocation Services, DCIM Systems, Integrated Design-Build, Integrated Electrical and Mechanical Systems, Manufacturing Automation, Sales AI).
- **Events:** Climate Week NYC 2026, Deep Tech Week Austin TX 2026 (with event map), Yotta 2026 (with event map).
- **Tooling and vertical toolkits:** CyrusOne, DeepInfra, Emerald AI, FlexGen Power Systems, Slashy, Open Design, Rapid API, TeleportHQ, and others. Venture firms Antler, Battery Ventures, and Seedcamp now sit under `vertical-toolkits/Venture-Capital-Firms/`.
- **Vocabulary:** Calls-to-Action, Compute in AI ML, Fabless Manufacturing, Graph Theory, Price Discrimination, Unit Economics.
- About 40 existing files updated, mostly vocabulary and AI-toolkit entries.
- **Moves:** `concepts/AI Factory.md` and `Building Energy Management Systems.md` moved into the new content area. `Image Generator` became `Image Generators`. `Compositional Generalization` moved from vocabulary to concepts. `organizations/Antler.md` moved to the venture-firms toolkit.
