# Six Keeping-Up Posts, and Lost in Public Hands Back Its Engineering Notes

## Why Care?

[Keeping Up](https://lossless.group/keeping-up) is where we write down what changed in the tools we actually use, dated to the release rather than the day we got around to it. Six new posts went up: a framework's major version, two terminal multiplexers built for coding agents, an agent development environment, and two pieces of infrastructure for giving agents search and memory.

The bigger change is subtraction. For two years, Lost in Public doubled as the filing cabinet for our engineering notes: the prompts we handed AI code assistants, the issues we resolved, the reminders, blueprints and refactor plans. Those notes belong next to the code they describe, in each repo's own `context-v/` folder, where the agents working in that repo can actually find them. This push sends more than 200 of them home. Lost in Public keeps what reads as writing for people: the posts, the market maps, and the explorations of things we haven't built yet.

## Where the Engineering Notes Went

| From `lost-in-public/` | Moved | Where they live now |
|---|---:|---|
| prompts | 93 | site (50), tidyverse (20), the monorepo root (8), and the rest in ai-labs, augment-it, content-farm and its plugins, lfm, and three astro-knots sites |
| issue-resolution | 40 | site (21) and tidyverse (8), plus content-farm, lfm, self-host-stack and the monorepo root; most filed as `context-v/issues` |
| reminders | 28 | the monorepo root (9), site (9), tidyverse (5), and content-farm, astro-knots and lfm |
| explorations | 27 | site (7), and the rest across ai-labs, content-farm, astro-knots, lfm, self-host-stack and the Hypernova site; five with no home yet stay here, public |
| blueprints | 13 | site (12); one superseded copy dropped |
| refactors | 6 | site (3) as plans, plus content-farm and the Lossless Changelog site |

Nine specs in `specs/` also moved to the repos that built them: tidyverse, site, content-farm, ai-labs and the client-portals site. The specs that remain are what we're calling Open Specs: ideas we thought through and are unlikely to build, left public because the thinking is free.

Every moved document got standard frontmatter on arrival (a `type`, a citation `hex_code`, a `site_uuid` where one was missing) and a line in its folder's index. Five pairs of documents that shared a `site_uuid`, a leftover from hand-copying files, each got their own. The old URLs on lossless.group redirect, so nothing linked from elsewhere breaks.
