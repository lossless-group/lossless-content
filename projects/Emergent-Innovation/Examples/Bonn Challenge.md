The [Bonn Challenge](https://www.google.com/search?q=bonn+challenge&kgmid=/m/0_x75c_) is a global effort to restore 150 million hectares of degraded and deforested land by 2020 and 350 million hectares by 2030. [^271qn0] 
## Key Details

* Launch: Launched in 2011 in Bonn, Germany, by the [Government of Germany](https://www.bonnchallenge.org/) and the [International Union for Conservation of Nature (IUCN)](https://iucn.org/). [^271qn0] [^uc8xq6] 
* Core Approach: Uses Forest Landscape Restoration (FLR) to balance ecological integrity with human well-being, food security, and rural development. [^4tqp2m] 
* Current Progress: Over 250 million hectares have been pledged across dozens of country and regional commitments worldwide. [^u0dcze] 
* Regional Initiatives: Supported by regional frameworks like AFR100 (Africa), Initiative 20x20 (Latin America/Caribbean), and ECCA30 (Europe, Caucasus, and Central Asia). [^u0dcze]
