# Patrick Collison
![Professional headshot of Patrick Collison, co‑founder and CEO of Stripe](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/az3D8DhyltY/hq720.jpg?sqp=-oaymwEhCK4FEIIDSFryq4qpAxMIARUAAAAAGAElAADIQj0AgKJD&rs=AOn4CLCf-NcS2uYezaxhMH6ivHVustaGAQ)

_Patrick Collison is a technology founder and thinker best known for building Stripe into one of the world’s most influential digital payments platforms and for his broader advocacy of “faster progress” in science and society._

Patrick Collison is a **person**: an Irish-born entrepreneur, programmer, and writer who co-founded Stripe in 2010 with his brother John Collison and has served as its CEO since then. He first drew public attention as a teenage programming prodigy, winning the 41st Young Scientist and Technology Exhibition in Ireland in 2005 before briefly attending [[organizations/Massachusetts Institute of Technology|MIT]] and later leaving to build startups. Stripe launched publicly in 2011 and has since become a foundational payments infrastructure company used by millions of businesses worldwide. Innovation-focused consultants return to Collison for his unusually clear writing on progress, science funding, and institutional design, as well as his track record of building high-leverage infrastructure products.

# The People Behind It

Since the “source” is a person, this section is Patrick Collison’s bio.

- **Origin and early life**
  - Patrick Collison was born in 1988 in Dromineer, County Tipperary / County Limerick region (sources differ slightly on description) in Ireland.  
  - As a teenager, he won Ireland’s prestigious **Young Scientist and Technology Exhibition** in 2005 for a project on artificial intelligence and Lisp, signaling early technical depth.

- **Education**
  - He enrolled at the **Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT)** but left before graduating to pursue startup opportunities.  

- **Key entrepreneurial roles**
  - Co-founded **Auctomatic**, an online auction and marketplace management system, which was acquired by Live Current Media in 2008.  
  - Co-founded **Stripe** with his brother John Collison in 2010; he has been **CEO and Director** of Stripe, Inc. since 2011. [^s10rai]  

- **Current positions and governance roles**
  - Serves as **Chief Executive Officer & Director** at Stripe, Inc. and founder of Stripe Payments International Holdings Ltd. [^s10rai]  
  - Serves as an **Independent Director at [[organizations/Meta|Meta]] Platforms, Inc.**, joining the board in 2020. [^s10rai]  

- **Signature public contributions**
  - Co-initiator (with [[Tyler Cowen]]) of the idea of **“Progress Studies”**, arguing for a more systematic, empirical study of the conditions that accelerate human progress in science, technology, and living standards.  
  - Writes and speaks extensively on science funding (e.g., role of philanthropic science, new research institutions), institutional design, and the importance of “faster progress.”

# Why It Matters to Innovators

- Collison frames **infrastructure as leverage**: Stripe demonstrates how building low-friction, developer-first infrastructure can unlock entire categories of online business, a powerful lens for founders designing platform or **enabling-layer** products rather than point solutions.  
- His essays like **“Fast”** document real-world cases of rapid execution, challenging the assumption that large projects must be slow and offering a mental model of **ambitious, time-compressed projects** that complements ideas like [[concepts/10x Engineering|10x Engineering]] and [[concepts/Operational Excellence|Operational Excellence]].  
- Through writing on **science funding** and **new science institutions**, he provides a practical framework for thinking about **institutional innovation**—how to redesign funding, governance, and incentives to accelerate discovery—directly relevant to founders working on deep tech, biotech, or research-heavy ventures.  
- The **Progress Studies** lens encourages innovators to view progress itself as an object of study, asking what policies, cultures, and organizational structures correlate with faster gains in productivity and welfare—connecting naturally to [[concepts/Progress Studies]] and [[concepts/Long-termism]].  
- As a practicing CEO of a high-growth, global company, his views are grounded in **operational experience**: Stripe’s evolution offers case studies in international expansion, regulation-savvy design, API-first product strategy, and organizational scaling that innovators can adapt to their own contexts.
