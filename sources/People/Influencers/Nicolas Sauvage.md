Nicolas Sauvage is a **person**, best known as the **Founder & President of TDK Ventures**, the corporate venture capital arm of TDK Corporation focused on hardtech and deeptech. # The People Behind It

Since the source is a **person**, this section covers Nicolas Sauvage’s own biography.  

- Nicolas Sauvage is a **French venture capitalist** who became President of **TDK Ventures**, leading TDK Corporation’s global corporate venture capital activities in hardtech and deeptech. [^f6qwrr] [^56wsx0] [^795grl]  
- His journey into venture began at **Stanford in 2018**, where he developed the thesis that corporate venture capital can evolve from purely “strategic” to a force for “scaling impact scalers,” which led to the founding of TDK Ventures in 2019. [^56wsx0] [^795grl]  
- TDK Ventures, under his founding leadership, manages approximately **$500M across four funds**, investing in early-stage startups that transform industries and accelerate decarbonization. [^56wsx0] [^795grl]  
- Prior to TDK Ventures, he held key management roles at **InvenSense**, where he managed strategic ecosystem relationships with companies such as Google and Qualcomm, and earlier at **NXP Software**, where he oversaw global sales and later profit-and-loss management and product strategy in the OEM business line. [^f6qwrr]  
- He serves on the boards of multiple companies, including Analog Inference, AutoFlight, Faction, Mach49, Metalenz, Virewirx, and TDK Ventures itself, reinforcing his role as both investor and operator in deeptech ecosystems. [^f6qwrr]  
- He has been repeatedly recognized in the **Global Corporate Venturing Powerlist**, appearing for at least five consecutive years and ranking #17 among over 4,000 heads of corporate venturing units in the 2022 edition. [^f6qwrr] [^fujq12] [^nb0p6o]  
- In 2026, he was named **chair of the Global Corporate Venturing Leadership Society**, positioning him at the forefront of discussions on corporate venture capital methodologies and standards. [^lgt3an]

# Why It Matters to Innovators

- **Practical frameworks for corporate VC design.** Sauvage’s Stanford-origin thesis—that corporate venture capital can be a mechanism for “scaling impact scalers,” not just tactical strategy—offers innovators a way to design CVC programs that align long-term value creation with impact-focused innovation. [^56wsx0] [^795grl] This directly informs how a corporate can structure an internal venture arm tied to [[concepts/Innovation Portfolio Management]].  
- **Hardtech and deeptech investment lens.** Through TDK Ventures’ focus on early-stage hardtech and deeptech across digital and energy innovations, he surfaces criteria for evaluating frontier technologies and founders, which is crucial for innovators in complex physical domains and climate-tech. [^f6qwrr] [^56wsx0] [^795grl] This connects strongly to [[concepts/Deep Tech Ventures]] and [[concepts/Climate Innovation]].  
- **Backcasting as an investment method.** In explaining TDK Ventures’ use of **backcasting**—starting from desired future states and working backward to present investment decisions—he gives innovators a concrete planning and portfolio tool for building roadmaps to transformative outcomes, useful for [[concepts/Foresight-Driven Strategy]] and [[concepts/Strategic Roadmapping]]. [[concepts/Innovation Foresight|Innovation Foresight]] [^a7dgkm]  
- **Corporate–startup collaboration patterns.** As chair of the Global Corporate Venturing Leadership Society and a frequent interviewer of corporate venturing leaders, he curates patterns for how corporates can structure governance, board roles, and ecosystem partnerships to work productively with startups at scale. [^f6qwrr] [^ksxqi2] [^lgt3an] This is directly relevant to [[concepts/Corporate-Startup Partnerships]].  
- **Risk and time-horizon framing.** Sauvage’s comment that “it takes four years for the best bets to look smart” reframes deeptech and AI investment horizons for corporate stakeholders, encouraging innovators to design programs that can withstand multi-year uncertainty while targeting step-change outcomes. [^56wsx0] This aligns with [[concepts/Patient Capital]] and [[concepts/Long-Term Innovation Bets]].
