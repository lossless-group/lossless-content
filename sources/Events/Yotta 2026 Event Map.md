## Observed themes

- **Power is the bottleneck.** Most of the floor was about power: onsite generation (fuel cells, CHP, nuclear, solar), getting grid capacity faster (GridCARE, Paces, Electric Era), and equipment with shorter lead times (Ayr, Mission Critical Group).
- **The move to 800V DC.** Solid-state transformers and DC conversion from Enphase, Amperesand, CE+T and Vision Group.
- **Liquid cooling is now standard.** Trane bought LiquidStack, XNRGY is reportedly for sale, and startups like Omen AI monitor coolant.
- **Inference neoclouds on alternative silicon.** General Compute (SambaNova), Positron (its own ASIC) and Deep Infra.
- **AI software running data center operations.** Phaidra, Hammerhead AI and Aravolta.
