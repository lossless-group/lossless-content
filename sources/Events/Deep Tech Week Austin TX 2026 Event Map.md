## Observed themes

- **Austin as a hardware town.** The Factory Crawl ran 12 tours over three days (ICON, TerraFirma, Zellerfeld, BSU Electronics, Galadyne, Bravo Ordnance, Subcritical, American Housing Corp) plus lab tours at Neurophos and Emerald Cloud Lab.
- **Defense autonomy cluster.** Allen Control Systems, Saronic, Galadyne, Bravo Ordnance and Fleetzero, with Overmatch VC hosting.
- **Nuclear and onsite energy.** Aalo Atomics closed the week at its factory; Subcritical, Base Power and Soma Energy also present. Aalo also appeared at Yotta 2026.
- **A frontier-science track.** M31 Capital's 'Physics of Consciousness' and Hyperstition's 'Kardashev Scaling' brought in Hal Puthoff, Stuart Hameroff, Garret Moddel and the Institute of Noetic Sciences.
