# Greenbuild International Conference & Expo

![Greenbuild International Conference & Expo hero image showing attendees, exhibition booths, and sustainable-building displays](https://media.licdn.com/dms/image/v2/D4E10AQGqrcIhL43EKg/image-shrink_800/B4EaFm.fneIIAg-/0/1792729021287?e=2147483647&v=beta&t=Nop2NpUuzlfCFAQBM1KkH2z5Vk-CfwaEBI9L01xFv7s)

- _Greenbuild is the built-environment sector’s major convening point for turning sustainability ideas, technologies, and partnerships into practical projects._
- Greenbuild is an **event**: an international conference and trade expo presented by the U.S. Green Building Council (USGBC) and owned and operated in North America by Informa Exhibitions U.S. [^7ohfz8] It began in 2002 with an inaugural event in Austin, Texas, attended by approximately 4,000 people. [^fjonf5] An innovation consultant returns to it for concentrated exposure to emerging building technologies, policy shifts, market signals, and the people shaping sustainable construction. [^9b4c3y]

## Type and Format

- **Type:** This source is an event.
- **Format details:** Greenbuild is an annual, primarily in-person conference and expo for the global green-building community, with programming spanning keynotes, panels, workshops, tours, continuing education, showcases, and certification workshops. [^9hdxso] [^ak3gil] [^yh7bq6] Its audience includes architects, engineers, developers, building owners, manufacturers, policymakers, investors, and sustainability professionals. [^9b4c3y]
- **Where it lives:** [Official Greenbuild site](https://informaconnect.com/greenbuild/) · [USGBC Greenbuild hub](https://www.usgbc.org/greenbuild)

## The People Behind It

- **Host and presenting organization:** USGBC presents Greenbuild and connects the event to its broader mission of advancing sustainable, resilient, equitable, healthy, and high-performing buildings and communities. [^7ohfz8] [^9hdxso]
- **Event operator:** In North America, Informa Exhibitions U.S. owns and operates Greenbuild. [^7ohfz8]
- **Foundational leadership:** USGBC was formed in 1993 by David Gottfried, Mike Italiano, and Rick Fedrizzi, who served as president, CEO, and founding chair, respectively. [^fjonf5]
- **Program direction:** The event’s editorial direction is expressed through USGBC-led keynotes, panels, deep dives, showcases, and certification programming. [^ak3gil]

## Catalog of Notable Works

- [Greenbuild 2002 — Austin inaugural edition](https://www.usgbc.org/about/mission-vision) — 2002 — USGBC / early green-building community — the first Greenbuild established the event as a gathering for a rapidly expanding industry and drew about 4,000 attendees. [^fjonf5]
- [Greenbuild keynote program](https://www.usgbc.org/press/about-greenbuild) — recurring — speakers including Barack Obama, Amal Clooney, Bill Clinton, Neil deGrasse Tyson, James Cameron, Al Gore, and Jane Fonda — a high-profile platform connecting green building with climate, science, public policy, and culture. [^7ohfz8]
- [Greenbuild 2026](https://www.usgbc.org/event/greenbuild-2026) — 2026 — “Invest for impact” — focuses on investment, new development, retrofits, sustainability, resilience, equity, health, and wellness. [^9hdxso] [^0f7v50]
- [USGBC at Greenbuild 2026](https://www.usgbc.org/greenbuild) — 2026 — keynotes, panels, deep dives, showcases, and certification workshops — a concentrated view of USGBC’s current priorities and professional-learning agenda. [^ak3gil]
- [Greenbuild 2026 education program](https://www.fedcenter.gov/Events/index.cfm?id=44324&date=10/20/2026) — 2026 — approximately 100 sessions — covers building performance, design, construction, sustainability, and related professional practice across basic-to-expert levels. [^yh7bq6]

## Why It Matters to Innovators

- **A market-sensing instrument:** Greenbuild brings together the demand side—owners, developers, investors, and policymakers—with the supply side—manufacturers, designers, engineers, and technology providers—making it useful for spotting commercialization signals before they appear in mainstream markets. [^9b4c3y]
- **A translation layer from technology to adoption:** Its combination of educational sessions, expo-floor products, workshops, tours, and certification programming shows how technical ideas move toward standards, procurement, implementation, and professional practice. [^0f7v50] [^ak3gil] [^yh7bq6]
- **A systems-innovation lens:** The event treats sustainability as an interconnected challenge involving buildings, cities, communities, resilience, health, wellness, equity, and quality of life rather than as an isolated energy-efficiency problem. [^9hdxso] [^0f7v50]
- **A partnership engine:** USGBC describes Greenbuild as a launchpad for emerging technologies, market transformation, and partnerships that turn innovation into action. [^9b4c3y] This makes it relevant to [[concepts/Systems Innovation]], [[concepts/Technology Adoption]], and [[concepts/Market Shaping]].
- **A credibility filter:** Recurring participation by major practitioners, public figures, policymakers, and industry organizations gives the event agenda-setting value, while the presence of certification and continuing-education content helps distinguish deployable practice from purely speculative discussion. [^7ohfz8] [^ak3gil] [^yh7bq6]
