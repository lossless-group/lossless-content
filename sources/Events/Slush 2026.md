# Slush 2026 Event Map

**Event:** [[Sources/Events/Slush|Slush]] 2026, **Nov 18–19, 2026**, Messukeskus (Helsinki Expo and Convention Centre), Helsinki. Around it: **Investor Day** on **Nov 17** at Finlandia Hall, **Slush x RAISE** (20 emerging managers pitching to LPs) on **Nov 18** with a Selection Committee dinner on **Nov 17**, and 600+ side events through Slush Week. Slush says it gathers "$4T AUM" and runs "20,000+ meetings"; third-party guides put attendance at 13,000+ with 500+ LPs (PipelineRoad, unverified). Over 80% of attendees come from the Nordics, UK, DACH, France & Benelux, CEE and Southern Europe.

**Mode: awareness only.** Michael is **not registered and not attending**. This map exists to get value from knowing the event is happening: put the firms and people into the Lossless Twenty as *observations* (never interactions), write vault notes, and grade the experiment about 90 days later (~2027-02-17). Nobody here has been met.

**What is 2026 vs. 2025.** The **2026** lists are confirmed on slush.org: 179 stage speakers, the Investor Day program, the Slush 100 (2026 cohort) and the Slush x RAISE Top 20 and Selection Committee (from Slush's own posts). The VC logo wall ("$4T AUM, see attending VCs") and the side-event catalogue are **Slush 2025**; for those firms, **2026 attendance is unverified**. Built from public pages fetched 2026-10-14, web research, and the Lossless Twenty CRM.

> [!warning] **ASSUMPTION: same two-hat lens as RAISE 2026; confirm before using the rankings**
> Inferred from the Lossless Twenty, exactly as for [[Sources/Events/RAISE Global Summit 2026|RAISE Global Summit 2026]]:
> 1. **Hypernova Capital (Venture Partner).** A fund-of-funds whose "Fund-with-Funds" strategy puts about 40% into solo GPs and emerging managers on Funds I–III. At Slush the sourcing pool is **European**: the Slush x RAISE Top 20 and the new solo-GP and spinout funds on stage.
> 2. **HumainVC.** Every active CRM thesis names HumainVC as its fund vehicle. At Slush the LP prospects are the **Slush x RAISE Selection Committee** (17 institutional LPs, several already in the CRM) and the LPs on the Investor Day stage.
>
> A third, smaller angle is **co-invest and deal flow** in the CRM theses: Physical AI and robotics, defense and dual-use, nuclear, fusion and energy, space, agent infrastructure, precision health. Slush 2026 is unusually strong here: 52 of the Slush 100 build hardware (per sesamers.com).

**The RAISE overlap is the headline.** Slush x RAISE is co-hosted with [[Sources/Events/RAISE|RAISE Global]]. Seven of the 20 Slush x RAISE funds are already in the CRM through RAISE (six 2026 RAISE Select, plus Tapestry VC as a RAISE sponsor), and five Selection Committee LPs came into the CRM through RAISE: University of Chicago, LGT and Top Tier (RAISE GP Day speakers) and Integra and Northwestern (RAISE LP logo wall). Two events, one cohort: anything learned at RAISE (Oct 22) is an opener for Slush (Nov 18).

**Lens:** **Hypernova** = emerging managers worth an allocation or a deeper look. **Humain** = LPs that back emerging managers, as HumainVC prospects. **Deal** = companies or GPs overlapping the CRM theses.

**Priority key:** *A* = seek out, prepared ask. *B* = worth a deliberate message. *C* = only if it comes up naturally. *CRM* = already in the Lossless Twenty. Blank = listed, not scored.

**Touchpoint key (awareness mode):** *Observed: stage* = on the 2026 speaker or Investor Day program. *Observed: list* = on a published 2026 list (Slush 100, Slush x RAISE). *Observed: logo wall 2025* = attended in 2025; 2026 unverified. Nothing in this map is *met*.
