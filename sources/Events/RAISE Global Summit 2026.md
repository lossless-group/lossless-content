# RAISE Global Summit 2026 Event Map

**Event:** [[Sources/Events/RAISE|RAISE]] Global Summit 2026, the 11th annual (Oct 22, 2026), at the Golden Gate Club, Presidio, San Francisco. **GP Day** is Oct 21 at the same venue, and **RAISE Week** (Oct 19-23) has 34 side events. The Summit is invitation-only: 101 "RAISE Select" emerging managers and 330+ LPs. LP tickets and GP Day are both **sold out**. This is **pre-event prep** for Michael Staton / Lossless. It was built only from public pages (the Cvent event site, raiseglobal.co, press) and the Lossless Twenty CRM. No attendee list was available, and nobody here has been met at this event yet.

> A third, smaller angle is **co-invest and deal flow** from GPs whose theses overlap the CRM's: Physical AI, defense, nuclear and energy, agent infrastructure, precision health.

### Protocol

- **LP-driven format.** The 1:1s are opt-in meetings that LPs request through the RAISE portal before the day. On the Hypernova hat, Michael should be an LP *requesting* meetings, which needs a ticket. On the HumainVC hat, he isn't a Select GP, so don't pitch HumainVC from the main floor. Use the side events and follow up afterwards.
- **One investing partner per GP firm** (2025 rule). Don't expect whole teams.
- **Main-stage pitches are 3 minutes, 5-6 slides.** Note which of the 101 present: the LP committee picked them to present.
- **Keep the hats separate in each conversation.** Lead with Hypernova (an LP looking to allocate) with GPs. Lead with HumainVC only with fund-of-funds and family-office LPs, and only when they ask.
- **Kauffman Fellows** is the warmest network here (South Loop and 14B co-host Tue). The CRM's Kauffman Fellows SIG record was entered from Michael's own description, which suggests he is in that community; unverified.
