# OceanX 2026 Event Map

**Event:** [[Sources/Events/OceanX|OceanX]] Summit 2026 (Oct 7-9, 2026), aboard the OceanXplorer, Singapore. Invitation-only, ~350 attendees, "60+ speakers" (42 published). **Pre-event prep** for [[Verena Sturm]] / The Water Foundation (TWF): built from the public speaker and partner list, web research, and the TWF Twenty CRM. Nobody here has been met yet.

**Lens:** TWF wants three kinds of contact. **LP** = capital for the Water Venture Builder, VC Fund and PE Fund (financial institutions, HNWIs, single family offices, catalytic philanthropy). **Deal** = scale-up deep tech in the Panthalassa mould. **Gov** = political leaders and multilaterals with a desertification, land-restoration or water-resilience mandate (a TWF Institute client).

**Priority key:** *A* = seek out, prepared ask. *B* = worth a deliberate conversation. *C* = only if it happens naturally. *CRM* = already in the TWF Twenty CRM.

## Founder prep

### The one-line positioning

> 90% of climate adaptation needs are water-based, yet less than 5% of climate finance goes to water. Everyone on this ship is solving the ocean end of the water cycle. **TWF finances the freshwater end**, with a venture builder, VC and PE funds, and the TWF Institute for sovereign strategy.

The room is ocean-first. Lead with **freshwater as the ocean's upstream** (runoff, coastal aquifers, desalination, blue carbon) rather than a pivot away from the ocean.

### Top 8 conversations, in order

| # | Who | Ask | Opening line |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Jennifer Lewis, Temasek Trust | Is concessional climate capital open to a first-loss tranche in a water fund? | "Your C3H capital got Equatic built with PUB. We want to do that for freshwater across emerging Asia and the Gulf." |

### Protocol

- **Singapore ministers:** a brief greeting, one line, a card. Follow up in writing through the minister's office asking for a referral to MSE / PUB. Singapore officials don't take paid advisory pitches; frame it as knowledge-sharing.
- **Budi Djiwandono:** "Pak Budi". Deference; he's fluent in English (US-educated). Expect to be routed to his staff (*tenaga ahli*) or the faction office. Don't imply access to the President. Sovereign advisory in Indonesia usually runs through Bappenas, the Finance Ministry, or the coordinating ministry for infrastructure.
- **Oman:** ESO is independent of government; go through Ms Al Harthi, not around her.
- **COP17 (desertification):** nobody aboard holds a UNCCD mandate. Use IUCN and TNC to reach their COP17 delegations.
