## Value Proposition & Features

Isomorphic Labs is a London-based biotechnology research company developing AI systems for drug design and discovery, with the goal of accelerating medicine development and improving human health. [^E1] It describes its model as developing AI models, generating biological data, discovering new biology, and advancing potential medicines toward clinical testing. [^c1h9v5]

Priority features:

- **Unified AI drug-design engine:** IsoDDE is intended to integrate molecular-structure prediction, biological-activity modeling, and candidate design in one system. [^8qn6yd]
- **Protein–ligand prediction:** Isomorphic reports improved prediction of protein and small-molecule binding structures compared with AlphaFold 3. [^89hsw4]
- **Antibody–antigen modeling:** The company reports higher antibody–antigen structure-prediction accuracy than AlphaFold 3. [^89hsw4]
- **Binding-affinity estimation:** IsoDDE estimates how strongly drug candidates bind biological targets without requiring experimental crystal structures. [^89hsw4]
- **AI-generated biological data:** The company combines models with internally generated data and new biological measurements. [^c1h9v5][^4e5jcd]
- **Internal drug development:** Isomorphic plans to use its models both with pharmaceutical partners and for medicines developed in-house. [^7peqmx]
- **Therapeutic-program development:** Its stated objective is to move AI-designed candidates toward clinical trials. [^tnxmj7]
- **Research partnerships:** The company has announced collaborations with Eli Lilly and Novartis. [^7peqmx]

## Product Roadmap / Announcements

As of October 8, 2026,

- **October 7, 2026:** Isomorphic Labs joined the [[Virtual Biology Initiative]], a Biohub-convened effort involving funding, data, computation, and biological-measurement technologies to generate AI-ready biological data. [^4e5jcd]
- **September 29, 2026:** The company published an account of its integrated approach to medicine development, spanning AI-model development, data generation, biological discovery, and clinical translation. [^c1h9v5]
- **February 2026:** Isomorphic published technical claims about IsoDDE’s performance in protein–ligand and antibody–antigen structure prediction and binding-affinity estimation. [^89hsw4][^8qc5zn]
- **End of 2026:** The company is reported to be targeting initiation of its first clinical trials by the end of 2026. [^tnxmj7]

## Recent Developments

Within the past 90 days, Isomorphic Labs joined the Virtual Biology Initiative and described a strategy to move from AI models and biological data through drug discovery and clinical development. # History and Origin Story

Isomorphic Labs was founded in 2021 as a spinout from Alphabet’s Google DeepMind, with the mission of applying DeepMind’s [[Vocabulary/AlphaFold|AlphaFold]]-related advances to drug discovery. [^7peqmx] Its key inflection points include a reported $600 million financing in 2025, the development and publication of IsoDDE results in February 2026, and a $2.1 billion Series B in May 2026. [^8qc5zn][^7nzlcn]

# Competitive Landscape

## Who it's for, who it's not for

Isomorphic Labs is aimed at pharmaceutical companies, biotechnology researchers, and internal drug-development programs that need computational support for target biology, molecular design, candidate selection, and therapeutic development. Its announced collaborations with Eli Lilly and Novartis indicate a focus on large-scale pharmaceutical research partnerships. [^7peqmx]

It is not positioned in the retrieved sources as a general-purpose AI tool, a consumer health application, or a publicly accessible drug-design SaaS product; its models are described as internal and its work as partnership-based or in-house. [^apnoa9]

## Viable Alternatives
