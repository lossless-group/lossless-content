# Eli Lilly

- _Eli Lilly and Company is a global pharmaceutical company whose scale, metabolic-medicine franchise, and drug-development pipeline make it a key organization to track in biopharma._
- Eli Lilly and Company is a **for-profit pharmaceutical company** focused on discovering, developing, manufacturing, and commercializing medicines. The company was founded in 1876 and is headquartered in Indianapolis, Indiana; its operations and markets are global. Its recent growth has been driven prominently by Mounjaro and Zepbound, which together helped produce third-quarter 2026 revenue of $24.4 billion. [^wozll6]

## What They Do

Lilly conducts pharmaceutical research, runs clinical-development programs, manufactures medicines, and sells approved therapies through healthcare systems and patients worldwide. Its portfolio spans metabolic disease, diabetes, obesity, oncology, immunology, neuroscience, and other therapeutic areas. [^wozll6] [^denp3x]

- **Mounjaro:** tirzepatide for type 2 diabetes; a major revenue driver. [^wozll6]
- **Zepbound:** tirzepatide for chronic weight management; a major revenue driver. [^wozll6]
- **Verzenio:** abemaciclib for breast cancer. [^denp3x]
- **Retevmo:** selpercatinib for certain cancers. [^denp3x]
- **Taltz:** ixekizumab for inflammatory and immune-mediated conditions. [^7p90km]
- **Jaypirca:** pirtobrutinib for certain blood cancers, including expanded use in previously untreated CLL/SLL. [^wozll6]
- **Clinical pipeline:** investigational programs include retatrutide, orforglipron, olomorasib, and sofetabart mipitecan. [^wozll6] [^denp3x] [^t5m7ja] [^60mbnv] [^ikthd6]

## History and Origin Story

Eli Lilly founded the company in Indianapolis in 1876 with the aim of producing higher-quality medicines through pharmaceutical science and manufacturing discipline. The organization has since evolved into a global research-based pharmaceutical company whose recent strategic center of gravity is metabolic medicine alongside oncology, immunology, and other specialty areas. [^denp3x]

- **1876** — Colonel Eli Lilly founded the company in Indianapolis. [^denp3x]

- **2026** — Lilly reported that Mounjaro and Zepbound were central drivers of its rapid revenue growth. [^wozll6]

- **2026** — Lilly expanded its oncology pipeline through clinical development of investigational ADC and KRAS G12C programs. [^denp3x] [^60mbnv] [^ikthd6]
