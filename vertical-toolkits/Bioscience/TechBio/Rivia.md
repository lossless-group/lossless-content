## Value Proposition & Features

Rivia is a Zurich-based clinical-trial intelligence company that consolidates data from disparate systems into a unified intelligence layer, helping biotech teams analyze patient safety, efficacy, and operational data in real time. [^E1] Its stated goal is more predictable operations and faster, more efficient trial execution. [^E1]

- **Unified trial-data engine:** Aggregates data across vendors and systems into one intelligence layer. [^E1]
- **Automated data-quality monitoring:** Checks and monitors clinical-trial data quality automatically. [^7m313m]
- **Real-time analysis:** Supports analysis of safety, efficacy, and operational data during trial execution. [^7m313m]
- **Scientific logic layer:** Applies scientific logic to unified data. [^i6guf2]
- **AI-agent workflows:** Embeds AI agents into review workflows. [^i6guf2]
- **Operational intelligence:** Aims to make clinical-trial operations more predictable. [^E1]
- **Faster trial execution:** Positions the platform as a way to improve trial efficiency. [^E1]

# History and Origin Story

Rivia was founded in 2022 and is headquartered in Zurich, Switzerland. [^E1] Publicly available material describes its origin around the problem of clinical-trial data being fragmented across vendors, with the company building a unified intelligence layer to support real-time trial analysis and more predictable execution. # Competitive Landscape

## Who it's for, who it's not for

Rivia is aimed at biotech and pharmaceutical teams that manage multi-vendor clinical-trial data and need real-time visibility into safety, efficacy, data quality, and trial operations. [^7m313m] [^E1]

It is not evidently aimed at patients, general-purpose analytics users, or organizations seeking a consumer health application; available descriptions focus specifically on clinical-trial intelligence and operational workflows. [^7m313m] [^i6guf2]
