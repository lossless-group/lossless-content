## Value Proposition & Features

Aquafortus Technologies develops non-thermal desalination and water-treatment technology. Its ABX™ system uses solvent extraction and membrane filtration to convert highly concentrated brine into clean water and dry salt, supporting continuous, regenerable zero-liquid-discharge processes. [^E1]

- **ABX™ liquid-to-liquid crystallizer:** Treats highly concentrated brines and produces dry salt plus clean water. [^E1]
- **Non-thermal operation:** Desalination without relying primarily on evaporation or thermal energy. [^E1]
- **Zero-liquid discharge:** Designed to eliminate liquid brine discharge. [^E1]
- **Continuous processing:** Supports continuous treatment rather than batch-only operation. [^E1]
- **Regenerable process:** The process is described as regenerable for repeated operation. [^E1]
- **Solvent extraction:** Absorbs water from brine through solvent extraction. [^E1]
- **Membrane filtration:** Produces clean water through membrane filtration. [^E1]
- **Dry-salt recovery:** Concentrates residual salts into a dry solid output. [^E1]

# History and Origin Story

Aquafortus Technologies was founded in 2016 and is headquartered in Māngere, Auckland, New Zealand. [^E1] The company’s disclosed technology focus is non-thermal desalination and water treatment, centered on its ABX™ crystallization system. [^E1]

# Competitive Landscape

## Who it's for, who it's not for

Aquafortus is aimed at industrial operators that must manage highly concentrated brines, recover water, or pursue zero-liquid discharge while minimizing dependence on thermal treatment. [^E1]

It is not clearly positioned as a consumer water-purification product or a conventional municipal point-of-use system; no evidence in the retrieved material supports those markets.

## Viable Alternatives

- **Conventional thermal evaporation and crystallization:** Established approaches for concentrating brine and achieving zero liquid discharge, but generally more energy-intensive than non-thermal methods.
- **Reverse osmosis:** Widely used membrane desalination technology, though concentrate handling remains necessary.
- **Electrodialysis and electrodialysis reversal:** Electrically driven separation approaches for selected brine and industrial-water applications.
- **Mechanical vapor compression:** A thermal brine-concentration alternative for industrial zero-liquid-discharge systems.
- **Constructed or hybrid crystallization systems:** Alternatives where salt recovery and solid-residue management are required.
