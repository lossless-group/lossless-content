## Value Proposition & Features

Almetra is an AI-based process-analytics platform for [[concepts/Market-Categories/Manufacturing Automation|Advanced Manufacturing]] that aims to give teams continuous visibility into manual shop-floor operations. It uses cameras and flexible AI analysis to identify productivity, quality, and process-improvement opportunities. [^E1]

Core features include:

- Camera-based capture of production processes. [^E1]
- AI analysis of manual shop-floor workflows. [^E1]
- Identification of process-improvement opportunities. [^E1]
- [[content-areas/AI-Factories-Datacenters/Concepts/Root-Cause Analysis|Root-Cause Analysis]] for operational issues. [^E1]
- Process-performance insights. [^E1]
- Data-driven productivity improvements. [^E1]
- Quality-improvement support. [^E1]
