[Benchmark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benchmark_%28venture_capital_firm%29) is a prominent American venture capital firm founded in 1995 and based in San Francisco and Woodside, California. [^x3bwnx] [^kffb53] 
## Overview & Model

* Equal Partnership: Operates with a flat structure of equal general partners who share economics and management responsibilities, with no junior staff or hierarchy. [^kffb53] [^ymcon2] 
* Focus Areas: Invests early in artificial intelligence (AI), open-source software, digital marketplaces, infrastructure, and enterprise tech. [^x3bwnx] 
* Recent Milestone (2026): Raised $2 billion across two new funds—comprising $750 million for its 12th flagship early-stage fund and a $1.25 billion first-ever growth fund, marking a historic shift from its traditional ~$425 million fund ceiling. [^ncr3a3] [^mjlbv6] 

## Notable Portfolio

* Historical & Public Exits: [[organizations/Uber|Uber]], Snap, Twitter, [[eBay]], Instagram, and [[Tooling/Enterprise Jobs-to-be-Done/Dropbox|Dropbox]]. [^9u1jhu] [^53ntam] [^f8mweu] 
* Current AI & Tech Focus: [[Tooling/AI-Toolkit/Model Producers/Cerbras|Cerbras]], [[Tooling/AI-Toolkit/Generative AI/Code Generators/Cursor|Cursor]], [[Tooling/AI-Toolkit/AI Infrastructure/Fireworks AI|Fireworks AI]], [[Tooling/AI-Toolkit/AI Programming Frameworks/LangChain|LangChain]], [[Tooling/AI-Toolkit/Generative AI/HeyGen|HeyGen]], and [[Tooling/AI-Toolkit/Model Producers/Manus AI|Manus AI]].
