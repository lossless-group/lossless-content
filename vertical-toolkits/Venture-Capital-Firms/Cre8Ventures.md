Siemens Cre8Ventures (by Siemens EDA) is a collaborative venture initiative that supports semiconductor startups, investors, and universities by accelerating the path from research to market under the [EU Chips Act](https://news.siemens.com/fr-fr/cre8ventures-eu-chips-act/). [^492ktg] [^w79lpr] [^3xzzsg] 
## Key Offerings & Resources

* Cloud-Based Labs & EDA Tools: Provides access to [[organizations/Siemens|Siemens]] Electronic Design Automation (EDA) tool flows and multi-physics [[concepts/Market-Categories/Digital Twins|Digital Twins]] technology. [^492ktg] [^1stf66] 
* Cost Reduction: Helps simulate and validate power and performance digitally, avoiding $20M–$30M in physical first-silicon manufacturing costs. [^492ktg] 
* Proof Stages: Assists with proof-of-technology, proof-of-concept, and proof-of-value designs. [^492ktg] 
* Funding & Support: Connects innovators with non-dilutive [[Chips Act]] funding, Siemens Financial Services, venture capital, and corporate/customer introductions. [^492ktg] [^2i6ik4]

* Digital Twin Marketplace: Features ecosystem members and partners like [[content-areas/AI-Factories-Datacenters/Organizations/SpinEdge.ai]], Minima, Solaya, [[vertical-toolkits/Venture-Capital-Firms/Cloudberry VC]], SpiNNcloud, ComposabilityPro, Athena P3M, BeyondFence, MemStera, and [[Durance AI]]. * Open Higher Education Program: Launched in collaboration with [[Sources/Standards-and-Specs/ARM|ARM]] and the University of Southampton's School of Electronics and Computer Science (ECS) to support student-led semiconductor innovation across Europe. [^1stf66]
