Fund-of-funds platform that writes anchor LP checks into new venture managers and pairs them with mentorship from a bench of GP Advisors (operators of their own firms). It launched in 2021 as a $50M vehicle for seed and early-stage funds from underrepresented managers ([fin-news](https://www.fin-news.com/2021/06/18/venture-capitalists-launch-50-million-initiative-for-emerging-managers/)). Per [Hunter Walk](https://hunterwalk.medium.com/after-investing-100-million-into-new-vc-firms-heres-what-i-look-for-traditional-but-better-or-4b342cc69bf0), it has since put about $100M into new firms, after looking at 1,500+ raising funds and backing roughly 1.5% of them, often as a first or second-largest LP.

**People:** [[Sources/People/Jamie Rhode|Jamie Rhode]] (Partner)

**Observed at:** GP Day speaker firm and listed fund-of-funds LP at [[Sources/Events/RAISE Global Summit 2026|RAISE Global Summit 2026]]. Observed, not met.

**Why it matters to us:** a Hypernova peer (same emerging-manager allocator strategy) and a HumainVC Fund I LP prospect.
