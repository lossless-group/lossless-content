### Kite — 
https://gokite.ai/ [^E1]

Kite builds infrastructure for autonomous agents to authenticate, transact, and operate independently, including agent-to-agent interactions and the agentic internet. [^E1]

## Value Proposition & Features

Kite’s value proposition is **agentic payment infrastructure**: enabling software agents to act on a user’s behalf with identity, authorization, governance, and stablecoin settlement. [^E1] Its public materials describe agent mandates that constrain spending, counterparties, and transaction terms, with cryptographic enforcement. [^0g2omk]

Priority features:

- **Agent Passport:** identity and wallet infrastructure that lets agents transact within user-defined limits. [^kmu1rt]
- **Delegated mandates:** owners can define what an agent may purchase, from whom, and at what price range; the mandate is cryptographically enforced. [^0g2omk]
- **[[Stablecoin]] payments:** native USDC-based settlement is supported through Kite’s payment infrastructure and Arc integration. [^al51vb] [^gxse1t]
- **Escrow and conditional release:** funds can remain in escrow until delivery and acceptance conditions are satisfied. [^ms2ors] [^gxse1t]
- **Seller Console:** sellers can define prices, delivery deadlines, and verification conditions. [^gxse1t]
- **Agent-to-agent commerce:** agents can discover services, negotiate terms, execute transactions, and verify delivery. [^gxse1t]
- **Kite Chain:** described in secondary coverage as an EVM-compatible settlement layer with sub-second finality and low transaction fees. [^kmu1rt]
- **Developer interfaces:** [[Vocabulary/SDK|SDKs]] and [[concepts/Explainers for AI/Agentic Workflows|Agentic Workflows]] are intended to support integrations and application development. [^kmu1rt]

# Competitive Landscape

## Who it's for, who it's not for

Kite is intended for developers, marketplaces, sellers, and financial or commerce platforms that need autonomous agents to discover services, negotiate, pay, and verify delivery under explicit authorization limits. [^kmu1rt] [^gxse1t]

It is not clearly positioned for ordinary human checkout, generic chatbot deployment, or applications that do not require machine-controlled identity, payment authorization, escrow, or agent-to-agent settlement. This anti-ICP assessment is an inference from Kite’s described product scope. [^E1] [^kmu1rt] [^gxse1t]

## Viable Alternatives

- **Circle Arc:** A settlement and payment-network alternative for USDC-based agent transactions; Kite’s announced integration uses Arc as the settlement layer. [^al51vb] [^gxse1t]
- **Coinbase x402:** A machine-payment approach in which servers return payment instructions and agents attach proof of payment when retrying requests. [^tn8xcd]
- **PayPal agentic-payment infrastructure:** A potentially overlapping ecosystem option, particularly because PayPal is identified as a Kite backer and PYUSD is cited as an ecosystem connection. [^by4hho] [^kmu1rt]
- **Traditional payment APIs and wallets:** Suitable for applications needing conventional human- or server-initiated payments rather than Kite’s agent identity, mandates, and escrow model. This is an architectural comparison based on Kite’s stated functionality. [^E1] [^0g2omk] [^gxse1t]
