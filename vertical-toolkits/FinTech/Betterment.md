## Value Proposition & Features

Betterment positions itself as a platform that makes saving, investing, and financial planning easier through automation and goal-based tools. Its offering spans managed investing, self-directed trading, cash management, retirement planning, and crypto exposure. [^E1] [^m7eszd]

- **Automated portfolio management:** Betterment builds portfolios using low-cost ETFs, rebalances them, reinvests dividends, and applies tax-management features. [^m7eszd] [^E1]
- **Goal-based investing:** Users can set goals such as retirement, a vacation, or a down payment, with risk levels adjusted according to the goal and investment timeline. [^m7eszd]
- **Self-directed investing:** Customers can choose individual stocks and ETFs while receiving tax-related insights. [^m7eszd] [^E1]
- **Cash management:** Cash Reserve is a high-yield cash account, while Checking supports everyday spending; Checking is offered through nbkc bank. [^m7eszd]
- **Retirement planning:** Betterment supports retirement-oriented planning and tax-advantaged investment accounts, including Traditional, Roth, and SEP IRAs. [^796nkh] [^ejf7d2]
- **Crypto investing:** Betterment offers automated crypto exposure through portfolios focused on Bitcoin and Ethereum market movements. [^72c2nl]
- **Automatic deposits and savings features:** The platform supports recurring deposits, cash goals, and rewards-oriented savings features. [^E1]
- **Advisor infrastructure:** Betterment Advisor Solutions provides portfolio construction, billing, onboarding, and managed-account tools for financial-advisor firms. [^lo1lej] [^1uiekd]

# Competitive Landscape

## Who it's for, who it's not for

Betterment is designed for consumers seeking automated, diversified investing; goal-based planning; retirement tools; cash management; and optional self-directed stock, ETF, or crypto exposure. [^m7eszd] [^E1] It also serves financial-advisor firms through Advisor Solutions, including portfolio construction, rebalancing, tax coordination, onboarding, and billing tools. [^lo1lej] [^1uiekd]

It is less suited to investors who want fully bespoke securities selection, direct control over every portfolio transaction, or a traditional in-person advisory relationship as their primary service. This is an inference from Betterment’s automation-first product design and should not be read as a stated company exclusion. [^m7eszd] [^E1]

## Viable Alternatives

- **[[Wealthfront]]:** A comparable automated-investing and financial-planning platform for users seeking digitally managed portfolios and cash-management tools.
- **Vanguard Digital Advisor:** An alternative emphasizing Vanguard’s investment ecosystem and automated portfolio management.
- **Schwab Intelligent Portfolios:** A robo-advisory alternative integrated with Charles Schwab’s brokerage platform.
- **Fidelity Go:** An automated-investing option within Fidelity’s broader brokerage and retirement ecosystem.
- **[[vertical-toolkits/FinTech/Range.com|Range.com]]:** A financial-planning alternative for users seeking advisor-oriented planning workflows; it pairs conceptually with [[vertical-toolkits/FinTech/Range.com|Range.com]] when comparing digital wealth-management tooling.
