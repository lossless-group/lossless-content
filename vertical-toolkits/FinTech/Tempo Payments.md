## Value Proposition & Features

Tempo is a payments-first [[Vocabulary/Layer 1|Layer 1]] [[Vocabulary/Blockchain|Blockchain]] designed for [[Vocabulary/Stablecoin|Stablecoin]] settlement, with dedicated payment lanes, sub-second finality, stablecoin-denominated fees, and sub-cent transaction costs. [^1nel61] It targets enterprises, fintechs, developers, and machine-to-machine commerce rather than general-purpose blockchain activity. [^1nel61] [^3ge1vr]

- **Dedicated payment lanes:** Payment traffic is isolated from unrelated network activity to keep throughput and costs predictable. [^1nel61] [^3ge1vr]
- **Sub-second settlement:** Payments settle onchain in under one second without reorganizations. [^1nel61]
- **Stablecoin fees:** Users can pay network fees in USD-denominated stablecoins instead of managing a volatile gas token. [^1nel61]
- **Payment memos:** Native transfers support 32-byte references for reconciliation with invoices and ISO 20022 records. [^1nel61] [^b57q3k]
- **Tempo Zones:** Private execution environments keep balances and activity hidden from the public while preserving operator visibility for audits and compliance. [^x955lg]
- **Agentic payments:** Tempo supplies the settlement layer for automated purchases by AI agents. [^3ge1vr]
- **Machine Payments Protocol:** Tempo and Stripe co-authored MPP, which uses HTTP 402 to coordinate machine-readable payment requests and settlements. [^3ge1vr]
- **Institutional settlement:** The network is positioned for cross-border settlement, treasury transfers, payroll, and tokenized-asset cash legs. [^b57q3k] [^hcmct1] [^1o6jsv] [^h0wi2n]

## Product Roadmap / Announcements

As of November 6, 2026, publicly visible announcements emphasize payment infrastructure, agentic commerce, institutional settlement, and privacy rather than a dated conventional roadmap. [^7x4t21] [^b57q3k] [^3ge1vr]

- **October 2026 — Tempo Zones:** Tempo described private execution environments connected to Mainnet for confidential enterprise payments, with limited access noted in its central-banking material. [^b57q3k] [^x955lg]
- **November 2026 — [[Sources/Standards-and-Specs/Machine Payments Protocol|Machine Payments Protocol]]:** Tempo documented MPP, co-authored with [[organizations/Stripe|Stripe]], for automated HTTP-based payments between agents and services. [^3ge1vr]
- **November 2026 — Enterprise payroll:** Tempo presented Zones as private chains for enterprise payroll while retaining Mainnet connectivity for deposits and withdrawals. [^7x4t21]

## Recent Developments

- Tempo is positioning its network for central-bank and institutional settlement, including a two-tier architecture separating central-bank money from institutional payment activity. [^b57q3k]
- Tempo’s materials identify validator participation from Stripe, Visa, and Zodia Custody by Standard Chartered. [^b57q3k]
- Tempo is expanding its stated use cases beyond stablecoin transfers to payroll, treasury management, tokenized-asset settlement, API monetization, and agentic payments. [^hcmct1] [^1o6jsv] [^h0wi2n] [^uxzlv3] [^3ge1vr]

## History and Origin Story

Tempo was founded in 2025 and is described as a payments-first Layer 1 incubated by Stripe and Paradigm. [^E1] Its core inflection point is the decision to specialize blockchain infrastructure around stablecoin payments, then extend that rail into enterprise privacy through Tempo Zones and automated commerce through MPP. [^b57q3k] [^x955lg] [^3ge1vr]

## Competitive Landscape

### Who it's for, who it's not for

Tempo is aimed at enterprises, fintechs, payroll and treasury providers, institutional settlement workflows, developers building payment applications, and services that need automated machine-to-machine payments. [^1nel61] [^hcmct1] [^1o6jsv] [^uxzlv3] [^3ge1vr] Its features also fit organizations that need stablecoin fees, fast settlement, structured reconciliation references, or controlled privacy. [^1nel61] [^b57q3k] [^x955lg]

It is not positioned as a general-purpose chain for arbitrary consumer applications, speculative gas-token activity, or workflows that do not benefit from stablecoin settlement and payment-specific execution. [^1nel61] [^3ge1vr]

### Viable Alternatives

- **[[Ethereum]]:** Broad smart-contract ecosystem and liquidity, but not specialized around dedicated payment lanes or stablecoin-denominated fees.
- **[[Solana]]:** High-throughput general-purpose chain that can support payment applications, but lacks Tempo’s stated payment-only architecture.
- **[[Stellar]]:** Payment-focused blockchain suitable for asset issuance and cross-border transfers.
- **Circle Arc:** Stablecoin-focused payments infrastructure aimed at institutional and enterprise settlement.
- **[[Visa]]/[[Mastercard]] stablecoin rails:** Established payment networks that can provide fiat-linked settlement and enterprise distribution without requiring customers to adopt a new Layer 1.
