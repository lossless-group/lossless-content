## Value Proposition & Features

Stellar is a public [[Vocabulary/Blockchain|Blockchain]] focused on global payments, asset issuance, and regulated financial applications. It has operated since 2014, with transactions settling in roughly five seconds and fees of a fraction of a cent. [^8esq0w]

- **Payments:** Connects applications to financial rails for remittances, commerce, and public-sector payments. [^8esq0w]
- **Asset issuance:** Supports trustline assets and tokenized financial instruments on the network. [^8esq0w]
- **Smart contracts:** Soroban provides smart-contract functionality, with SDKs and APIs for deployment, transaction submission, and blockchain data access. [^ti4kun]
- **Consensus:** Uses the Stellar Consensus Protocol, described as a proof-of-agreement protocol for validating transactions. [^mzcwp0]
- **Native currency:** XLM covers transaction fees, minimum balances, and smart-contract rent on Mainnet. [^9xpr1h] [^zuxn9p]
- **Network environments:** Mainnet supports production applications, Testnet offers free testing, and Futurenet exposes experimental features. [^9xpr1h]
- **Developer tooling:** Official SDKs, APIs, documentation, and utilities support application development. [^ti4kun]

- **October 29, 2026 — MetaMask integration:** Stellar became a default network in MetaMask Mobile and Extension, enabling users to manage XLM, trustline assets, Soroban tokens, and cross-network swaps. [^8esq0w]

# History and Origin Story

Stellar was founded in 2014, and the Stellar Development Foundation is the nonprofit organization supporting the network and its ecosystem. [^E1] The network’s original lumen allocation funded the foundation, while network inflation was ended by validator vote in October 2019. [^zuxn9p]

# Competitive Landscape

## Who it's for, who it's not for

Stellar is suited to financial institutions, payment companies, remittance providers, asset issuers, and developers building applications that require global payments or tokenized assets. Its production-oriented Mainnet connects applications to real financial rails. [^9xpr1h] [^8esq0w]

It is less suited to teams seeking a general-purpose chain without payment or financial-asset requirements, or developers who need only a local test environment; Testnet does not connect to real money. [^9xpr1h]

## Viable Alternatives

- [[vertical-toolkits/FinTech/Tempo Payments|Tempo Payments]] — A payment-focused blockchain alternative for teams prioritizing payment-specific infrastructure.
- Ethereum — A broad smart-contract platform for applications requiring a large general-purpose ecosystem.
- Solana — A high-throughput blockchain alternative for payment and asset applications.
- Polygon — An Ethereum-compatible infrastructure option for teams prioritizing EVM tooling.
- XRP Ledger — A payments- and asset-oriented blockchain alternative for cross-border financial applications.
