# Range

## Retrieved sources

The provided material identifies Range as a wealth-management platform founded in 2020, headquartered in McLean, Virginia, with reported total funding of $102.4 million and 234 employees. [^E1]

### Value Proposition & Features

Range combines digital wealth-management tools with human financial advice for high-earning individuals. Its stated coverage includes investments, retirement, estate planning, taxes, insurance, and related financial decisions. [^E1]

The service uses AI-assisted workflows alongside human planners; Range describes its AI wealth manager, “Rai,” as supporting research, meeting preparation, and first drafts while advisors focus on judgment and client conversations. [^8jrv9e]

# Competitive Landscape

## Who it’s for, who it’s not for

Range is designed for high-earning individuals seeking coordinated advice across investments, taxes, retirement, estate planning, insurance, and broader financial decisions. [^E1]

It is less suited to customers seeking only low-cost self-directed brokerage, a standalone budgeting tool, or advice limited to a single financial product; this is an inference from Range’s integrated advisory positioning. [^E1]

## Viable Alternatives
