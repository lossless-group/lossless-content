## Value Proposition & Features

BIOTRONIK is a German medical-technology company founded in 1963 and headquartered in Berlin. It develops active implants for **Cardiac Rhythm Management, Monitoring, and Neuromodulation**, alongside solutions for Electrophysiology. [^E1]

- Cardiac rhythm-management implants for treating and monitoring heart-rhythm disorders. [^E1]
- Remote and implant-based patient monitoring capabilities. [^E1]
- Neuromodulation therapies delivered through active implantable devices. [^E1]
- Electrophysiology products supporting diagnosis and treatment procedures. [^E1]
- Integrated therapy and monitoring technologies intended to support clinicians and patients. [^E1]
