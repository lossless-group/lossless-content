Azara Healthcare is a U.S. health-tech company founded in 2011 and headquartered in Burlington, Massachusetts. It provides population-health analytics, quality measurement, reporting, and workflow tools for community health centers, physician practices, provider networks, and value-based-care organizations. [^E1]

## Value Proposition & Features

Azara’s core value proposition is to unify clinical, claims, and supplemental data so providers can identify care opportunities, monitor performance, and act on population-health and value-based-care priorities. [^E1]

Priority capabilities include:

- **Azara DRVS:** Population-health analytics, reporting, dashboards, and centralized data warehousing for clinical and performance management. [^awnb5c]
- **Clinical quality measurement:** Measure surveillance, care-gap reporting, scorecards, and contract-specific quality tracking. [^4cnxqq] [^awnb5c]
- **Risk stratification:** RAF-gap identification and analysis of patient risk, cost, and utilization. [^awnb5c] [^skotl4]
- **Care management and coordination:** Azara Care Connect supports care management and coordination workflows. [^skotl4] [^dhin4u]
- **Patient outreach:** Azara Patient Outreach enables SMS-based care-gap outreach. [^skotl4] [^dhin4u]
- **Cost and utilization analytics:** Azara Cost & Utilization analyzes claims data and spending drivers. [^awnb5c] [^skotl4]
- **EHR integration:** The EHR Plug-In surfaces DRVS and care-management information at the point of care, including pre-visit alerts and RAF gaps. [^80qc3t]
- **Pre-visit planning:** Patient Visit Planning helps teams identify patient needs and plan follow-up before appointments. [^3fn8vd] [^skotl4]
# History and Origin Story

Azara Healthcare was founded in 2011 and is based in Burlington, Massachusetts. [^E1] Publicly available search results describe its evolution around DRVS, a flagship platform for population-health and value-based-care reporting and analytics, and indicate that Insight Partners acquired Azara in December 2024 before combining it with i2i after acquiring i2i in February 2025. # Competitive Landscape

## Who it's for, who it's not for

Azara is aimed at community health centers, FQHCs, physician groups, clinically integrated networks, critical-access hospitals, care-delivery networks, health plans, and other organizations managing quality, risk, utilization, and value-based-care performance. [^E1] [^awnb5c]

It is not positioned as a consumer health application, standalone electronic health record, or general-purpose analytics tool for organizations without population-health, quality-reporting, or care-management needs.
