## Value Proposition & Features

BodySpec provides accessible, full-body DEXA scans for tracking body fat, lean muscle, visceral fat, bone density, and related health metrics in under 15 minutes. [^2a5e4e] Its reports are designed to help users understand their current composition, monitor changes over time, and make more informed training, nutrition, and health decisions. [^2a5e4e]

- **DEXA body-composition analysis:** Low-dose X-rays measure body fat, lean tissue, visceral fat, and bone density. [^2a5e4e]
- **Regional measurement:** Reports show fat and muscle distribution across body regions. [^g4wbmf]
- **Medical-grade positioning:** BodySpec markets approximately 1% measurement accuracy compared with greater variance from home devices. [^g4wbmf]
- **Progress tracking:** Users can repeat scans to monitor changes in composition over time. [^2a5e4e]
- **Same-day reporting:** Results are available the same day at some mobile-clinic events. [^7ta7ww]
- **AI guidance:** An AI Health Coach explains results and provides nutrition and training guidance. [^g4wbmf]
- **Mobile access:** Mobile clinics visit offices, gyms, employers, health centers, and public spaces. [^E1]
- **No referral required:** Customers can book directly, with BodySpec physicians ordering the scan. [^2a5e4e]

# History and Origin Story

BodySpec was founded in 2013 and operates from Culver City, California. [^E1] The company’s model combines DEXA scanning with mobile clinics that serve gyms, employers, health centers, and public locations, while its more recent product messaging emphasizes AI-assisted interpretation and longitudinal tracking. [^E1] BodySpec has also partnered with Future for DEXA integration. [^E1]

# Competitive Landscape

## Who it's for, who it's not for

BodySpec is aimed at people pursuing measurable changes in body composition, including fitness users, athletes, weight-management customers, health-conscious consumers, employers, and organizations seeking mobile scanning services. [^2a5e4e] [^7ta7ww] [^E1]

It is less suitable for users seeking a continuous wearable-monitoring product, a substitute for diagnosis by a clinician, or a low-cost scale-based estimate rather than periodic DEXA measurement.

## Viable Alternatives

- **DEXA facilities and hospital imaging centers:** Offer comparable DEXA-based body-composition or bone-density measurement, though they may be less convenient or less fitness-oriented.
- **InBody:** Provides rapid bioelectrical-impedance body-composition testing, generally with lower equipment and scheduling requirements but different measurement characteristics.
- **Styku:** Uses 3D body scanning for visual and dimensional tracking rather than DEXA-derived tissue and bone-density analysis.
- **Bod Pod:** Uses air-displacement plethysmography to estimate body composition without X-rays.
- **At-home smart scales:** Offer inexpensive, frequent estimates but do not provide the regional DEXA measurements or visceral-fat reporting described by BodySpec. [^2a5e4e] [^g4wbmf]
