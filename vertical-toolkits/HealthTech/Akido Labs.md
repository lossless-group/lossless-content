## Value Proposition & Features

Akido describes itself as an AI-native healthcare system designed to anticipate needs, support clinical decisions, and expand physician capacity across financial and geographic barriers. [^E1]

Publicly available material identifies the following product priorities:

- AI-assisted clinical investigation and discovery. [^E1]
- Reduction of physicians’ cognitive workload. [^E1]
- Extension of physician capacity and patient reach. [^E1]
- Integrated healthcare-system infrastructure rather than a standalone consumer app. [^E1]
- Access-oriented care delivery regardless of patients’ financial means or geography. [^E1]

# History and Origin Story

Akido was founded in 2015 and is headquartered in Los Angeles, United States.
