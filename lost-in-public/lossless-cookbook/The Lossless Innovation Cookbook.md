This cookbook is a living document, and an effort to codify the most important movements, trends, concepts, and lingo within [[concepts/State of the Art]] technology, platforms and business practice. 

As a web publication, it's anonymous and de facto private. As of now, nothing is indexed by search engines, or otherwise discoverable and clickable to anyone that doesn't have a direct link.

To the best of our ability, we've tried to use, if not abuse, internal links. (they look like this [[Tooling/Productivity/Advanced Documents/Obsidian]] links) In many of these documents, we've saved "properties" including a source URL, an Amazon or GitHub link, yet we're still trying to figure out how to make them accessible to this web publication.

To the best of our ability, we've included videos that explain ideas we feel are essential.

We hope this is a productive experiment in how to communicate complex ideas. 

## Concepts
We have a Concepts section that include what we believe are our, well, core concepts that we really want to communicate, convey, and transfer. 

## Vocabulary
Outside of the core concepts, we have a running vocabulary list. In the vocabulary list, and sometimes in other places, we are hacking our productivity by using [[Large Language Models|LLMs]] to generate coherent explanations of the word, concept, trend, category, etc.  

## AI Toolkit
We have been developing a living list of AI tooling, we call the AI-Toolkit.  (Look for the folder to the right hand navigation and click on AI-Toolkit, as folders don't have internal links in Obsidian.)

## Tooling
Beyond that, we've also been developing a list of pretty much any interesting technology service, which we've sloppily put into a folder called Tooling. It is called Tooling because it's part of one of our core concepts of [[concepts/Cognitive, Collaborative Tooling]]. 

## Sources
To the extent possible, we've documented the sources of our ideas.  This includes, broadly, books and influencers.  We encourage anyone who wants to "double click" on a concept to look at the footnotes, download the e-book, watch the [[YouTube]] video.
