# Siemens

![Siemens industrial factory floor illustrating digital manufacturing](https://images.sw.cdn.siemens.com/siemens-disw-assets/public/0amZh4P30CLsfE8Z2haED/en-US/siemens-hq-dschaefer-023%20640x480.jpg?auto=format%2Ccompress&w=1280&h=720&fit=crop&crop=faces%2Cedges&q=60)

- _Siemens is a publicly traded industrial-technology company connecting software, automation, infrastructure, electrification, and mobility for large-scale digital transformation._
- Siemens is a **for-profit company** founded in 1847 and headquartered in Berlin and Munich, Germany. It develops and sells industrial, infrastructure, mobility, and digital-engineering technologies for enterprises and public-sector customers. Consultants track Siemens because its portfolio and partnerships provide a leading reference point for industrial digitalization, AI-enabled manufacturing, and infrastructure modernization.[^318oho][^ou5hup]

## Identity and Form

- **Type:** This organization is a **for-profit company**.[^318oho]
- **Legal form and jurisdiction:** Siemens AG is a publicly traded German company; its shares trade on the Frankfurt Stock Exchange under the ticker SIE.[^318oho]
- **Headquarters and presence:** Siemens is headquartered in Berlin and Munich and operates globally.[^318oho]
- **Size:** Siemens employed approximately 318,000 people worldwide on a continuing-operations basis as of September 30, 2025.[^318oho]
- **Where it lives online:** [Homepage](https://siemens.com/) · [Newsroom](https://press.siemens.com/global/en) · [Investor Relations](https://www.siemens.com/global/en/company/investor-relations.html)

## Mission and Identity

> “We create technology with purpose.”[^318oho]

Siemens presents itself as a technology company focused on combining the real and digital worlds across industry, infrastructure, mobility, and healthcare-related applications. Its stated positioning emphasizes helping customers improve productivity, sustainability, resilience, and quality of life through connected hardware, software, and services.[^318oho][^ou5hup]

- **Operating principles:** Siemens’ “ONE Tech Company” program emphasizes connecting technologies and capabilities across businesses to bring innovation to market faster and offer integrated customer solutions.[^318oho]

## What They Do

Siemens earns revenue by selling industrial and infrastructure equipment, software, automation systems, mobility products, engineering services, and lifecycle support to businesses, governments, transport operators, and other institutions. Its operating model increasingly combines physical products with digital twins, industrial AI, cloud-connected software, and recurring digital services.[^318oho][^ou5hup]

- **Industrial automation and digital manufacturing:** Automation, factory software, digital twins, industrial AI, and production engineering.
- **Smart infrastructure:** Building technology, electrification, energy management, fire safety, and infrastructure controls.[^xxh1wy]
- **Mobility:** Passenger trains, rail signaling, rail electrification, and transport digitalization.[^jyt47s][^hm0w9m][^bsagq1]
- **Industrial software:** Design, simulation, manufacturing, product lifecycle, and semiconductor-design software.
- **Healthcare technology:** Medical-imaging and diagnostic technologies through Siemens Healthineers, a separately listed affiliated company.
- **Industrial investment and venture building:** [[Cre8Ventures]] supports robotics, digital twins, semiconductors, quantum technologies, and photonics ecosystems.[^byk038]

## Leadership and People

- [Roland Busch](https://www.siemens.com/global/en/company/about/management/roland-busch.html) — President and Chief Executive Officer — leads Siemens’ global industrial-technology strategy.[^318oho]
- Sabrina Herrmann — spokesperson for Siemens’ newly unified Germany leadership committee from October 1, 2026 — also serves as Managing Director of Buildings Germany.[^318oho]
- Katherina Westrich — Global Vice President for Electronics and Semiconductors — represents Siemens’ semiconductor-industry activities in digital twins and AI.[^mrk59w]

## History and Origin Story

Siemens began in Berlin in 1847 when Werner von Siemens and Johann Georg Halske founded a telegraph-construction company; it later expanded from communications technology into electrification, industrial engineering, transportation, healthcare, and software. Its modern strategy centers on integrating industrial hardware with software and digital systems.[^318oho][^ou5hup]

- **1847** — Siemens was founded in Berlin as a telegraph business.[^318oho]
- **Late nineteenth and twentieth centuries** — The company expanded into electrical engineering, power systems, transportation, communications, and industrial technologies.[^318oho]
- **2016** — Siemens established Siemens Healthineers as a separately listed healthcare technology company.
- **2020s** — Siemens increasingly emphasized digital twins, industrial software, AI, and connected infrastructure.[^byk038][^ou5hup]
- **2026** — The company began implementing a “ONE-Tech-Company” leadership model in Germany to coordinate its businesses more closely.[^318oho]

## Milestones and Signature Output

- **Telegraph business — 1847** — Siemens’ founding communications technology established the company’s original industrial base.[^318oho]
- **Industrial electrification — nineteenth and twentieth centuries** — Siemens expanded from telegraphy into electrical systems and industrial infrastructure.[^318oho]
- **Digital twins and industrial software — 2010s–2020s** — Siemens made virtual representations of products, factories, and infrastructure central to its industrial-digitalization strategy.[^byk038][^ou5hup]
- **Siemens Mobility rail systems — ongoing** — The company supplies trains, signaling, and rail-infrastructure technologies; its Signaling X framework is intended to support Austria-wide digital interlocking modernization.[^bsagq1]
- **Siemens–NVIDIA industrial-AI partnership — 2026** — The companies are working toward AI-powered, autonomous, and adaptive manufacturing sites, with Siemens’ Erlangen factory positioned as a blueprint.[^ou5hup]
- **Amtrak Airo — 2026** — Siemens-built Airo trainsets entered passenger service in the Pacific Northwest, with additional trainsets planned through early 2027.[^jyt47s]
- **Solido Layout Analyzer — 2026** — Siemens launched an AI-powered post-layout-analysis tool for semiconductor design at DAC 2026.[^vka1ps]

## Impact

- **Impact on society**
  - Siemens-built Airo trains entered service connecting Seattle, Portland, and Eugene, with the fleet intended to replace the existing Amtrak Cascades fleet over time.[^jyt47s]
  - Siemens’ infrastructure portfolio includes building controls, fire safety, electrification, and energy-management systems deployed in public and commercial environments.[^xxh1wy]

- **Impact on innovation**
  - Siemens has helped industrialize the use of digital twins, industrial software, and connected automation as tools for designing, operating, and optimizing physical production systems.[^byk038][^ou5hup]
  - Its work with NVIDIA is advancing a model of factory automation in which AI agents and industrial data operate directly within manufacturing environments.[^ou5hup]

- **Impact on its industry or domain**
  - Siemens is pushing industrial technology toward integrated “one-company” solutions that combine automation, infrastructure, mobility, software, and AI rather than treating them as isolated product categories.[^318oho][^ou5hup]
  - Its €2.6 billion framework agreement with ÖBB-Infrastruktur illustrates the scale at which digital signaling is being positioned as a national rail-infrastructure modernization platform.[^bsagq1]

- **Historical significance**
  - Siemens’ 1847 founding and subsequent expansion from telegraphy into electrification, industrial systems, transportation, and software place it among the long-lived industrial technology companies that helped shape modern European infrastructure.[^318oho]
  - Its current strategy reflects the broader convergence of industrial engineering, software, AI, and sustainability-oriented infrastructure.[^byk038][^ou5hup]
