# Machine Intelligence Research Institute

- [IMAGE 1: Machine Intelligence Research Institute logo or homepage hero image]
- _MIRI is a nonprofit research and advocacy organization focused on preventing catastrophic outcomes from advanced artificial intelligence._
- MIRI is a nonprofit focused on the existential risk posed by superintelligent AI, combining technical-governance research, public communication, and policy advocacy.[1][7] Search results available here do not reliably establish its founding date, headquarters, or current geographic footprint; consultants track it because it is an unusually prominent voice advocating restrictions on advanced AI development.[1][7]

## Mission and Identity

> “MIRI’s mission is to ensure that smarter-than-human artificial intelligence has a positive impact.” [1]

MIRI presents itself as an organization working to prevent human extinction from superintelligent AI, arguing that advanced systems may become impossible to control if developed before adequate safety and governance mechanisms exist.[7] Its current public-facing work emphasizes technical governance, policy intervention, public communication, and opposition to unrestricted development of superintelligence.[1][7]

- **Stated operating principle:** MIRI’s recent policy position identifies banning the development of superintelligence as its primary policy goal.[7]

## What They Do

MIRI conducts research and advocacy related to AI alignment, technical governance, and existential risk, while communicating its concerns to policymakers and the broader public.[1][7] Recent activities include congressional briefings, policy advising, a research fellowship, public commentary, and an AI-news digest.[1]

- Technical-governance research and policy advising.[1]
- Briefings for members of Congress.[1]
- Research fellowship programming.[1]
- Public communication about advanced-AI risks.[1]
- AI StopWatch, a digest and commentary project covering AI developments.[1]
- Advocacy for a pause or ban on superintelligence development.[7]

- **Impact on society**
