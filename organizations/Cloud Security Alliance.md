# Cloud Security Alliance

- _The Cloud Security Alliance is a global, not-for-profit cybersecurity community that turns cloud, AI, and [[Zero Trust]] security research into practical standards, education, credentials, and implementation guidance._ [^ca4438]
- The organization is a not-for-profit focused on cloud-security assurance and education, operating through volunteer research, standards, credentialing, chapters, associations, governments, and corporate members. [^ca4438] The available sources identify CSA’s 501(c)(3) CSAI Foundation but do not provide a founding date, headquarters city, or current headcount. [^ca4438] Innovation consultants track CSA because its vendor-neutral frameworks and certifications influence how organizations operationalize emerging-security practices. [^ca4438]

## Mission and Identity

> “The Cloud Security Alliance is a not-for-profit organization with a mission to promote the use of best practices for providing security assurance within Cloud Computing, and to provide education on the uses of Cloud Computing to help secure all other forms of computing.” [^ca4438]

CSA says it serves practitioners, corporations, associations, governments, chapters, and other stakeholders through practical research, globally accepted standards, vendor-neutral education, and credentialing. [^ca4438] Its current public positioning extends this work beyond cloud to AI and Zero Trust, with an emphasis on helping organizations adopt new technology securely. [^ca4438]

- **Operating principles:** Volunteer-driven research, vendor neutrality, practical implementation, education, standards, and credentialing. [^ca4438]

## What They Do

CSA conducts volunteer-led cybersecurity research, publishes implementation frameworks and intelligence, develops certifications and education, runs events, and coordinates a broad coalition of industry and public-sector participants. [^ca4438] Its current output spans cloud security, AI security, Zero Trust, vulnerability coordination, and organizational maturity models. [^ca4438]

- **[[Cloud Security]] best practices:** Research and guidance intended to provide security assurance in cloud computing. [^ca4438]
- **Education and credentialing:** Vendor-neutral educational programs and cybersecurity credentials. [^ca4438]
- **Security standards and assurance:** Globally accepted standards and assurance initiatives, including STAR for AI Level 2. [^d3wx49]
- **Research publications:** Cloud, AI, Zero Trust, vulnerability-management, and threat-intelligence research. [^ca4438] [^lfq47z] [^r7pesa]
- **Industry events:** Conferences and summits addressing cloud and emerging-security issues. [^gdgi8r]
- **Intelligence briefings:** Daily CISO briefings synthesizing security research, threat-intelligence feeds, and industry reporting. [^t2a3y7]
- **Community coordination:** Collaboration among practitioners, corporations, associations, governments, chapters, and other stakeholders. [^ca4438]
