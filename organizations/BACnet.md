# BACnet

![BACnet International logo or website hero image](https://bacnetinternational.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2022/08/TradeShow1-1024x768.jpg)

- _BACnet International is an industry association that promotes interoperable building-automation systems through testing, education, and advocacy._
- BACnet International is a **trade / industry body** supporting adoption of the BACnet communications protocol in building automation and control systems.[1] It oversees interoperability testing through the BACnet Testing Laboratories (BTL), maintains a tested-products database, and promotes the protocol globally.[1] The organization matters to consultants because BACnet conformance and interoperability affect how building systems from different suppliers integrate.[1]

## Mission and Identity

> “BACnet International is an industry association that encourages and assists in the successful use of the BACnet protocol in building automation and control systems through interoperability testing, educational programs, and promotional activities.”[1]

BACnet International positions itself as a coordinating and promotional body for organizations using BACnet in building automation.[1] It emphasizes interoperability, testing, education, and global adoption while complementing the work of ASHRAE standards committees and BACnet-related groups worldwide.[1]

- **Operating principles:** Open interoperability, conformance testing, education, and promotion of the BACnet standard.[1]

## What They Do

BACnet International supports the practical adoption of BACnet by organizing interoperability testing, providing educational programming, and promoting the protocol to the building-automation industry.[1] It also operates the BACnet Testing Laboratories and maintains a global database of tested products.[1]

- Operates or oversees the **BACnet Testing Laboratories (BTL)**.[1]
- Maintains a global listing of tested BACnet products.[1]
- Promotes BACnet as a global building-automation communications protocol.[1]
- Provides educational programs about BACnet and interoperability.[1]
- Conducts promotional activities for BACnet adoption.[1]
- Supports a corporate membership community of more than 170 building-automation suppliers.[1]
