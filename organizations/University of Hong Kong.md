# University of Hong Kong

- [IMAGE 1: University of Hong Kong Main Building and campus exterior]
- _The University of Hong Kong is Hong Kong’s flagship research university, combining broad-based education with internationally oriented research and innovation._
- The University of Hong Kong is a university founded in 1911 and based in Hong Kong.[1] It serves as a degree-granting institution and research organization, and consultants track it as a source of talent, scientific research, technology transfer, and regional innovation activity.[2][10]

## Mission and Identity

> “To pursue excellence in teaching and learning, research and innovation, and knowledge exchange.” [2]

HKU presents itself as a research-led institution connecting education, scientific discovery, innovation, government, industry, and society.[2][10] Its current positioning emphasizes aligning research breakthroughs with national strategies while maintaining international and interdisciplinary engagement.[2][10]

- **Stated operating principles:** Excellence in teaching and learning, research and innovation, and knowledge exchange.[2]

## What They Do

HKU delivers undergraduate and postgraduate education, conducts research across disciplines, and transfers knowledge through partnerships, innovation programmes, and engagement with industry and government.[2][3][10] Its activities include conventional academic teaching as well as interdisciplinary courses, career development, research commercialization, and public-facing research events.[3][5][10]

- Broad undergraduate and postgraduate degree education.[2]
- Faculty-based research across medicine, science, engineering, education, sustainability, and other fields.[4][9][10]
- Interdisciplinary innovation education through the LINK programme, combining artificial intelligence, design thinking, entrepreneurship, leadership, and industry immersion.[3]
- Research commercialization and startup support through the HKU Entrepreneurship Engine Fund.[10]
- Innovation Week, showcasing more than 120 university innovations across biotechnology, medicine, smart living, architecture, engineering, and sustainability.[10]
- Career services and employer engagement, including the 2026 Autumn Career Fair.[5]
- International health and academic collaboration, including HKUMed’s role in the World Health Summit Regional Meeting 2027.[9]

## Milestones and Signature Output

- HKUMed is structurally part of HKU and is the university’s health and medical platform.[9]
- Industry, investment, government, and academic representatives participate in HKU Innovation Week.[10]
- The World Health Summit is a major international health partner connected to HKUMed’s planned 2027 regional meeting.[9]
