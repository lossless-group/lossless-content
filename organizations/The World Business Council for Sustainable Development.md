## Mission and Identity

WBCSD positions itself as a platform where businesses collaborate on practical sustainability implementation and policy alignment. Its published work emphasizes accelerating a low-carbon and circular economy, nature-positive action, climate adaptation, sustainable agriculture, inclusive transitions, and responsible technology governance. ## What They Do

WBCSD convenes member companies and external stakeholders, produces guidance and research, supports sector and regional initiatives, and develops common frameworks intended to improve comparability and coordinated action. [^x1sw3c] [^sx9owc] [^uvi6ps] [^nljzs1] Its value proposition is primarily collaborative and knowledge-based rather than the sale of conventional commercial products. [^x1sw3c] [^nljzs1]

- **Climate and decarbonization:** Guidance on supply-chain decarbonization, electrification, avoided emissions, and climate-smart agriculture. [^nwk7v9] [^ru4t27] [^nljzs1] [^rtabw7]
- **Circular economy and alternative feedstocks:** Frameworks for defining, assessing, and scaling sustainable alternatives to fossil-based feedstocks. [^x1sw3c]
- **Nature and biodiversity:** Nature reporting support, regenerative-landscape initiatives, and preparation for biodiversity-related policy processes. [^uvi6ps] [^05dgdq]
- **Sustainable agriculture:** Programs and case studies addressing climate-smart agriculture, farmer transitions, and value-chain investment. [^ftwb17] [^ru4t27] [^qxzz8o]
- **Finance and investment:** Work on financing regenerative landscapes, adaptation, and investment barriers to the energy transition. [^ftwb17] [^rtabw7] [^5qfe7e]
- **Responsible artificial intelligence:** Business guidance on governance structures and responsible AI practices beyond minimum compliance. [^98tdjz]
- **Policy and regional engagement:** Recommendations for governments and businesses, including ASEAN’s inclusive green transition. [^sx9owc]
