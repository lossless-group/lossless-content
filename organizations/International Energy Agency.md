## What They Do

The IEA produces recurring market reports, forecasts and sector analyses for governments and other energy-system decision-makers. [^bkp5iq] [^aki211] [^0qy8sc] It also supports coordinated responses to energy disruptions, including collective oil-stock releases, and evaluates policy and technology options across electricity, transport, buildings, industry and data centers. [^86wnwh] [^fe7zwy] [^1my2ei] [^m13le4]

- **Energy-security coordination:** Supports member-government collective action during oil-market disruptions; approximately 325 million barrels had been released under the March 2026 action by October 2026. [^86wnwh]
- **Oil-market analysis:** Publishes the Oil Market Report, covering supply, demand, inventories, prices, refining and trade. [^0qy8sc]
- **Renewables analysis:** Publishes annual forecasts covering hydropower, solar, wind, geothermal, biofuels and biogases. [^bkp5iq]
- **Energy-efficiency analysis:** Tracks energy intensity, demand, investment, employment and policy across major sectors. [^aki211]
- **Buildings policy:** Assesses efficiency, electrification, equipment standards and clean cooking as routes to reduce building energy intensity. [^1my2ei]
- **Digital-energy policy:** Advises on integrating data centers into electricity systems, including affordability, sustainability, security, emissions and water-use concerns. [^m13le4]
- **Technology collaboration:** Supports cooperative research and development through technology-collaboration programs, including wind-energy work with governments and industry. [^pqhjc2]
