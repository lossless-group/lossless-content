# Framework

![Framework homepage hero image showing a modular laptop with its components separated](https://images.frame.work/frameworkmarketplace/adzd5p1ZCF7ETIsS_fw-for-business_hero_desktop.jpg?auto=format,compress)

_*Framework is a modular computer company that designs repairable laptops and desktops to reduce electronic waste and give users long-term, upgradable hardware.*_ [^v1szce] [^0wnto5]

Framework is a **for-profit computer hardware company** that makes modular, repairable laptops—such as the Framework Laptop 13 Pro, Framework Laptop 13, Framework Laptop 16, and Framework Laptop 12—and sells individual components through its own marketplace. [^v1szce] [^o9uh0n] [^0wnto5] [^w0l238] [^8dynf2] It operates globally via direct online sales, with a strong presence in North America and Europe, and was founded in **2021**. [^t0erg9] A consultant tracks Framework because it is a leading reference case for circular-economy hardware and user-upgradable PCs, influencing how OEMs think about sustainability and repairability in personal computing. [^v1szce] [^q8dvao] [^0wnto5] [^k5iv9q]

## Mission and Identity

- **Stated mission:** Tech coverage describes Framework as “a company that makes modular repairable computers,” emphasizing design for repairability and modularity as its core mission. [^v1szce]

Framework positions itself as serving consumers who want laptops and desktops that are easy to repair, upgrade, and customize, with individual components sold separately through its marketplace. [^v1szce] [^0wnto5] It aims to change the PC industry by offering long-lived, upgradable hardware and by engaging an active community around repair, configuration, and resale of Framework devices. [^8a7pk5] [^o9uh0n] [^f2u41x] [^s2rfsn] [^f3scwy] [^0z99xx] [^t0erg9] Reviews and coverage highlight values around sustainability, repairability, and user control, describing its products as the “best repairable laptop” it has shipped and as a “MacBook Pro for Linux users” focused on openness and modular design. [^0wnto5] [^1xvj9o] [^k5iv9q]

- Stated values and principles include modular design, repairability, and sustainability, reflected in its focus on individually replaceable components, DIY editions, and marketplace-based upgrades rather than sealed, disposable devices. [^v1szce] [^q8dvao] [^0wnto5] [^1xvj9o] [^k5iv9q]

## What They Do

Framework designs, manufactures, and sells modular laptops and desktops that can be easily repaired and upgraded, generating revenue from device sales and individual components offered through its marketplace. [^v1szce] [^q8dvao] [^0wnto5] Day-to-day activities include engineering new laptop models (such as the Laptop 13 Pro and Laptop 16), maintaining BIOS and drivers, and supporting a community-driven ecosystem for configuration, resale, and support. [^8a7pk5] [^o9uh0n] [^0wnto5] [^f3scwy] [^0z99xx] [^w0l238] [^t0erg9]

- Framework Laptop 13 Pro — a thin-and-light, “Linux-first” modular laptop positioned as a “MacBook Pro for Linux users,” with Intel Core Ultra processors, modular expansion cards, and a DIY and pre-built purchase model. [^q8dvao] [^0wnto5] [^1xvj9o] [^k5iv9q]
- Framework Laptop 13 — a modular 13-inch laptop available in DIY and pre-built configurations, using AMD Ryzen and Intel processors, with configurable RAM, storage, and expansion cards. [^o9uh0n] [^f2u41x] [^s2rfsn] [^t0erg9]
- Framework Laptop 16 — a larger modular laptop with AMD Ryzen 7040 series CPUs and discrete graphics options, designed with an expansion bay for further customization. [^8a7pk5] [^f3scwy] [^0z99xx]
- Framework Laptop 12 — a smaller laptop form factor with modular components, such as 13th Gen Intel Core i5 processors, upgradeable RAM and storage, and expansion cards. [^8dynf2]
- DIY Edition devices — kits sold without certain components (e.g., storage, memory, OS) so customers can assemble and configure systems themselves. [^q8dvao] [^0wnto5] [^f2u41x] [^f3scwy]
- Expansion cards and modules — swappable ports (USB-C, USB-A, HDMI, DisplayPort, Ethernet, MicroSD) and other modules that allow port configuration and functional customization. [^o9uh0n] [^f2u41x] [^s2rfsn] [^f3scwy] [^t0erg9]
- Component marketplace — Framework sells each component individually (mainboards, RAM, storage, displays, chargers, and more) via its marketplace, enabling upgrades and repairs over time. [^0wnto5]
- BIOS & drivers support — Framework maintains a dedicated BIOS and drivers portal for its devices, supporting ongoing firmware updates and OS compatibility. [^w0l238]

## History and Origin Story

Framework was founded in 2021 to address the problem of disposable, non-upgradeable laptops by creating modular, repairable computers that users can maintain and upgrade themselves. [^v1szce] [^0wnto5] [^t0erg9] The company’s origin story centers on a small team (reported as “sub 100 people” by 2026) building a new kind of PC business model around component marketplaces and DIY hardware, with subsequent inflection points including new product lines (Laptop 16, Laptop 13 Pro) and growing recognition as a leader in repairable laptops. [^fqxcw7] [^8a7pk5] [^q8dvao] [^0wnto5] [^1xvj9o] [^k5iv9q]

- **2021** — Framework “didn’t exist until 2021,” marking its founding year and initial launch of modular laptops. [^t0erg9]
- **2023–2024** — Framework Laptop 16 DIY Edition with AMD Ryzen 7040 Series CPU and expansion bay shell appears in community listings, indicating commercial availability of the larger form factor. [^f3scwy] [^0z99xx]
- **April 2026** — Framework announces the Laptop 13 Pro; CEO Nirav Patel calls it the “MacBook Pro for Linux users,” marking an inflection point toward premium, Linux-first positioning. [^1xvj9o]
- **July 27, 2026** — Multiple independent reviews of the Laptop 13 Pro are published, with coverage noting that it is “definitively the best repairable laptop the company has ever shipped.” [^q8dvao] [^0wnto5] [^1xvj9o] [^k5iv9q]
- **August 7, 2026** — TechCrunch reports a data breach affecting Framework customers due to an upstream cyberattack at business-intelligence provider Metabase, indicating a significant operational and security event. [^v1szce]

## Impact

- **Impact on society:** Framework’s modular, repairable laptops give consumers the ability to upgrade and repair devices instead of replacing them, which coverage describes as central to its design philosophy and highlights as “repairable” computers, contributing to reduced electronic waste. [^v1szce] [^q8dvao] [^0wnto5] [^1xvj9o] [^k5iv9q]
- **Impact on innovation:** Framework has popularized a modern, consumer-ready model of modular laptops, with individually sold components through a marketplace and expansion card systems, making it a reference point for innovative, repair-focused PC design. [^v1szce] [^q8dvao] [^0wnto5] [^1xvj9o]
- **Impact on its industry or domain:** Reviews describe the Laptop 13 Pro as the “best repairable laptop” Framework has shipped and note its positioning as a “MacBook Pro for Linux users,” pushing traditional OEMs by demonstrating demand for repairable, Linux-first premium hardware. [^0wnto5] [^1xvj9o] [^k5iv9q]
- **Historical significance:** As a company founded in 2021 that quickly became a leading example of modular, repairable laptops, Framework is emerging as a significant case in the historical narrative of sustainable consumer electronics and circular-economy hardware models. [^v1szce] [^0wnto5] [^t0erg9]
