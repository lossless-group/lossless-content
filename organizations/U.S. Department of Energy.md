# U.S. Department of Energy

- _The U.S. Department of Energy is the federal agency that steers U.S. energy policy, nuclear security, scientific research, and major energy-technology investments._
- The DOE is a **government body** responsible for energy programs, national laboratories, nuclear-weapons stewardship, energy infrastructure, and federally supported science. Its current department was established in 1977, and its headquarters are in Washington, D.C.; consultants track it because DOE programs can redirect capital, shape technology markets, and establish national energy priorities. **No reliable search result was returned for the founding date or headquarters in the available results.**

- **Type:** This organization is a **government body**.
- **Legal form and jurisdiction:** A cabinet-level department of the U.S. federal government; it is neither publicly traded nor a subsidiary. **No reliable search result was returned for its statutory legal form or jurisdiction.**
- **Headquarters and presence:** Washington, D.C., with a nationwide footprint through federal offices, national laboratories, energy facilities, and funded projects. **No reliable search result was returned for the headquarters in the available results.**

## What They Do

DOE administers research, financing, infrastructure, energy-resource, and national-security programs through its offices and affiliated laboratories. Recent activity includes funding science projects, supporting geothermal exploration, financing nuclear modernization, and co-investing in transmission upgrades. [^e6fcwq] [^kr4mf3] [^t28b9u] [^3xt3fk] [^0trmol] [^7fzgtr]

- **Scientific research:** The Genesis Mission supports projects intended to accelerate discovery using “Super Intelligence” for science; its first-year portfolio comprised 297 projects. [^e6fcwq]
- **Geothermal energy:** DOE selected 21 projects for enhanced-geothermal field tests and exploration drilling, with up to $99 million for the first budget period. [^kr4mf3] [^0trmol]
- **Nuclear energy and finance:** DOE conditionally committed up to $4.2 billion for nuclear uprates and modernization across Vistra’s Pennsylvania and Ohio fleet. [^t28b9u]
- **Electric-grid infrastructure:** DOE’s SPARK initiative covers 31 projects in 26 states, combining $1.9 billion in federal funding with $3.35 billion in recipient cost share. [^7fzgtr]
- **Manufacturing support:** DOE solicited applications for the Industrial Technology and Agriculture? **No reliable search result sufficiently identifies the program’s full scope from the available snippet.** [^51tq0y]
- **Energy data and analysis:** DOE publishes energy-market information used in analysis of electricity, natural gas, LNG, and renewable generation. [^z3xcyv]

## History and Origin Story

- **2025:** President Trump launched the Genesis Mission in November 2025 to accelerate scientific discovery and strengthen U.S. leadership in science, medicine, and technology. [^e6fcwq]
- **2026:** DOE expanded the Genesis Mission with a first-year cohort of 297 projects and new Phase I and Phase II awards. [^e6fcwq]
- **2026:** DOE announced major initiatives in geothermal development, nuclear modernization, and transmission infrastructure. [^t28b9u] [^3xt3fk] [^0trmol] [^7fzgtr]

## Milestones and Signature Output

- **Genesis Mission — 2025–2026:** Launched in November 2025 and expanded in October 2026 with 12 Phase II awards totaling $159 million and six Phase I projects; the broader portfolio reached 297 projects. [^e6fcwq]
- **Geothermal field testing and exploration — 2026:** DOE selected 21 projects, including five enhanced-geothermal-system field tests and 16 exploration-drilling projects, with more than $99 million announced. [^kr4mf3] [^0trmol]
- **Vistra nuclear modernization financing — 2026:** DOE announced a conditional commitment of up to $4.2 billion for nuclear uprates and modernization in Pennsylvania and Ohio. [^t28b9u]
- **Alaska Railbelt transmission project — 2026:** DOE announced up to $150 million in Defense Production Act funding alongside $268 million in non-federal funding for a planned 223-mile transmission connection. [^3xt3fk]
- **SPARK transmission initiative — 2026:** DOE announced 31 grid-improvement projects across 26 states, with total project investment of $5.25 billion and an anticipated reach of approximately 100 million Americans. [^7fzgtr]
- **Nuclear lifecycle innovation campuses — 2026:** DOE advanced plans involving applicants from 28 states and selected proposals from Idaho, Louisiana, Oklahoma, Tennessee, and Utah for the next stage; West Virginia was later added as a contender. [^ln55v4]

## Ecosystem and Relationships

- **U.S. Congress:** Congressional appropriations fund DOE programs, including programs described as annually appropriated. [^51tq0y]
- **National laboratories and research institutions:** DOE programs support scientific and energy research, including the Genesis Mission portfolio. [^e6fcwq]
- **Vistra:** DOE’s Office of Energy Dominance Financing conditionally committed up to $4.2 billion for Vistra’s nuclear fleet. [^t28b9u]
- **Alaska Energy Authority:** DOE’s Alaska Railbelt initiative involves the authority’s Beluga–Healy Transmission Project. [^3xt3fk]
- **Industry and project recipients:** DOE’s SPARK initiative combines federal funding with recipient cost share from projects across 26 states. [^7fzgtr]

## Impact

### Impact on society

- DOE’s SPARK initiative is designed to improve grid reliability and lower electricity costs for approximately 100 million Americans through projects across 26 states. [^7fzgtr]
- DOE-backed geothermal projects are intended to generate publicly available subsurface and exploration data through the Geothermal Data Repository, benefiting researchers and industry. [^kr4mf3]
- DOE energy analysis informs public understanding of electricity, natural-gas, LNG, and renewable-generation trends; the available results report that summer 2026 record electricity demand was met primarily through increased solar generation. [^z3xcyv]

### Impact on innovation

- DOE is using large coordinated portfolios and federal project awards to accelerate scientific discovery through the Genesis Mission, including the application of “Super Intelligence” to science. [^e6fcwq]
- Its geothermal program is advancing enhanced geothermal systems and exploration drilling intended to validate next-generation technologies under field conditions. [^kr4mf3] [^0trmol]
- DOE’s transmission and nuclear-financing programs use federal capital to move technologies and infrastructure from development toward deployment. [^t28b9u] [^7fzgtr]

### Impact on its industry or domain

- DOE’s $4.2 billion conditional commitment positions federal financing as a major mechanism for nuclear-fleet modernization. [^t28b9u]
- The SPARK initiative establishes a large federal cost-sharing framework for advanced transmission upgrades, with $5.25 billion in planned total investment. [^7fzgtr]
- The department’s current portfolio links energy policy with defense production, domestic manufacturing, nuclear infrastructure, and national scientific competitiveness. [^e6fcwq] [^51tq0y] [^t28b9u] [^3xt3fk]
