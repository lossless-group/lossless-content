The National Research and Innovation Agency (Indonesian: Badan Riset dan Inovasi Nasional, or BRIN) is Indonesia's sole cabinet-level national research agency, headquartered in Jakarta. [^ncjuz7] [^3ns97v] 
## Key Facts

* Formation: Established as an independent non-ministerial agency on April 28, 2021, and granted cabinet-level status in August 2021. [^3ns97v] 
* Predecessors: Absorbed major historical research bodies into a single unified structure, including LIPI (Indonesian Institute of Sciences), BPPT, BATAN (National Nuclear Energy Agency), LAPAN (National Institute of Aeronautics and Space), and the Eijkman Institute. [^ncjuz7] [^23bl1w] 
* Structure: Comprises 12 research organizations and 85 research centers covering fields like aeronautics, nuclear power, health, agriculture, and social sciences. [^0962ae] 
* Recent Developments: In October 2026, BRIN partnered in pilot carbon-capture technologies like the Olimpus Apollo 1.0 photobioreactor and supported educational research for local community programs. [^6nol2j] [^5nxny3] 

You can read more on the [National Research and Innovation Agency Official Website](https://www.brin.go.id/en). [^0fuwuj]
