# Stanford University

- _Stanford University is a research university whose interdisciplinary culture connects fundamental scholarship with technology, medicine, policy, and entrepreneurship._
- Stanford is a university and research institution focused on teaching, research, health care, and public service. Its schools and institutes span disciplines including engineering, medicine, business, sustainability, and human-centered AI. [^q4udw4] [^mj0p5p] [^ig4v85] Founded in 1885, it is headquartered in Stanford, California, and its primary geography is the San Francisco Bay Area. Consultants track Stanford because its research, talent, and commercialization networks influence emerging technologies and institutional innovation.

## Mission and Identity

> “The Stanford Doerr School of Sustainability advances knowledge, solutions, and impact through research and education, creating a thriving, enduring future for humanity and the planet.” [^q4udw4]

Stanford’s schools and institutes present the university as a place where education and discovery address major societal challenges. [^q4udw4] Its human-centered AI institute emphasizes technological innovation alongside societal impact, interdisciplinary research, and technologies that improve human productivity and quality of life. [^mj0p5p] Stanford Medicine frames its role around patient care, education, and research aimed at preventing illness and improving diagnosis and treatment. [^ig4v85]

- **Stated values and principles:** Interdisciplinary research, human-centered technology, societal impact, and the translation of scholarship into practical solutions are repeatedly foregrounded by Stanford’s major institutes. [^q4udw4] [^mj0p5p]

## What They Do

Stanford educates undergraduate, graduate, professional, and medical students while conducting research across science, engineering, medicine, business, law, humanities, and social sciences. Its operating model combines tuition and philanthropy with endowment-supported teaching, financial aid, faculty research, and public-service activity; the available results identify the endowment as a major funding source but do not provide complete university financial statements. [^iv5b3a]

- **Stanford Medicine:** Medical education, clinical care, and biomedical research. [^u6i2gm] [^ig4v85]
- **Stanford Doerr School of Sustainability:** Sustainability education, discovery, applied research, and technology and policy solutions. [^q4udw4] [^bwl556]
- **Stanford Institute for Human-Centered AI:** AI research, education, policy engagement, fellowships, and grant programs. [^mj0p5p]
- **Stanford Graduate School of Business:** Business education and research. [^qkjp8k]
- **Stanford Sustainability Accelerator:** Support for teams developing solutions in climate resilience, clean energy, ocean health, and water access; it has awarded nearly $5 million across 22 departments. [^q4udw4]
- **Interdisciplinary research institutes:** Cross-school research in areas such as environmental policy, economic policy, food security, and human health. [^bwl556] [^8g5163]

## Leadership and People

- [Marshall Burke](https://sustainability.stanford.edu/people/marshall-burke/) — professor of global environmental policy — senior fellow at several Stanford institutes, including the Woods Institute for the Environment and the Stanford Institute for Economic Policy Research. [^8g5163]

## History and Origin Story

Stanford University was founded in 1885 as a private university in California; the available search results do not provide a detailed account of its founders, original mandate, or founding location. Its modern trajectory is marked by the creation of interdisciplinary institutes and, most recently, the launch of the Doerr School of Sustainability as Stanford’s first new school in 75 years. [^bwl556]

- **1885** — Stanford University was founded. [^bwl556]
- **2022** — The Stanford Doerr School of Sustainability launched as the university’s first new school in 75 years. [^bwl556]
- **2026** — Stanford professor Karl Deisseroth received the Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine for work leading to optogenetics. [^f2mxd9]
- **2026** — Stanford’s STRIVE initiative began studying physiological resilience, recovery, aging, sleep, and healthspan. [^ig4v85]

- **Stanford Doerr School of Sustainability** — 2022 — established Stanford’s first new school in 75 years and consolidated interdisciplinary sustainability education and research. [^bwl556]
- **Stanford Institute for Human-Centered AI** — established before October 2026 — created a university-wide platform for AI research, fellowships, grants, education, and analysis of societal impacts. [^mj0p5p]
- **Optogenetics research** — 2026 recognition — Karl Deisseroth’s Stanford work helped enable control of individual nerve cells in living brains and received the Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine. [^f2mxd9]
