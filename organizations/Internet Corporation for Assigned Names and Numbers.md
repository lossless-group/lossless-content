# Internet Corporation for Assigned Names and Numbers

- _ICANN is the nonprofit, globally coordinated institution that helps keep the Internet’s domain-name and identifier system stable, secure, and interoperable._
- ICANN is a nonprofit public benefit corporation formed in 1998 to coordinate the Internet’s unique identifiers, including domain names and associated systems. [^yu7hcv] Its work is global and community-led, making it relevant to consultants tracking digital infrastructure, Internet governance, cybersecurity, and the evolution of online naming markets. [^ayz3bu]

- **Type:** This organization is a nonprofit.
- **Legal form and jurisdiction:** ICANN is a nonprofit public benefit corporation; the available source confirms its nonprofit corporate form but does not specify the incorporation jurisdiction. [^yu7hcv]
- **Headquarters and presence:** ICANN has a global operating footprint with participants from around the world. [^yu7hcv]
- **Size:** No reliable current headcount or membership figure was available in the returned sources.

## Mission and Identity

> “ICANN's mission is to help ensure a stable, secure, and unified global Internet.” [^ayz3bu]

ICANN positions itself as the coordinator of the Internet’s unique identifier systems, helping ensure that domain names and related identifiers work consistently across the world. [^ayz3bu] Its self-described institutional identity emphasizes global participation, accountability, transparency, security, stability, resiliency, and a multistakeholder model. [^ayz3bu] [^v7j6zz]

- **Operating principles:** ICANN’s work is organized around Bylaws obligations, strategic objectives, accountability reviews, transparency mechanisms, diversity, and multistakeholder participation. [^ayz3bu]

## What They Do

ICANN coordinates and supports the Internet’s unique identifiers, particularly the Domain Name System, so that users and computers can reliably locate one another online. [^ayz3bu] [^yu7hcv] It also administers policy and operational processes involving registries, registrars, generic top-level domains, [[Vocabulary/Domain Name Services|DNS]] security, and contracted-party compliance. [^v7j6zz] [^83pcsv]

- Coordinates the Domain Name System and other unique Internet identifiers. [^ayz3bu]
- Oversees processes for generic top-level domains and new gTLD application rounds. [^vo1jwk] [^v7j6zz]
- Supports DNS security and stability, including DNSSEC-related work. [^czxha2]
- Works with contracted parties on DNS abuse mitigation and enforcement. [^83pcsv]
- Operates through a global multistakeholder policy-development model. [^v7j6zz]
- Publishes accountability, transparency, diversity, strategic-planning, and financial reporting materials. [^ayz3bu]

## History and Origin Story

ICANN was formed in 1998 as a nonprofit public benefit corporation to coordinate the Internet’s unique identifiers through a global community of participants. [^yu7hcv] Its continuing mandate centers on maintaining a stable, secure, unified, and resilient Internet while balancing policy input from multiple stakeholder groups. [^ayz3bu] [^v7j6zz]

- **1998** — ICANN was formed as a nonprofit public benefit corporation. [^yu7hcv]
- **2025** — The Board approved procedures for terminating remaining unsuccessful 2012-round applications and allowing eligible applicants to seek refunds. [^vo1jwk]

- **2026** — The Board affirmed that the 2026–2030 strategic plan would remain unchanged after its annual strategy review. [^v7j6zz]

## Impact

- **Impact on society**
  - ICANN’s coordination of unique identifiers underpins the global ability of users and computers to locate one another through the Internet’s naming system. [^ayz3bu] [^yu7hcv]
  - Its DNS-abuse investigations affect registries, registrars, and domain holders by bringing contracted-party practices into a formal enforcement process. [^83pcsv]

- **Impact on innovation**
  - ICANN’s new gTLD programs expanded the institutional pathway for introducing additional generic top-level domains, creating a governance mechanism for experimentation in Internet naming. [^vo1jwk] [^v7j6zz]
  - Its multistakeholder model institutionalized a form of global Internet governance involving community bodies, public comment, Board review, and accountability mechanisms. [^58bad2] [^v7j6zz]

- **Impact on its industry or domain**
  - ICANN’s funding model and policy processes directly shape the operating environment for registries, registrars, and new-domain applicants. [^y7oxpf] [^v7j6zz]
  - Its DNS-abuse enforcement work increases pressure on contracted parties to address abuse beyond individual domains and across broader operational systems. [^83pcsv]

- **Historical significance**
  - ICANN has served since 1998 as a central nonprofit institution for coordinating the Internet’s unique identifier infrastructure. [^yu7hcv]
  - Its ongoing role links technical DNS coordination with global, multistakeholder governance of a critical communications layer. [^ayz3bu] [^v7j6zz]
