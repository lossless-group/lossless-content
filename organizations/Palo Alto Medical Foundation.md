# Palo Alto Medical Foundation

- [IMAGE 1: Palo Alto Medical Foundation clinical facility or Sutter Health–PAMF signage]
- _Palo Alto Medical Foundation is a large, nonprofit, multispecialty healthcare organization serving patients across the San Francisco Bay Area._
- Palo Alto Medical Foundation, formally associated with Sutter Palo Alto Medical Foundation, provides outpatient medical care and operational support to affiliated physicians within the Sutter Health network.
