# VC Labs

- _VC Lab is [[content-areas/Finance/Private-Markets/Decile Group|Decile Group]]’s free accelerator and research-and-development center for emerging venture-capital managers._
- VC Lab is a venture-capital accelerator operated as Decile Group’s R&D center. [^rr84wp] It helps participants launch venture firms through a structured program, and its community spans more than 88 countries and 1,000-plus investors. [^rr84wp] [^9bmvh7] The available sources do not state a founding date, legal headquarters, or incorporation jurisdiction; consultants track it because it is a significant infrastructure and talent pipeline for new venture funds.

## Identity and Form

- **Type:** This organization is a **venture-capital firm / accelerator** focused on developing emerging fund managers. [^rr84wp] [^x4h4mp]
- **Legal form and jurisdiction:** No reliable source found for VC Labs’ standalone legal form, incorporation jurisdiction, or public/private status; it operates within Decile Group. [^rr84wp] [^9bmvh7]
- **Headquarters and presence:** No reliable source found for a physical headquarters; its programs are global, with participants in more than 88 countries and sessions delivered online. [^rr84wp] [^j0mhxw]
- **Size:** VC Lab reports a community of 1,000-plus investors and more than 1,000 venture firms launched across more than 90 countries. [^x4h4mp] [^j0mhxw]
- **Where it lives online:** [Homepage](https://govclab.com/)

## Mission and Identity

> “Venture capital done right is one of the most powerful tools we have for solving the biggest problems we face.” [^x4h4mp]

VC Lab presents itself as an open-access alternative to the traditional venture-capital career path, which it describes as historically closed to people without a decade inside an established firm. [^vxd94o] It says its programs are free, charge no fees, and take no equity because broader participation can improve the industry. [^x4h4mp] [^vxd94o]

- **Operating principle:** Expand access to venture-capital careers and fund formation through free programming with no equity taken from participants. [^x4h4mp] [^9bmvh7]

## What They Do

VC Lab runs a free 14-week accelerator for first-time fund managers, combining education, peer groups, fundraising work, and access to a global investor community. [^x4h4mp] [^9bmvh7] It also functions as Decile Group’s R&D center for emerging venture capital and directs later-stage fund managers toward the Emerging Institute and prospective limited partners toward the LP Institute. [^rr84wp] [^j0mhxw]

- **VC Lab accelerator:** Helps first-time managers launch venture firms and raise institutional capital. [^x4h4mp]
- **Fundraising sprints:** Participants work through the steps of building demand and raising a fund. [^rr84wp] [^9bmvh7]
- **Cohort-based education:** Programs organize managers into cohorts and peer groups based on fund model. [^9bmvh7]
- **Investor network:** Connects participants with a community of more than 1,000 investors across over 88 countries. [^rr84wp]
- **Emerging Institute:** Serves managers raising funds two through four. [^x4h4mp]
- **LP Institute:** Educates and connects people investing, or preparing to invest, as limited partners. [^j0mhxw]
- **Post-launch administration:** Decile Partners may provide fund administration after launch, but participation is optional. [^9bmvh7]

VC Lab was created within Decile Group to widen access to venture-capital fund formation and challenge the industry’s traditional, experience-gated entry route.
