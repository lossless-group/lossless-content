# Stanford Institute for Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence

![Stanford HAI homepage or campus-facing hero image illustrating interdisciplinary human-centered AI research](https://hai.stanford.edu/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fhai.stanford.edu%2Fassets%2Fimages%2Fhai-offices.jpg&w=3840&q=100)

- _Stanford HAI is [[organizations/Stanford University|Stanford University]]’s interdisciplinary institute for advancing AI research, education, policy, and applications around human needs and societal benefit._ [^roz9mi]
- Stanford HAI is a **university-based research institute** focused on discovery, technological innovation, educational transformation, and societal impact. [^roz9mi] It operates across Stanford’s seven schools and connects technical AI research with work on policy, ethics, education, and society. [^roz9mi] Consultants track it because it is an influential source of AI research, measurement, governance ideas, and executive education.

## Mission and Identity

> “Stanford HAI’s mission is to advance humanity through AI and data science.” [^roz9mi]

The institute says its work spans “discovery, technological innovation, educational transformation, and societal impact.” [^roz9mi] It positions human-centered AI as an approach that prioritizes “human needs, values, and well-being” throughout the development and deployment of AI systems. [^roz9mi] Its stated audience includes researchers, educators, business leaders, policymakers, civil servants, and civil society. [^roz9mi]

- **Stated principles:** Stanford HAI says AI should be “collaborative, augmentative, and enhancing human productivity and quality of life.” [^roz9mi]

## What They Do

Stanford HAI funds and convenes interdisciplinary research, develops education for technical and nontechnical audiences, produces policy analysis, and supports AI measurement and governance work. [^3gsmoq] [^roz9mi] Its activities serve Stanford researchers and students as well as leaders in education, industry, government, policy, and civil society. [^roz9mi]

- **Interdisciplinary AI research** spanning technical foundations, applications, and human dimensions. [^3gsmoq] [^roz9mi]
- **Research funding and grant programs** supporting Stanford AI and data-science work. [^roz9mi]
- **Data Science Scholars Program**, a two-year fellowship for Stanford PhD researchers across disciplines. [^3gsmoq]
- **Executive and professional education** on AI technologies and business implications. [^roz9mi]
- **Government and policymaker training** focused on emerging-technology governance. [^roz9mi]
- **Policy research and global convenings** addressing AI governance challenges and opportunities. [^roz9mi]
- **AI measurement and evaluation activities**, including seminars and research on how AI systems are assessed. [^rdfi16] [^0hay3e]
- **Public events and expert dialogue**, including the “Prompt Response” series on fast-moving AI developments. [^lf2old]
