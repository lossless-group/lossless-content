# Organization of Economic Co-operation and Development

- _The OECD is an intergovernmental policy forum that turns comparative data and analysis into practical guidance for governments._
- The Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development is a government body and global policy forum working with more than 100 countries to promote individual liberty and improve economic and social well-being. [^p1uvlv] Its headquarters are in Paris, France, and its current Secretary-General is Mathias Cormann. [^p1uvlv] Innovation consultants track it because its comparative indicators, policy recommendations, and international frameworks influence how governments and businesses define economic and social priorities. [^p1uvlv]

## Identity and Form

- **Type:** This organization is a government body—specifically, an intergovernmental organization.
- **Legal form and jurisdiction:** It is an international organization rather than a public company or subsidiary; no ticker or domestic incorporation jurisdiction is applicable.
- **Headquarters and presence:** Paris, France; its policy work has a global footprint, with cooperation involving more than 100 countries. [^p1uvlv]
- **Size:** No reliable source found in the available results for current headcount or member count.

## Mission and Identity

> “Working with over 100 countries, the OECD is a global policy forum that promotes policies to preserve individual liberty and improve the economic and social well-being of people around the world.” [^p1uvlv]

The OECD presents itself as a forum for governments and international partners to develop evidence-based policy across economic, social, digital, agricultural, pension, migration, and tax issues. [^p1uvlv] [^rjqry9] [^p3ioz3] [^n36j0s] [^bdr722] Its public identity foregrounds individual liberty, economic and social well-being, comparative evidence, international cooperation, and policy reform. [^p1uvlv]

- **Operating principle:** Translate member priorities into a funded programme of work while maintaining sound management of the organization’s financial resources. [^wj6sp8]

## What They Do

The OECD produces comparative statistics, outlooks, policy reviews, recommendations, and international frameworks for governments and participating jurisdictions. [^p1uvlv] [^rjqry9] [^p3ioz3] [^n36j0s] [^bdr722] Its work generates value primarily through shared evidence, peer comparison, policy coordination, and technical guidance rather than commercial sales. [^p1uvlv]

- **Economic and social policy analysis:** Publishes cross-country outlooks and policy recommendations. [^p1uvlv] [^rjqry9]
- **Digital policy:** Assesses AI adoption, cloud access, interoperability, open standards, and digital transformation. [^p1uvlv] [^8bjfvu]
- **Pensions policy:** Evaluates retirement systems, pension assets, contributions, financial literacy, and investment governance. [^rjqry9]
- **Agricultural policy:** Monitors government support and provides country-specific reform recommendations. [^n36j0s]
- **Migration and integration:** Tracks migration trends, immigrant labour-market conditions, and integration policy. [^p3ioz3]
- **International tax cooperation:** Hosts the OECD/G20 Inclusive Framework on Base Erosion and Profit Shifting, which had 148 members in October 2026. [^bdr722]
- **Infrastructure policy:** Convenes stakeholders on infrastructure planning, financing, and economic and social impact. [^dss2bm]

## Leadership and People

- [Mathias Cormann](https://www.oecd.org/en/about/secretary-general.html) — Secretary-General — leads the OECD and speaks publicly on digital, pension, agricultural, and tax-policy priorities. [^p1uvlv] [^rjqry9] [^n36j0s] [^bdr722]
- Fabrizia Lapecorella — Deputy Secretary-General — opened the OECD Infrastructure Forum in November 2026. [^dss2bm]

## Ecosystem and Relationships

- OECD member governments and participating jurisdictions are the organization’s core institutional constituency; its published materials describe cooperation with more than 100 countries. [^p1uvlv]
- The G20 is a major institutional partner through the OECD/G20 Inclusive Framework on Base Erosion and Profit Shifting. [^bdr722]
- Guatemala joined the Inclusive Framework in January 2026, contributing to its reported 148-member structure. [^bdr722]
- The Centre on Migration, Policy and Society and the Migration Observatory at the University of Oxford collaborated with the OECD on the 2026 International Migration Outlook launch. [^p3ioz3]
- The OECD Conference Centre in Paris hosted the 2026 Infrastructure Forum. [^dss2bm]
