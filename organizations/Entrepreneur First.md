# Entrepreneur First

![Entrepreneur First website hero section showing its founder-investment offering](https://automatic-wonder-4bcaab3cfc.media.strapiapp.com/unnamed_93_f730d3ed56.webp)

- _Entrepreneurs First is an investor and accelerator that backs exceptional individuals before they have a startup, helping them find cofounders, develop an idea, and build a venture._
- Entrepreneur First is a privately held accelerator and venture investor focused on company formation at the earliest stage. Its program offers an equity-free grant during ideation, up to $250,000 after company formation, and continued follow-on support potentially reaching $5 million through Series B. [^bht1l0] Founded companies in its portfolio are collectively valued at more than $24 billion, making EF a significant observer of frontier startup formation. [^bht1l0]

## Mission and Identity

> “Build a startup from scratch with Entrepreneur First.” [^bht1l0]

EF positions itself as a platform for ambitious people who have not necessarily chosen a company, idea, or cofounder yet. Its model emphasizes identifying individuals early, helping them form founding teams, and supplying initial capital before conventional venture investors would typically invest. [^bht1l0]

- **Operating principle:** Back the entrepreneur before the idea, then support company formation and later fundraising. [^bht1l0]

## What They Do

EF recruits individuals, helps them meet potential cofounders and test ideas, provides financial support during ideation, and invests once a company is formed. [^bht1l0] It generates value through early-stage equity investments and follow-on participation in companies emerging from its programs. [^bht1l0]

- Equity-free grant for the ideation stage. [^bht1l0]
- Up to $250,000 investment after company formation. [^bht1l0]
- Up to $5 million in follow-on funding through future rounds and support up to Series B. [^bht1l0]
- Cofounder discovery and matching. [^bht1l0]
- Startup formation and early validation support. [^bht1l0]
- Portfolio-company support and investor access. [^bht1l0]
