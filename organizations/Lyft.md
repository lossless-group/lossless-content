# Lyft

![Lyft logo and ride-request app branding](https://i.redd.it/glpsod58xmwg1.png)

- _Lyft, Inc. is a public mobility platform that connects riders with drivers and is expanding from rideshare into a broader transportation and membership ecosystem._
- Lyft is a for-profit company operating a rideshare marketplace and related mobility services. Its Q3 2026 results reported $1.8 billion in revenue and $5.6 billion in gross bookings. [^7jlxqb] [^5jdkv8] CEO David Risher remains the company’s public-facing leader as of November 2026. ## Mission and Identity

> “Request a ride whenever you need one. Drivers earn on their own terms.” [^6vl7ga]

Lyft presents itself as a two-sided platform serving riders who need transportation and drivers seeking flexible earning opportunities. [^6vl7ga] Its current positioning also emphasizes convenience, loyalty benefits, charitable giving, and integration with adjacent services through its DoorDash partnership. [^6vl7ga]

- **Stated operating principle:** Lyft emphasizes rider rewards, driver flexibility, and mobility delivered through its app. [^6vl7ga]

## What They Do

Lyft operates an app-based marketplace in which riders request transportation and drivers provide rides, while the company generates revenue from transactions and related services. [^7jlxqb] [^5jdkv8] It also offers membership benefits and has expanded its consumer ecosystem through a partnership with DoorDash. [^6vl7ga]

- On-demand rideshare
- Driver-side earning platform
- Lyft Pink membership
- Lyft Go membership, including ride credits and DashPass benefits
- Lyft Credits
- Corporate and business transportation services
- Partnerships connecting rides with other mobility or delivery services
