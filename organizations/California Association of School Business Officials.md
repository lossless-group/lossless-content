# California Association of School Business Officials

- [IMAGE 1: CASBO logo or Sacramento headquarters imagery]
- _CASBO is California’s professional membership association for school business officials, combining professional development, advocacy, networking, and industry events for the K–12 education finance and operations community._
- CASBO is a **trade body** and membership organization serving California’s K–12 education sector. Its visible activities include conferences, section events, professional-development webinars, legislative analysis, and publications for school business professionals. [^nx8gj9] [^jq4ih3] [^zm3y2f] [^4axjjz] The organization is headquartered at 1001 K Street, 5th Floor, Sacramento, California. ## What They Do

CASBO operates a statewide professional network and event program for school business officials and related education-sector professionals. It creates value through conferences, paid or member-linked participation, professional learning, publications, advocacy information, and associate-member sponsorship or exhibitor relationships. [^nx8gj9] [^jq4ih3] [^qc2wla] [^4axjjz]

- **Conferences and symposia:** CASBO promotes events including the CBO Symposium and CASBO Con. [^nx8gj9] [^71u9mn]
- **Professional development:** The organization publishes and hosts webinars and educational sessions on operational, legal, communications, and technology topics. [^jq4ih3] [^kem7fg]
- **Legislative and policy analysis:** CASBO publishes updates on California legislation affecting school districts, including AB 2008 and AB 218. [^zm3y2f] [^4axjjz]
- **Member sections and networking:** Local or regional sections organize events such as the Sacramento Section Holiday Event. [^dz4zco]
- **Associate-member engagement:** Associate-member participation includes exhibitor and sponsor activity connected to conference benefits. [^jq4ih3]
- **Sector publications and articles:** CASBO distributes articles for school business management and professional-development audiences. [^zm3y2f] [^qc2wla] [^kem7fg]
