# Bateman Horne Center

_An integrated medical and education nonprofit center focused on improving care, understanding, and resources for people with ME/CFS, fibromyalgia, post-viral syndromes, and related comorbidities._

Bateman Horne Center is a **nonprofit clinical and education organization** focused on myalgic encephalomyelitis/chronic fatigue syndrome (ME/CFS), fibromyalgia, post-viral syndromes, and related comorbidities. [^un7y3f] [^fhx2xe] It operates as a specialized medical center providing care and resources to patients while also advancing education about these conditions. [^un7y3f] [^u5nd4u] [^fhx2xe] The center is based in Salt Lake City, Utah, serving primarily a regional patient base alongside wider online educational outreach. [^fhx2xe] [^u5nd4u] A consultant tracks Bateman Horne Center as a niche but influential player in complex chronic illness care and clinician education around ME/CFS and post-viral conditions. [^un7y3f] [^u5nd4u]

## Mission and Identity

> “Improving the lives impacted by myalgic encephalomyelitis/chronic fatigue syndrome (ME/CFS), fibromyalgia (FM), post-viral syndromes, and related comorbidities.”[^un7y3f]

The center positions itself as a specialized resource for patients and families dealing with ME/CFS, fibromyalgia, and post-viral syndromes, emphasizing both clinical care and practical education. [^un7y3f] [^u5nd4u] It highlights serving individuals with complex chronic conditions, caregivers, and clinicians by providing tools for recognizing symptoms such as post-exertional malaise (PEM) and guidance for navigating school, family, and healthcare environments. [^u5nd4u] Its self-image centers on providing “help that doesn’t hurt” through evidence-informed care and accessible educational resources for pediatric and adult populations. [^u5nd4u]

- Stated values and operating principles include providing “free pediatric resources” and practical tools to help families and caregivers understand conditions and manage symptoms, signaling an emphasis on accessibility, education, and supportive care. [^u5nd4u]

## What They Do

Bateman Horne Center delivers day-to-day value through specialty clinical care for ME/CFS, fibromyalgia, and related post-viral conditions, combined with patient and caregiver education. [^un7y3f] [^fhx2xe] It generates impact rather than revenue by offering consultations, developing guides and videos, and making pediatric resources freely available to improve understanding and management of complex chronic illnesses. [^un7y3f] [^u5nd4u]

Main offerings, programs, and activities:

- Specialty outpatient clinical care for patients with ME/CFS, fibromyalgia, and post-viral syndromes, led by clinicians experienced in these conditions. [^un7y3f] [^fhx2xe]
- Development and distribution of **free pediatric resources** to help families understand these conditions and navigate conversations with teachers, relatives, coaches, and healthcare providers. [^u5nd4u]
- A Clinical Care Guide and expert video content from physicians (e.g., Melanie Hoppers, MD) providing practical tools for recognizing PEM, supporting children at school, and managing symptoms. [^u5nd4u]
- Education of caregivers and families through online videos and guides focused on symptom recognition and day-to-day management. [^u5nd4u]
- Public engagement through social media (e.g., Instagram reels) to disseminate clinical education content to a broader audience. [^u5nd4u]
- Participation in researcher profile initiatives, such as featuring founder and medical director Lucinda Bateman, MD, in chronic illness research campaigns. [^un7y3f]
