The supplied retrieved-source block identifies Papermark as a New York-based data-security software company with five employees, providing secure data rooms and document sharing for M&A, due diligence, and fundraising. ## Value Proposition & Features

Papermark positions itself as an open-source, lower-cost alternative to DocSend for secure document sharing and data rooms. Its primary users are founders, investors, and deal teams that need controlled distribution, engagement visibility, and evidence of document access. [^E1] [^9u93r2]

Core features include page-by-page document analytics, which show engagement with individual pages, and granular file or folder permissions that can restrict viewing or downloading. [^E1] [^9u93r2]

The product also supports branded sharing experiences, data-room controls, and security features such as NDA gating, dynamic watermarking, screenshot protection, and custom domains. [^9u93r2]

## Competitive Landscape

### Who it's for, who it's not for

Papermark is aimed at founders, investors, and deal teams sharing pitch decks, fundraising materials, and sensitive transaction documents that require analytics, access controls, branding, or data-room workflows. [^E1] [^9u93r2]

It is less suited to ordinary internal file storage or general-purpose collaboration where deal-specific analytics, permissions, NDA gating, and audit controls are unnecessary.

### Viable Alternatives

- **DocSend:** The closest named alternative for tracked pitch-deck and document sharing; Papermark explicitly positions itself as a DocSend alternative. [^9u93r2]
- **Dedicated virtual data rooms:** Better suited to larger transactions requiring mature compliance, administrator controls, and established enterprise support.
- **General cloud storage:** Appropriate for basic file sharing but typically less specialized for page-level engagement analytics and fundraising workflows.
- **Self-hosted document-sharing tools:** Suitable for organizations prioritizing deployment control and open-source customization over turnkey deal workflows.
