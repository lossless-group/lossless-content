## Value Proposition & Features

Hetzner is a German hosting and infrastructure provider offering dedicated servers, cloud servers, web hosting, storage, and GPU systems from company-operated data centers in Germany and Finland. Its positioning centers on independently operated European infrastructure, data protection, digital sovereignty, and “fair prices.” [1]

- **Cloud servers:** Virtual machines with private networks, firewalls, and direct public-network connectivity. [2]
- **Dedicated servers:** Bare-metal systems for customers needing greater hardware control and predictable performance. [4]
- **GPU infrastructure:** Dedicated GPU servers for large-language-model inference, fine-tuning, rendering, simulation, and data science. [3]
- **Network architecture:** Hetzner Cloud has used Open vSwitch for public networking, VXLAN-based private networks, and stateful cloud firewalls. [2]
- **Emerging network stack:** Hetzner is developing an eBPF-based network stack called hcnet. [7]
- **Data-center operations:** Its infrastructure is operated in Nuremberg, Falkenstein, and Helsinki, with the cited locations described as ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certified. [1]
- **European hosting jurisdiction:** The company emphasizes hosting data and applications within the European legal framework. [1]

# History and Origin Story

Hetzner was founded in 1997 and is headquartered in Gunzenhausen, Germany. [^E1] Its infrastructure evolved from early vServers launched in 2011 to Hetzner Cloud in 2018, followed by Open vSwitch-based public networking, private VXLAN networks, and stateful cloud firewalls. [2]

# Competitive Landscape

## Who it's for, who it's not for

Hetzner is suited to developers, businesses, and technical teams seeking self-managed cloud or bare-metal infrastructure, European data residency, high hardware control, and price-focused hosting. Its GPU and dedicated-server offerings also target AI, rendering, simulation, and data-science workloads. [1][3][4]

It is less suited to customers seeking fully managed application platforms, extensive hyperscaler-native services, or an infrastructure experience with minimal server administration.

## Viable Alternatives

- **Amazon Web Services:** Broad hyperscale cloud portfolio and managed services for organizations prioritizing service breadth.
- **Microsoft Azure:** Enterprise cloud platform with strong Microsoft ecosystem integration.
- **Google Cloud:** Hyperscale infrastructure with data, AI, and machine-learning services.
- **[[Tooling/Software Development/Cloud Infrastructure/OVHcloud]]:** European cloud, dedicated-server, and hosting provider with a similar sovereignty-oriented positioning.
- **DigitalOcean:** Developer-focused cloud infrastructure with simpler product abstractions.
