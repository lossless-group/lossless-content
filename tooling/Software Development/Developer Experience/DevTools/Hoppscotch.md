# Competitive Landscape

## Who it's for, who it's not for

Hoppscotch is positioned for developers seeking an API-development workflow accessible through the web, particularly users interested in open-source or self-hosted tooling.

It is less suited to teams that specifically require a desktop-only workflow or proprietary enterprise capabilities that cannot be verified from the available sources.

- **Bruno** — A desktop-oriented API client and a directly relevant alternative for teams preferring local, file-based workflows.
