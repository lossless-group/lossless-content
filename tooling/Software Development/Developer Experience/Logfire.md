# Pydantic Logfire

## Value Proposition & Features

Logfire is an AI-native observability platform from the Pydantic team, designed to show an application’s complete behavior rather than only its LLM calls. [^t1j1xr] It is built as an opinionated wrapper around OpenTelemetry and supports traces, conversation replay, tool-call inspection, and token and cost breakdowns. [^t1j1xr]

- **AI-agent observability:** Inspect agent runs, model requests, tool calls, and decisions in one trace. [^t1j1xr] [^2m5m2j]
- **OpenTelemetry instrumentation:** Use standard OpenTelemetry-based instrumentation and connect application operations to Logfire traces. [^t1j1xr] [^wxlj6h]
- **Conversation replay:** Review agent conversations and execution behavior after or during a run. [^t1j1xr]
- **Tool-call inspection:** Examine the tools invoked by an agent and the resulting execution details. [^t1j1xr]
- **Token and cost visibility:** Track token usage and estimated model costs. [^t1j1xr] [^wxlj6h]
- **Live agent views:** Inspect agent runs, model runs, and decision spans through the Agent Run interface. [^2m5m2j]
- **Dashboards:** Create, clone, edit, and navigate dashboards with autosaving and improved performance. [^jmiet9]
- **Broad Pydantic integration:** Logfire is positioned alongside Pydantic AI and Pydantic Evals as part of Pydantic’s AI engineering stack. [^E1]

## History and Origin Story

Logfire originated within Pydantic’s broader AI engineering stack, which combines [[Tooling/AI-Toolkit/Agentic AI/Pydantic AI|Pydantic AI]], Pydantic Evals, [[concepts/Explainers for AI/LLM Gateways|AI Gateways]], and observability capabilities for teams building AI applications. [^E1] The product evolved toward AI-native observability by combining OpenTelemetry instrumentation with agent-specific views such as conversation replay, tool-call inspection, token tracking, cost analysis, and decision tracing. ## Competitive Landscape

### Who it’s for, who it’s not for

Logfire is aimed at teams building production AI applications or agents that need to inspect end-to-end behavior, including model requests, tool calls, conversations, token usage, costs, and routing decisions. [^t1j1xr] [^2m5m2j] Its OpenTelemetry foundation also suits teams that want AI-specific observability without abandoning standard telemetry practices. [^t1j1xr] [^wxlj6h]

It is less suited to teams seeking only basic application logging, a standalone log viewer, or an observability product with no need for AI-agent tracing. This anti-ICP is an inference from Logfire’s documented emphasis on AI-agent behavior and GenAI telemetry. [^t1j1xr]

### Viable Alternatives

- **[[Tooling/Data Utilities/DataDog|DataDog]]:** A broad observability platform that may suit organizations wanting logs, metrics, traces, and application monitoring in one established suite.
- **[[New Relic]]:** A general-purpose application-observability alternative for teams prioritizing traditional APM capabilities.
- **[[Tooling/Software Development/Developer Experience/DevOps/Grafana Labs|Grafana Labs]] stack:** A flexible option for teams preferring composable, self-managed dashboards and telemetry backends.
- **[[Honeycomb]]:** A trace- and event-oriented observability alternative for teams focused on high-cardinality debugging.
- **Arize Phoenix:** An AI-observability alternative focused on tracing and evaluation of LLM and agent applications.
