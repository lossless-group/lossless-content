## Value Proposition & Features

Capella is a Dublin-based workplace-analytics company founded in 2020 with 29 employees. [^E1] It provides integrated occupancy analytics, workplace bookings, health-and-safety compliance tools, and AI-driven insights for managing office space. [^E1]

- Unifies badge, booking, sensor, and calendar data into a consolidated workplace view.
- Measures occupancy and space utilization.
- Supports desk and workplace booking.
- Uses AI-driven insights to inform space planning.
- Helps organizations right-size office space.
- Supports hybrid-work planning.
- Provides health-and-safety compliance services.
- Offers enterprise-wide and security-first positioning, as reflected in the supplied product metadata.

## Competitive Landscape

### Who it's for, who it's not for

Capella is aimed at organizations managing office portfolios, hybrid work, desk booking, space utilization, and workplace compliance through combined operational data. [^E1]

It is not clearly positioned for small teams seeking only a standalone room-booking or desk-booking tool; the available description emphasizes integrated analytics, compliance, and enterprise-wide workplace management. [^E1]

### Viable Alternatives

- **Microsoft Places** — A workplace-management alternative for organizations already standardized on Microsoft 365.
- **Robin** — A workplace and desk-booking platform focused on hybrid-office coordination.
- **Workplace from Meta** — A collaboration-oriented option rather than a specialized occupancy-analytics system.
