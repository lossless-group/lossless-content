## Value Proposition & Features

Nexor AI is an AI-agent platform for B2B revenue teams that coordinates lead conversations, follow-ups, and actions across phone, WhatsApp, email, web chat, Slack, [[Vocabulary/CRM|CRMs]], and connected tools. Its agents use company-provided context and operational rules to contact leads and escalate cases to people when needed. [^g0kdb5] [^n0atug] [^u6r5gr] [^o92lrl] [^160tgr] [^fyp7l0] [[concepts/Market-Categories/Sales AI|Sales AI]] is the closest category label.

- **Goal-driven agents:** Configure tasks with goals, channels, rules, follow-ups, stages, variables, and tools. [^2ps9cm]
- **Multichannel outreach:** Agents can communicate through phone, WhatsApp, email, SMS, and web chat. [^g0kdb5] [^n0atug] [^u6r5gr] [^fyp7l0]
- **Inbound web chat:** A website widget lets visitors start conversations, captures selected lead fields, and supports an npm SDK for application-controlled behavior. [^u6r5gr]
- **Context and knowledge:** Agents can be configured with identity, role, company, language, instructions, knowledge, and files. [^2ps9cm]
- **AI management assistant:** Nexor AI can create agents, modify tasks and schedules, inspect conversations, review lead progress, and export conversations or calls to CSV. [^g0kdb5]
- **Human escalation:** Teams can receive alerts when a lead needs a person, a tool or webhook fails, or an integration stops working. [^o92lrl]
- **Slack workspace:** Teams can ask about leads and results or make permitted changes through NexorGPT in connected Slack channels. [^160tgr]
- **Integrations and extensibility:** Nexor supports CRM and calendar connections, apps such as Notion, Linear, Airtable, and Zapier, plus remote MCP servers. [^2ps9cm] [^9przoh]

## Competitive Landscape

### Who it's for, who it's not for

Nexor is aimed at B2B revenue teams that need automated, multichannel lead engagement connected to their CRM, calendars, communication channels, and internal workflows. [^2ps9cm] [^fyp7l0] It is particularly suited to teams that want configurable agents, human escalation, and Slack-based operational oversight. [^o92lrl] [^160tgr]

It is not an obvious fit for organizations seeking only a basic chatbot, a standalone email sequencer, or an unmanaged autonomous system with no human-escalation and integration requirements; Nexor’s documented product centers on configurable agents, tasks, channels, tools, and team controls. [^g0kdb5] [^u6r5gr] [^2ps9cm] [^o92lrl]

### Viable Alternatives

- **Salesforce Agentforce:** A broader CRM-native agent platform for organizations already standardized on Salesforce.
- **HubSpot AI:** A CRM-centered option for teams wanting sales automation inside HubSpot.
- **[[Tooling/Enterprise Jobs-to-be-Done/Outreach]]:** A sales-engagement platform oriented toward structured prospecting sequences and rep workflows.
- **[[Tooling/Enterprise Jobs-to-be-Done/Apollo|Apollo]]:** A prospecting and sales-engagement suite combining lead data with outreach automation.
- **Intercom Fin:** A customer-conversation agent focused primarily on support and inbound engagement rather than Nexor’s broader lead-outreach workflow.
