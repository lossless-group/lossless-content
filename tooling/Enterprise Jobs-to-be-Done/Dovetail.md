## Value Proposition & Features

Dovetail is a customer-intelligence platform that centralizes customer feedback and context, then turns unstructured data into evidence-backed insights and actions. Its positioning has expanded from a research repository into an organization-wide “customer context layer” ingesting data from customer and business tools.[^E1] [^sgfs43]

Priority features:

- **Sources:** Connects customer-feedback systems into a shared workspace source.[^qlf16k]
- **Projects:** Supports analysis of interviews, usability tests, sales calls, and surveys.[^xxhk0f]
- **Channels:** Continuously analyzes incoming feedback, groups related problems, and ranks actionable ideas using evidence such as frequency, severity, affected accounts, and ARR.[^pkcjm7] [^o0lrln]
- **Chat:** Lets users ask questions across workspace data and receive cited answers.[^xxhk0f]
- **Agents:** Automates monitoring, reporting, and alerting on customer data.[^xxhk0f]
- **Digital Twins:** Answers questions from the perspective of a customer segment using that segment’s feedback.[^xxhk0f]
- **Docs:** Generates and shares reports linked to underlying customer data.[^xxhk0f]
- **Integrations and MCP:** Connects systems such as Salesforce, Zendesk, Gong, Slack, Jira, and Figma, while MCP provides controlled access for AI assistants.[^ztaf11] [^ef08bl]

## Competitive Landscape

### Who it's for, who it's not for

Dovetail is aimed at research, product, design, customer-success, sales, and revenue teams that need to consolidate qualitative feedback, analyze it with AI, and distribute evidence-backed insights across an organization.[^xxhk0f] [^ef08bl] It is particularly suited to companies with dispersed feedback across systems such as support, CRM, survey, review, and conversation platforms.[^pkcjm7] [^ef08bl]

It is less suited to teams seeking only a lightweight survey tool, a standalone quantitative analytics product, or a dedicated roadmap-management system; Dovetail’s own product material positions it as an evidence layer that works alongside roadmap tools rather than replacing them.[^ef08bl]

### Viable Alternatives

- **[[Tooling/Software Development/Developer Experience/DevTools/ProductBoard|ProductBoard]]:** A roadmap and product-management platform that can consume customer insights; Dovetail is positioned as the evidence layer behind that workflow.[^ef08bl]
- **[[Qualtrics]]:** A broad experience-management and survey platform that can serve as an upstream feedback source.[^pkcjm7]
- **Salesforce:** A CRM and customer-data system that can supply customer context to Dovetail through integrations.[^ztaf11] [^ef08bl]
- **Zendesk:** A support platform whose tickets can feed Dovetail’s ongoing feedback analysis.[^ef08bl]
- **[[Tooling/Enterprise Jobs-to-be-Done/Gong|Gong]]:** A revenue-conversation platform that can provide sales-call context for analysis.[^xxhk0f] [^ef08bl]
