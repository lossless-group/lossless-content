Ora App is an events services platform headquartered in Austin, Texas, with a reported workforce of one. [^E1] It provides event discovery and ticketing for nightlife, wellness, salsa, food, and other shared experiences across multiple cities. [^E1]

# Value Proposition & Features

Ora positions itself as an event ticketing and marketing platform: attendees discover experiences and purchase tickets, while organizers sell tickets and use Ora’s promotional reach. [^c74o69]

Core features include event discovery by city, category, neighborhood, and occasion; ticket checkout; digital tickets stored under “My Tickets”; and QR-code entry without printing. [^c74o69]

Priority features:

- Event discovery across nightlife, wellness, salsa, food, parties, classes, and community events. [^E1] [^c74o69]
- City- and neighborhood-specific event listings. [^c74o69]
- Online ticket purchasing with multiple ticket types. [^c74o69]
- Digital QR-code tickets for venue entry. [^c74o69]
- Organizer event listings and ticket sales. [^c74o69]
- Event marketing and advertising for organizers. [^c74o69]
- Mobile access through the App Store and Google Play. [^c74o69]

# Competitive Landscape

## Who it's for, who it's not for

Ora is aimed at consumers seeking local experiences—especially nightlife, wellness, dance, food, parties, classes, and community events—and at organizers that need ticket sales and event promotion. [^E1] [^c74o69]
