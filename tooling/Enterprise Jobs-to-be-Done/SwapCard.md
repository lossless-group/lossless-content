## Value Proposition & Features

Swapcard is a Paris-based event-management platform for in-person, virtual, and hybrid events, with particular emphasis on exhibitor ROI, attendee networking, and business meetings. [^21f7uh] [^dzb0tl] Its core audience includes trade shows, conferences, associations, and communities. [^21f7uh]

- **AI matchmaking:** Recommends relevant attendees, exhibitors, sponsors, sessions, and meetings. [^dzb0tl] [^yoci1a]
- **Registration and ticketing:** Supports event registration, ticketing, conditional logic, and promotional codes. [^21f7uh]
- **Attendee engagement:** Provides a mobile event app, networking, chat, meeting booking, and virtual-session access. [^dzb0tl] [^yoci1a]
- **Exhibitor lead capture:** Enables badge scanning and lead retrieval for exhibitors. [^21f7uh] [^dzb0tl]
- **Organizer operations:** Includes event websites, onsite check-in, and badge printing. [^dzb0tl] [^yoci1a]
- **Analytics:** Measures engagement, meetings, connections, and exhibitor outcomes. [^dzb0tl] [^xz5oma]
- **Hybrid-event support:** Combines onsite and online participation in one event experience. [^21f7uh] [^eh9wl1]

# Competitive Landscape

## Who it's for, who it's not for

Swapcard is best suited to trade shows, exhibitions, associations, and B2B conferences where exhibitor leads, qualified meetings, sponsor visibility, and networking outcomes are central success measures. [^dzb0tl] [^yoci1a] It is also positioned for mid-market and enterprise organizers running onsite, virtual, or hybrid events. [^21f7uh] [^yoci1a]

It is less suitable for simple webinar-style events or organizers primarily seeking lightweight session delivery, because its strongest differentiation is matchmaking, exhibitor engagement, and lead generation rather than basic broadcasting. [^dzb0tl] [^eh9wl1]

## Viable Alternatives

- **PheedLoop:** A modular event platform with registration, exhibitor tools, floor-plan capabilities, and onsite check-in. [^f2in3p]
- **Eventify:** Offers registration, interactive maps, lead scanning, and an event app, with annual plans. [^f2in3p]
- **Map Your Show:** A specialist alternative for trade-show floor plans, booth sales, and exhibitor portals. [^f2in3p]
- **VenueSight:** Combines registration, booth sales, floor plans, check-in, badge printing, and lead retrieval. [^f2in3p]
- **Cvent:** A broader event-management alternative for registration, event operations, and enterprise programs; the retrieved results identify Swapcard primarily as an alternative to larger event platforms. [^yoci1a]
