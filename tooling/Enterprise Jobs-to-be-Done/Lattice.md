Lattice is an HR and people-management software company founded in 2015, headquartered in San Francisco, and focused on managing performance, goals, engagement, compensation, growth, and related people processes through one platform. [^E1]

## Value Proposition & Features

Lattice positions itself as an integrated people platform connecting employee data, management workflows, and AI-powered assistance across the employee lifecycle. [^E1]

- **Performance management:** Reviews and performance processes are connected to goals, feedback, updates, and analytics. [^57urkm]
- **Goals and OKRs:** Teams can create, update, assign, and track goals, including through supported AI-tool integrations. [^51tsbh]
- **One-on-ones and meetings:** Lattice supports agendas, notes, action items, recaps, and meeting context linked to employee records. [^ekpi46]
- **AI assistance:** The AI Agent can draft reviews, coach managers, summarize meetings, analyze people data, and surface patterns or risks. [^yoblv2]
- **Feedback and engagement:** Feedback, employee surveys, engagement analysis, and trend reporting are part of the platform’s people-management workflow. [^57urkm] [^8bwqm5]
- **Growth and development:** AI can recommend growth areas for individual development plans using recent feedback and review data. [^byx058]
- **Integrations:** Lattice highlights connections with workplace systems, including Workday, Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Claude, and ChatGPT. [^yoblv2] [^51tsbh] [^ekpi46]
- **Governance and controls:** Administrators can centrally enable or disable AI features and control whether newly released AI capabilities activate automatically. [^51tsbh]

# History and Origin Story

Lattice was founded in 2015 as a people-management platform focused on helping organizations manage goals, performance, and employee development. [^E1] Its disclosed financing progressed from seed funding in 2016 through Series B, C, D, E, and F rounds, with Tiger Global leading several later rounds. [^F7] [^F5] [^F4] [^F3] [^F2] [^F1] Jack Altman later moved from CEO to executive chairman while Sarah Franklin was announced as CEO, although the available search result is internally dated inconsistently and should not be treated as a current leadership record. # Competitive Landscape

## Who it's for, who it's not for

Lattice is aimed at organizations seeking an integrated system for performance management, goals, feedback, engagement, growth, and AI-assisted people operations. [^E1] It is particularly suited to companies with structured management processes and enough organizational complexity to benefit from connected employee data and workflow controls. [^yoblv2]

It is less suitable for very small teams seeking a lightweight standalone review tool, organizations requiring only payroll or core HR administration, or buyers unwilling to adopt structured performance and people-data processes.

## Viable Alternatives

- **[[concepts/Explainers for Tooling/Complex Coordination|Complex Coordination]]** — Lattice is relevant where organizational alignment requires shared goals, feedback, and management processes.
- **[[Tooling/Enterprise Jobs-to-be-Done/15Five|15Five]]** — An adjacent people-management platform focused on performance, engagement, and manager workflows.
- **[[Workday]]** — A broader enterprise HCM alternative, with Lattice positioned as a more specialized people and performance layer.
- **Culture Amp** — An adjacent employee engagement, performance, and people-analytics platform.
- **[[Tooling/Enterprise Jobs-to-be-Done/Zeck|Zeck]]** — Lattice can overlap with leadership communication workflows, but it is not primarily a board-update tool, unlike [[Tooling/Enterprise Jobs-to-be-Done/Zeck|Zeck]].
