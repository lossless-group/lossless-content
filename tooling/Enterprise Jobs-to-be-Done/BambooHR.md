## Value Proposition & Features

BambooHR is a cloud HR software platform that organizes employee information for hiring, onboarding, time-off tracking, and workplace culture. [^o29gdv] Its broader HR SaaS offering covers the employee lifecycle, including payroll, time, benefits, performance, compensation, reporting, and employee experience. [^E1]

- Employee records and HR administration [^o29gdv]
- Hiring and onboarding [^o29gdv]
- Time-off and time tracking [^o29gdv]
- Payroll [^E1]
- Benefits administration [^E1]
- Performance management [^E1]
- Compensation management [^E1]
- Reporting and employee experience [^E1]

BambooHR’s architecture is delivered as SaaS and can connect with external applications through integrations; one documented integration synchronizes employee records, approved time off, policies, and balances through the BambooHR API. [^qlbdk7]

## Who it's for, who it's not for

BambooHR is aimed at small and medium-sized businesses seeking an integrated HR system for employee records, hiring, onboarding, payroll, time, benefits, performance, compensation, and reporting. [^E1]
