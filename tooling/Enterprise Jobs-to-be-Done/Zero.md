## Value Proposition & Features

Zero is an AI CRM and end-to-end revenue platform for scaleups, positioning itself as a replacement for traditional CRM systems and their bolt-on sales tools. It brings teams, customer data, and AI agents together across the customer journey to help companies close more deals with fewer sales hires. [^E1]

- Pipeline management
- Lead search
- Contact and company enrichment
- Warm introductions
- Automated sales sequences
- Revenue reporting
- AI-assisted go-to-market workflows [^E1]

# Competitive Landscape

## Who it's for, who it's not for

Zero is aimed at [[Vocabulary/Scale‑Up|Scale Ups]] that want one system for pipeline management, prospect discovery, enrichment, introductions, sales automation, and reporting. [^E1]

It is less clearly suited to very small teams seeking a lightweight standalone CRM, or to large enterprises that require publicly documented governance, procurement, security, and integration specifications.
