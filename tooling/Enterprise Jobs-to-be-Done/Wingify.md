## Value Proposition & Features

Wingify is an Agentic Experience Optimization Platform that combines experimentation, personalization, customer insights, commerce optimization, feature management, and engagement on a shared data foundation. [^hom5j0] It is positioned as a [[concepts/Explainers for Tooling/Digital Experience Platforms|Digital Experience Platform]] for turning behavioral data into decisions, live experiences, measurement, and continuous optimization. [^hom5j0]

- **Analytics:** [[Heatmaps]], session recordings, journey analytics, form analytics, and user feedback help teams identify friction and generate hypotheses. [^6rwqxk]
- **Experimentation:** Supports [[Vocabulary/A-B Testing|A/B Testing]], multivariate, and split-URL testing through visual and code editors, with targeting, segmentation, reporting, and SmartStats statistical analysis. [^6rwqxk] [^ul9sf2]
- **Feature management:** Feature flags, progressive delivery, controlled rollouts, monitoring, rollback, and mobile-app experimentation support the full feature lifecycle. [^n5s4io] [^lk4l6d]
- **Personalization:** Behavioral, contextual, customer, and predictive signals are used to create targeted experiences. [^885u8o] [^ul9sf2]
- **AI and intelligence:** Wingz connects behavioral, statistical, historical, release, experiment, and metric data to identify opportunities and recommend next actions. [^lk4l6d] [^885u8o]
- **Audience activation:** Data Studio, Predictive Data, Emotions, segmentation, and targeting support audience creation and relevant experiences. [^n5s4io] [^ul9sf2]
- **Cross-channel optimization:** The platform is described as activating experiences across web, apps, search, merchandising, email, SMS, and push. [^hom5j0]
