## Value Proposition & Features

Platoon’s value proposition is **[[Expert-Interview Automation]] for professional services**: it replaces a manual workflow that spans sourcing, interviewing, and synthesis with a single platform that produces structured intelligence. [^E1] The site frames the product as “the platform that automates expert sourcing and turns interviews into structured intelligence.” [^E1]

Core product details published on the site are limited, but the positioning implies three primary functions: expert sourcing, interview capture, and conversion of interview content into usable research outputs. [^E1] The current public materials do not disclose a detailed feature list, technical architecture, or named integrations. [^E1]

## Competitive Landscape

### Who it’s for, who it’s not for

Platoon is aimed at **professional services firms** that do research-heavy work and rely on expert interviews to build client-ready intelligence. [^E1] It is not positioned, based on the public site, as a general-purpose transcription tool or a broad consumer note-taking app. [^E1]

It is also not clearly aimed at teams that need a fully open-ended research platform with public pricing, extensive enterprise documentation, or a disclosed API-first integration stack. [^E1]

### Viable Alternatives

- **AlphaSense** — broader enterprise search and market-intelligence platform; a plausible substitute for teams that need discovery plus synthesis rather than interview-first workflows.
- **GLG** — expert-network provider; a substitute when the primary need is sourcing experts rather than software-driven interview synthesis.
- **Guidepoint** — expert-call marketplace; similar to GLG for expert sourcing with less emphasis on Platoon-style workflow automation.
- **Dovetail** — qualitative research repository and synthesis tool; relevant if the core problem is organizing interview findings rather than sourcing experts.
- **Otter.ai** — general transcription and meeting notes; a looser alternative for teams that only need capture, not structured intelligence.
