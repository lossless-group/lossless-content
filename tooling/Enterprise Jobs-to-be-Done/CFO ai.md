## Value Proposition & Features

CFO.ai is an AI finance coworker centered on **Ari**, which answers business questions by building a financial model from company actuals and assumptions. Users can inspect every number, modify assumptions, and test decisions such as hiring, pricing changes, or spending cuts. [^0suhh4]

- **Live financial model:** Represents variables, dimensions, formulas, pages, actuals, forecasts, and scenarios in an editable model. [^7ng8x2]
- **Accounting [[Vocabulary/API Integrations|integrations]]:** Connects QuickBooks, Xero, NetSuite, and Rillet to import actuals. [^0suhh4]
- **Spreadsheet migration:** Rebuilds an existing workbook as a live model and checks that it ties out. [^0suhh4]
- **[[Forecasting]]\:** Models revenue, expenses, cash runway, headcount, budgets, and financial statements. [^pe302t]
- **Scenario planning:** Tests decisions without changing the approved plan and preserves alternative versions. [^0suhh4] [^pe302t]
- **Reporting and sharing:** Produces reports and read-only links for investors, boards, and other stakeholders. [^0suhh4]
- **Automation:** Skills and Triggers let Ari repeat workflows on a schedule. [^7ng8x2] [^pe302t]
- **Agent connectivity:** An [[concepts/Explainers for AI/MCP Servers|MCP server]] lets external AI clients such as Claude Code and Codex read and modify the live model. [^0suhh4] [^4wq4vk]

# Competitive Landscape

## Who it's for, who it's not for

CFO.ai is designed for finance teams, founders, fractional CFOs, and operators who need connected actuals, forecasts, scenario analysis, reporting, and recurring finance workflows. [^0suhh4] [^pe302t] [^hw229w]

It is less suitable for organizations seeking only bookkeeping, a static spreadsheet, or a detached chatbot: its workflow depends on a structured live model, connected data, and user review of assumptions and changes. [^0suhh4] [^4wq4vk]

## Viable Alternatives

- **Spreadsheet-based FP&A:** Flexible for teams that want manual control but lacks CFO.ai’s integrated AI workflow and model automation.
- **Traditional FP&A platforms:** Suitable for larger finance organizations needing established planning, reporting, and governance workflows.
- **Accounting-system reporting:** Useful for historical reporting but generally narrower than CFO.ai’s forecasting and scenario capabilities.
- **General-purpose AI assistants:** Useful for questions and drafting, but CFO.ai connects answers to a persistent financial model and auditable changes. [^0suhh4] [^4wq4vk]
