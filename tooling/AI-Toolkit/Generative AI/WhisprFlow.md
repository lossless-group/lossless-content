### Wispr Flow — 
https://wisprflow.ai/ [^E1]

WhisprFlow—branded on its official site as **Wispr Flow**—is a [[concepts/Explainers for AI/Speech-to-Text|Voice-to-Text]] AI service that turns speech into polished writing across applications. [^E1]

# Value Proposition & Features

Wispr Flow is designed for people who want to write by speaking rather than typing, with automatic punctuation, capitalization, paragraphing, and removal of filler words. [^ez49bn] It supports writing across apps and devices, including Mac, Windows, and mobile platforms. [^ez49bn]

Core features include multilingual transcription, a personal dictionary for names and jargon, voice-based editing, and cloud transcription. [^ez49bn] The service requires an internet connection because dictated audio is processed in the cloud on every platform. [^ez49bn]

- **Polished voice dictation** with formatting and filler-word removal. [^ez49bn]
- **Cross-application input** that works in ordinary text fields. [^E1]
- **100+ languages**, including regional variants, with automatic language detection. [^ez49bn]
- **Personal dictionary** for names, products, and specialized terminology. [^ez49bn]
- **Voice editing** of selected text on Mac and Windows. [^rev4li]
- **Cross-device synchronization** for dictionaries and settings. [^ez49bn]
- **Cloud processing** for transcription. [^ez49bn]
- **Wispr Notetaker**, which records meetings locally rather than joining calls as a bot. [^v1aa8m]

# Competitive Landscape

## Who it's for, who it's not for

Wispr Flow is aimed at professionals, developers, multilingual users, and other people who produce substantial written communication and want to dictate email, documents, messages, prompts, or code across multiple devices. [^ez49bn]

It is less suitable for users who require offline transcription, because Flow processes dictation in the cloud and requires a connection. [^ez49bn] It is also not a strict end-to-end-encrypted or on-premise service, according to Wispr’s security documentation. [^wcf9en]

## Viable Alternatives

- **Apple Dictation:** Built into Apple devices, but Wispr’s comparison positions Flow as offering more formatting, terminology personalization, and broader cross-platform support. [^ez49bn]
- **[[Gboard]] Voice Typing:** A mobile voice-input alternative; Wispr compares itself on language coverage, editing, and synchronized dictionaries across laptop and phone workflows. [^rev4li]
- **[[Tooling/AI-Toolkit/Fireflies.ai|Fireflies.ai]]:** A meeting-intelligence alternative, whereas Wispr Flow combines dictation with a Notetaker that records meetings locally without joining as a bot. [^jm8paa] [^tkecy3]
- **Microsoft Teams transcription:** Useful for Teams-native meetings, while Wispr Notetaker is described as working across Zoom, Meet, Teams, and Slack huddles. [^tkecy3]
