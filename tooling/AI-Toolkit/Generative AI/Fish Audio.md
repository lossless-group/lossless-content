# Value Proposition & Features
Fish Audio is an AI voice platform for **text-to-speech, [[concepts/Explainers for AI/Voice Cloners|Voice Cloning]], speech-to-text, and audio translation** with strong emotion and effect control. [^fo2vlr] [^p95q0f] Its core pitch is high-quality, human-sounding speech for creators, developers, and enterprises, with self-hosting options for teams that want control over data and infrastructure. [^j3o1x8] [^p95q0f]

- **Text-to-speech**: Generates natural-sounding speech from written text. [^p5hqwl] [^p95q0f]
- **Voice cloning**: Clones a voice from about 15 seconds of audio. [^fo2vlr] [^rw1n8c]
- **Emotion control**: Supports fine-grained control over emotion and effects, including inline tag control. [^fo2vlr] [^g8jkg7]
- **Large voice library**: Offers **2,000,000+ voices** across **80+ languages**. [^g8jkg7] [^p5hqwl]
- **API access**: Provides REST, WebSocket streaming, and SDK access for developers. [^p95q0f]
- **Self-hosting**: Open models can be self-hosted in VPC or air-gapped environments. [^p95q0f] [^j3o1x8]
- **Speech-to-text**: Includes STT as part of the product suite. [^3z70ue] [^p95q0f]
- **Audio translation**: Listed as part of the platform capabilities. [^fo2vlr] [^p95q0f]

# History and Origin Story
Fish Audio grew out of **Fish Speech**, an open-source voice project that began with co-founder and chief scientist **Shijia Liao** training models on a single gaming GPU. [^j3o1x8] [^tau7my] The company says it was founded in **2025**, is headquartered in **Palo Alto**, and was built to make voice AI sound more human and controllable. [^j3o1x8]

# Competitive Landscape

## Who it's for, who it's not for
Fish Audio is for creators, developers, and enterprises that need **voice cloning, expressive TTS, API access, and self-hosting**. [^j3o1x8] [^p95q0f] It is not for buyers who only want a simple consumer narration tool with no technical integration needs or data-control requirements. [^p95q0f] [^j3o1x8]

## Viable Alternatives
- **ElevenLabs** — a leading voice generation platform often used as the closest commercial comparator for quality and pricing. [^27ghq1] [^rw1n8c]
- **PlayHT** — a TTS and voice-cloning service aimed at similar creator and developer use cases.
- **[[Resemble AI]]** — focuses on voice cloning and enterprise speech workflows.
- **Azure AI Speech** — a broader enterprise speech platform with TTS and speech recognition.
- **Google Cloud Text-to-Speech** — a mainstream cloud TTS alternative for developer integration.
