Aligned Data Centers, founded in 2013 and headquartered in Plano, Texas, develops and operates sustainable, adaptive data-center infrastructure for hyperscale campuses and turnkey data halls. Its stated focus is performance, resilience, energy efficiency, and global reach. [^E1]

## Value Proposition & Features

Aligned serves hyperscale and demanding-compute customers with scalable data-center capacity designed to combine rapid deployment, operational resilience, and energy efficiency. [^E1]

Core features include:

- **Adaptive infrastructure:** Modular, standardized systems intended to support changing customer requirements and phased expansion. [14]
- **Hyperscale campuses:** Large-scale campuses designed for high-density digital infrastructure and advanced compute workloads. [1]
- **Turnkey data halls:** Ready-to-deploy environments for customers requiring dedicated capacity. [^E1]
- **Liquid-cooling support:** Aligned’s ODATA subsidiary is deploying hybrid air- and liquid-cooling architectures through DeltaFlow at facilities in Brazil and Mexico. [6]
- **Water stewardship:** The Shippingport campus uses closed-loop cooling designed to protect local water resources. [11]
- **Ultra-efficient operations:** The company positions its infrastructure around energy efficiency, resilience, and performance. [^E1]
- **Sustainability certification:** Aligned says its new facilities are covered by an initiative seeking Green Globes certification. [14]
- **Global reach:** The company operates across the Americas through its data-center portfolio and ODATA platform. [15]

Aligned’s campus-scale infrastructure pairs naturally with [[content-areas/AI-Factories-Datacenters/Concepts/Datacenter Operations|Datacenter Operations]] for monitoring, reliability, and downstream reporting.

# History and Origin Story

Aligned Data Centers was founded in 2013 and is headquartered in Plano, Texas. [^E1] The company developed around adaptive, energy-efficient infrastructure for hyperscale campuses and turnkey data halls, later expanding its Americas footprint through ODATA. [^E1] A major recent inflection point is Project Phoenix, which repurposes a legacy industrial site in Shippingport, Pennsylvania, for a planned 2 GW campus. [3]

## Competitive Landscape

## Who it's for, who it's not for

Aligned is aimed at hyperscalers, large enterprises, AI-compute operators, and other customers needing high-density, resilient, scalable data-center capacity. Its offering is particularly relevant to customers requiring dedicated campuses, turnkey halls, advanced cooling, and phased expansion. [^E1] [1]

It is not positioned as a retail cloud application, small-business hosting provider, or conventional shared-server vendor; the public materials focus on campus-scale and enterprise infrastructure rather than self-service compute plans. [^E1]

## Viable Alternatives

- **Digital Realty:** A large global data-center operator offering colocation and interconnection for enterprise and hyperscale customers.
- **Equinix:** A global colocation and interconnection provider suited to customers prioritizing network ecosystems and carrier access.
- **QTS Data Centers:** A hyperscale and enterprise data-center developer and operator with large campuses in North America.
- **CyrusOne:** A large-scale data-center operator focused on hyperscale and enterprise capacity.
- **Vantage Data Centers:** A hyperscale-focused developer and operator competing on large campuses and high-capacity infrastructure.
