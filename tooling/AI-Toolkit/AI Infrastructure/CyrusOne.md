## Value Proposition & Features

CyrusOne’s value proposition is to provide scalable data-center capacity and connectivity for hyperscale, enterprise, and AI workloads. [^E1] The company combines custom build-to-suit deployments with colocation and interconnection services. [^E1]

Core features:

- **Build-to-suit data centers:** Custom facilities designed for specific customer capacity, power, and deployment requirements. [^E1]
- **Colocation:** Space, power, and data-center infrastructure for enterprise and hyperscale workloads. [^E1]
- **Interconnection:** Connectivity services linking customers, networks, cloud platforms, and other infrastructure. [^E1]
- **High-density AI infrastructure:** Capacity intended for demanding AI and other high-density applications. [^E1]
- **Digital-infrastructure modernization:** Facilities and services supporting migration and modernization programs. [^E1]
- **Global data-center locations:** Infrastructure delivered across a geographically distributed portfolio. [^E1]
- **Hyperscale support:** Large-scale deployments for cloud and internet companies. [^E1]
- **Enterprise workloads:** Infrastructure serving corporate and institutional computing requirements. [^E1]

# History and Origin Story

CyrusOne was founded in 2000 and is headquartered at 2850 N Harwood Street, Suite 2200, Dallas, Texas. [^E1] The company was acquired by Kohlberg Kravis Roberts & Co. in a transaction valued at approximately $15.0 billion on November 15, 2021. [^E1] Its operating focus is data-center and digital infrastructure for hyperscale and enterprise customers. [^E1]
