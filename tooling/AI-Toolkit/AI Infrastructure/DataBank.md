## Value Proposition & Features

DataBank provides enterprise colocation, managed cloud, interconnection, and network infrastructure designed for secure, compliant, scalable workloads. Its positioning emphasizes edge locations, cloud connectivity, and infrastructure suitable for high-performance and AI-era applications. [^E1] [^gautk6]

Core architecture combines data centers with carrier connectivity, cloud on-ramps, private networking, and controlled physical environments. DataBank describes colocation as an anchor for hybrid architectures because it connects private infrastructure, branch networks, partner systems, and multiple clouds. [^46v1jn] [^hy72q5]

- **Colocation:** Secure facilities for enterprise, technology-provider, and content-provider workloads. [^91atky] [^gautk6]
- **Interconnection:** Meet-me rooms, multiple carriers, redundant fiber entrances, and connectivity to cloud providers. [^wg2pyp] [^gautk6]
- **Managed cloud:** [[Managed Cloud Services]] integrated with colocation and network infrastructure. [^wg2pyp]
- **Network services:** Internet access, SD-WAN, private connectivity, and cloud on-ramps. [^hy72q5] [^5fyk0p]
- **High-performance infrastructure:** More than 70 HPC-ready facilities positioned for dense and AI-oriented workloads. [^gautk6]
- **Security and compliance:** Physical controls include staffed facilities, mantraps, CCTV, and dual-factor authentication. [^wg2pyp]
- **Geographic reach:** A U.S. footprint spanning more than 25 markets and 20 interconnection hubs. [^gautk6]

# History and Origin Story

DataBank was founded in 2005 and originated in the former Federal Reserve Bank building in downtown Dallas, according to a company feature recounting co-founder Jerry Blair’s historical account. [^E1] [^iu39x1] The company developed from an early Dallas data-center operation into a provider of enterprise colocation, interconnection, managed cloud, and network services across a national footprint. [^iu39x1]

# Competitive Landscape

## Who it's for, who it's not for

DataBank is aimed at enterprises, technology companies, content providers, and organizations needing secure colocation, hybrid-cloud connectivity, interconnection, and scalable high-density infrastructure. [^91atky] [^gautk6]

It is not a natural fit for small customers seeking a simple self-service cloud account, consumer hosting, or fully standardized low-cost infrastructure without facility, network, or compliance requirements.

## Viable Alternatives

- **[[content-areas/AI-Factories-Datacenters/Organizations/Digital Realty]]:** Large digital-infrastructure and data-center operator serving enterprise and cloud customers.
