# Value Proposition & Features

NLyte provides a **Data Center Infrastructure Management (DCIM)** platform that acts as a centralized system of record for assets, power, cooling, space, connectivity, workflows, and capacity planning across data-center environments. [^vjwh73] Its stated value is to create a unified operational view that can provide AI systems with reliable infrastructure data for recommendations and insights. [^vjwh73]

- **Infrastructure system of record:** Centralizes operational data covering assets, power, cooling, space, connectivity, workflows, and capacity planning. [^vjwh73]
- **Capacity planning:** Supports analysis of available power, cooling, and space, including evaluation of deployment scenarios. [^vjwh73]
- **Asset and device visibility:** Helps organizations maintain inventory information for infrastructure deployed across data centers, colocation facilities, and edge locations. [^5g8zmq]
- **Connectivity and dependency information:** Provides infrastructure and network-related operational context for AI-assisted analysis. [^vjwh73]
- **Workflow support:** Can support operational workflows and integrations, including ticketing and provisioning-related processes. [^vjwh73]
- **AI and MCP readiness:** NLyte describes its platform as a trusted data foundation for connecting large language models to live infrastructure data through Model Context Protocol. [^vjwh73]
- **Device governance:** NLyte Device Management is described as a vendor-agnostic platform for certificate lifecycle management, firmware governance, and security monitoring across multi-vendor infrastructure. [^5g8zmq]
- **Managed services:** NLyte offers services covering platform administration, data configuration, integrations, reporting, performance optimization, and training. [^4x73zs]

# Competitive Landscape

## Who it's for, who it's not for

NLyte is intended for organizations operating data centers, colocation facilities, edge locations, and other complex infrastructure environments that need centralized visibility into assets, capacity, power, cooling, connectivity, and operational workflows. [^vjwh73] [^5g8zmq]

It is not evidently aimed at consumers, small businesses seeking basic IT asset tracking, or organizations without physical infrastructure-management requirements; the available sources describe enterprise infrastructure operations rather than general-purpose IT management. [^vjwh73] [^5g8zmq]
