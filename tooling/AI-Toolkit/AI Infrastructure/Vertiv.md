## Value Proposition & Features

Vertiv supplies integrated power, cooling, IT-infrastructure, monitoring, and lifecycle-service solutions intended to keep mission-critical digital facilities available from the cloud to the network edge. [^3vaayy] Its portfolio is aimed at rising power density and thermal-management requirements associated with AI and high-performance computing. [^cyrt3n]

Core features, in priority order:

- **Power infrastructure:** UPS systems, power distribution, switchgear, busways, and related critical-power equipment. [^3k4qms]
- **[[content-areas/AI-Factories-Datacenters/Concepts/Thermal Management|Thermal management]]:** Perimeter, in-row, liquid-cooling, and coolant-distribution systems for conventional and high-density workloads. [^zcp4bt]
- **[[content-areas/AI-Factories-Datacenters/Concepts/AI Factories|AI-Factory]] infrastructure:** Vertiv OneCore is described as a prefabricated design spanning grid-level power delivery through chip-level thermal management. [^k97wks]
- **Modular and prefabricated systems:** Integrated solutions such as SmartCabinet target deployments requiring packaged power, cooling, and IT infrastructure. [^zcp4bt]
- **[[High-Density Racks]] infrastructure:** Vertiv’s rack portfolio addresses deeper, heavier AI and HPC equipment and integrates airflow and cable-management capabilities. [^i8aq5b]
- **Monitoring and controls:** Vertiv provides infrastructure-management software and controls alongside physical power and cooling systems. [^3k4qms]
- **Engineering and lifecycle services:** The company’s offering includes commissioning, maintenance, fluid management, and other services for critical facilities. [^3vaayy]

Vertiv’s portfolio fits naturally into the broader **Datacenter Operations Systems** landscape, where infrastructure monitoring and facility-side operational depth are key requirements: [[lost-in-public/market-maps/Datacenter Operations Systems|Datacenter Operations Systems]].

[^3vaayy] Its headquarters are listed at 505 N. Cleveland Ave., Westerville, Ohio, in the Columbus area. [^E1]

## Competitive Landscape

Vertiv is for hyperscale, colocation, enterprise, telecommunications, and other organizations operating facilities where uninterrupted power, thermal control, high-density computing, and lifecycle support are critical. [^3vaayy] It is not primarily for small offices or ordinary IT environments that do not require specialized power, cooling, modular infrastructure, or facility-level services.
