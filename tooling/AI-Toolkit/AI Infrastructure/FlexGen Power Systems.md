# FlexGen Power Systems

## Value Proposition & Features

FlexGen provides software and services for deploying, managing, and optimizing battery energy-storage systems (BESS). Its HybridOS® energy-management software is designed to integrate with battery manufacturers and support diverse power-market strategies. [1]

Core features include compatibility with more than 80 battery and power-conversion-system configurations, modular software configurations for site- and regulatory-specific requirements, and analytics with AI-driven insights. [1] FlexGen also offers One-Touch Commissioning®, which uses preconfigured and lab-tested systems, remote operational support, and field commissioning to accelerate deployment. [3]

- HybridOS® energy-management system (EMS) [1]
- Hardware-agnostic integration with battery OEMs and power-conversion systems [1]
- Advanced analytics and AI-driven operational insights [1]
- Support for diverse power-market strategies [1]
- Modular and scalable software configurations [1]
- One-Touch Commissioning® [3]
- Remote Operations Center support [3]
- Project delivery and lifecycle services [2][3]

## Product Roadmap / Announcements

As of October 4, 2026,

- **September 17, 2026:** FlexGen announced a commercial and operational leadership realignment, appointing Diane Giacomozzi as chief commercial officer and Hugh Scott as chief operating officer. [2]
- **September 16, 2026:** FlexGen announced expanded collaboration with Lightshift Energy across 16 BESS projects in Massachusetts, Virginia, and Vermont; six projects were complete and ten were under construction. [1]
- **September 9, 2026:** FlexGen announced that SMT Houston IV, a 160-MW battery-storage facility, had completed commissioning and energization after a six-week delivery process. [3]

## Recent Developments

FlexGen and Lightshift Energy reported six completed projects totaling more than 25 MW/85 MWh, with ten additional projects under construction expected to add more than 55 MW/200 MWh. [1] FlexGen also reported that its software would serve as the EMS for the additional projects and that HybridOS® supports more than 80 battery and power-conversion-system configurations. [1]

FlexGen, SMT Energy, and Irby Construction reported commissioning SMT Houston IV in six weeks, described as three times faster than the original timeline. [3] The project brought the SMT–FlexGen portfolio supporting ERCOT to more than 300 MW across over a dozen projects. [3]

## Competitive Landscape

### Who it's for, who it's not for

FlexGen is aimed at utility-scale battery-storage owners, developers, operators, and project partners that need software, commissioning, project delivery, or lifecycle support across heterogeneous battery and power-conversion hardware. [1][2][3]

It is not positioned in the retrieved results as a consumer product, residential battery-management application, or general-purpose software platform unrelated to energy storage.

### Viable Alternatives

- **Tesla Megapack and Autobidder:** A vertically integrated battery-storage and energy-trading alternative for customers seeking Tesla hardware and software.
- **Fluence:** A utility-scale energy-storage and optimization provider competing across storage systems, controls, and services.
