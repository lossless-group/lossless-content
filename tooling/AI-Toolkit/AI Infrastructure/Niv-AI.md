[Niv-AI](https://il.linkedin.com/company/niv-ai) is a Tel Aviv-based startup that builds software to manage power spikes and optimize electricity use in AI data centers. [^bydj2h] [^v11e6j] 
## Company Overview

* Founded: May 2025 by CEO Tomer Timor and CTO Edward Kizis.
* Funding: Raised $12 million in a seed round in March 2026.
* Investors: Led by Glilot Capital Partners, with [[Grove Ventures]], [[Arc VC]], [[Encoded VC]], [[Leap Forward Ventures]], and [[Aurora Capital Partners]]. [^v11e6j] 

## Core Technology

* The Problem: High-intensity GPU workloads create sharp, millisecond-level power spikes. Data centers waste up to 30% of their power capacity maintaining safety buffers against these surges. [^v11e6j] 
* The Solution: Niv-AI uses high-frequency sensors to capture the "electrical fingerprint" of AI workloads. [^es8o6t] 
* The Result: Its AI models predict power shifts and balance workloads in real time. This unlocks trapped electrical capacity without adding bulky physical hardware or slowing down GPUs. [^es8o6t] 

## Value Proposition & Features

Niv-AI is an Israeli **AI power-orchestration** company that analyzes AI-workload fingerprints and dynamically schedules compute to synchronize demand with the electrical grid, reducing instantaneous power spikes. [^E1] Its stated goal is to help data centers unlock stranded power capacity without adding hardware while increasing tokens per watt for AI training and inference. [^E1]

Core capabilities include:

- **AI workload fingerprinting:** Identifies the power-demand characteristics of AI workloads. [^E1]
- **[[Dynamic Workload Orchestration]]:** Adjusts compute scheduling in response to workload and grid conditions. [^E1]
- **Power-spike reduction:** Coordinates demand to eliminate instantaneous electrical-load spikes. [^E1]
- **Stranded-capacity utilization:** Enables data centers to use available power capacity that would otherwise remain inaccessible because of peak-demand constraints. [^E1]
- **Hardware-free capacity expansion:** Pursues greater usable capacity without requiring additional servers or electrical hardware. [^E1]
- **Training and inference optimization:** Targets higher tokens-per-watt efficiency across both AI training and inference. [^E1]
- **Data-center [[content-areas/AI-Factories-Datacenters/Issues/Grid Synchronization]]:** Connects AI-compute operations with electrical-grid availability. [^E1]

The company’s positioning fits the emerging **AI-factory and data-center operations** market, alongside organizations pursuing hyperscale and neocloud infrastructure strategies. [[lost-in-public/market-maps/Datacenter Operations Systems|Datacenter Operations Systems]] [^E1]

## Who it's for, who it's not for

Niv-AI is aimed at AI data-center operators, hyperscalers, neocloud providers, and other organizations whose training or inference workloads are constrained by electrical-capacity peaks.
