## Value Proposition & Features

Pocket.ai refers to **packet.ai**, an on-demand [[Vocabulary/Graphics Processing Units|GPU]] cloud rather than the unrelated AI note-taking device also called Pocket. It offers NVIDIA GPU access with per-hour billing, no minimum term, and pricing positioned at 50% or more below market rates. [^tff4rf]

Core features include shared **Dynamic** GPUs, exclusive **Dedicated** GPUs, and multi-node **clusters**; packet.ai describes Dynamic as the lowest-cost option, Dedicated as providing the whole card with a 99% SLA, and Clusters as custom-priced. [^tff4rf] The service also advertises no egress fees, no platform fee, and the ability to begin without a credit card. [^tff4rf]

- On-demand NVIDIA GPU rentals, including B200, A100, RTX 6000 Pro, L40S, and RTX 4090. [^tff4rf] [^yjjtq1]
- Dynamic shared-GPU capacity for lower-cost workloads. [^tff4rf]
- Dedicated whole-GPU instances with a stated 99% SLA. [^tff4rf]
- Multi-node GPU clusters with custom pricing. [^tff4rf]
- Per-GPU-hour billing that stops when the job finishes. [^tff4rf]
- No egress fees. [^tff4rf]

# Competitive Landscape

## Who it's for, who it's not for

Packet.ai is aimed at solo developers, researchers, startups, and teams that need short-term access to NVIDIA GPUs for open-weight model inference, fine-tuning, experimentation, or production workloads. [^tff4rf] [^yjjtq1] Its hourly billing and stated lack of minimum commitment are suited to workloads that can be started and stopped rather than continuously reserved. [^tff4rf]

It is not positioned as a provider of proprietary hosted models such as GPT-4o or Claude; packet.ai’s own comparison instead emphasizes running open-weight models such as Llama, Qwen, DeepSeek, Mistral, Kimi, and GLM on rented infrastructure. [^yjjtq1]

## Viable Alternatives

- **AWS**, for broad cloud infrastructure, managed machine-learning services, and extensive regional availability.
- **Google Cloud**, for GPU infrastructure integrated with Google’s AI and data platforms.
- **Microsoft Azure**, for enterprise cloud procurement and integration with Microsoft’s AI services.
- **Lambda**, for GPU cloud instances and AI-focused infrastructure.
- **CoreWeave**, for large-scale GPU cloud capacity and AI-oriented infrastructure.
