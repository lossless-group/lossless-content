# Value Proposition & Features

Planon Software provides smart, sustainable building-management software that connects buildings, people, and processes through an integrated information platform, reducing data silos and supporting actionable insight. [^E1]

Its platform spans real-estate, workplace, facilities, asset, maintenance, sustainability, IoT, and digital-twin functions, with capabilities unified through a common data model, authorization framework, and upgrade path. [3]

- **Real-estate management:** Supports portfolio, property, development, and project-control processes. [4]
- **Space and workplace management:** Addresses space planning, workplace services, and campus-management decisions. [4]
- **Asset and maintenance management:** Manages assets, work orders, preventive maintenance, inspections, and service workflows. [9]
- **Integrated services management:** Covers service delivery across facilities and field operations. [^E1]
- **Energy and sustainability management:** Supports energy, emissions, and building-performance processes. [13]
- **IoT and digital-twin integration:** Connects internet technologies and building data within the platform. [3]
- **Enterprise integration:** Supports connections with systems such as SAP, CAD, BIM, building-automation systems, and IoT devices. [13]
- **Analytics and reporting:** Consolidates operational information for reporting and decision-making. [9]

# Competitive Landscape

## Who it's for, who it's not for

Planon is suited to large or complex organizations managing real-estate portfolios, campuses, workplaces, facilities, assets, maintenance, sustainability, and connected-building data in one environment. [^E1] [9] Its listed use cases include corporate real estate, facilities management, service providers, and higher-education organizations. [9]

It is less suited to small organizations seeking a lightweight standalone maintenance or room-booking tool, because the platform spans multiple enterprise domains and typically requires configuration, integrations, and implementation services. [13]

## Viable Alternatives

- **Eptura:** A broad workplace and facilities-management alternative for organizations seeking related IWMS capabilities. [13]
- **Accruent:** An alternative for facilities, maintenance, real estate, and related enterprise-management workflows. [13]
- **Spacewell:** A comparable facilities and workplace-management platform with maintenance, asset, vendor, inventory, compliance, and space capabilities. [8]
- **Maintenance Connection:** A more maintenance-centered alternative for work orders, assets, vendors, service levels, and inspections. [14]
- **ADOX AI CAFM:** A facilities-management alternative with publicly compared work-order and maintenance functionality. [10]
