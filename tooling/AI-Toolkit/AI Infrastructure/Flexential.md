## Value Proposition & Features

Flexential is a U.S. data-center and hybrid-IT infrastructure provider focused on secure, scalable infrastructure for enterprise and high-density workloads. Its platform combines colocation, private cloud, interconnection, data protection, and professional services across more than 40 data centers in 18 markets. [^E1] [10]

Priority features:

- **Colocation:** Enterprise data-center space and power without requiring customers to build and operate their own facilities. [2]
- **Hosted private cloud:** Managed private-cloud infrastructure based on VMware Cloud Foundation 9, covering compute, storage, networking, and management. [10]
- **Interconnection:** Connectivity to carriers, cloud providers, and enterprise environments through a private network backbone; one industry source reports access to more than 80 carriers and hyperscale providers. [2]
- **Cloud connectivity:** Support for hybrid- and multicloud deployments through cloud on-ramps and network interconnection. [2] [9]
- **High-density infrastructure:** Liquid-ready cooling and expanded capacity for artificial-intelligence and machine-learning workloads. [2]
- **Data protection:** Backup, disaster-recovery, and related infrastructure-protection services. [^E1]
- **Managed and professional services:** Migration, infrastructure management, and implementation support for complex IT environments. [^E1] [10]

# Competitive Landscape

## Who it's for, who it's not for

Flexential is aimed at enterprises, cloud and network providers, and organizations requiring secure colocation, hybrid-cloud connectivity, managed private cloud, disaster recovery, or high-density computing capacity. [^E1] [10] Its footprint and interconnection model are also relevant to customers deploying workloads across multiple U.S. markets. [10]

It is not a natural fit for small teams seeking self-service public-cloud resources, consumer hosting, or transparent pay-as-you-go pricing without a sales-led infrastructure engagement. No public source in the available results documents a small-business product tier.
