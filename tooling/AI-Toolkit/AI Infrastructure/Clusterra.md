# Value Proposition & Features

Clusterra.cloud operates managed Slurm clusters end-to-end in a customer’s AWS account, keeping research data in the customer’s VPC while allowing the customer’s AWS and NVIDIA credits to fund compute. [^E1] Its target use cases include computer-aided drug discovery, cryo-electron microscopy, and multi-omics workflows. [^E1]

Core features include:

- **Managed Slurm operations** for research teams running HPC and AI workloads. [^E1] This aligns with [[Tooling/AI-Toolkit/Slurm Workload Manager|Slurm Workload Manager]].
- **API-first cluster management** for programmatic provisioning and workload submission. [^E1]
- **OIDC authentication** for integrated identity and access management. [^E1]
- **Per-user and per-job cost visibility** with submission metadata and trackable per-run costs. [^E1]
- **Usage quotas** for controlling resource consumption. [^E1]
- **Unified queue** for classic HPC and AI workloads. [^E1]
- **Scientific workflow support**, including AlphaFold and nf-core/sarek examples. [^E1]
- **Spot GPU support** and an open-source scientific software stack. [^E1]

# Competitive Landscape

## Who it's for, who it's not for

Clusterra.cloud is aimed at research teams in drug discovery, cryo-EM, multi-omics, and related scientific-computing fields that need managed Slurm operations, reproducible workflows, workload-level cost visibility, and AWS-account or VPC control. [^E1]

It is not clearly positioned for general-purpose web hosting, conventional enterprise IT workloads, or teams seeking a fully provider-owned public supercomputing environment rather than infrastructure operated in their own AWS account.

## Viable Alternatives

- **Self-managed [[Tooling/AI-Toolkit/Slurm Workload Manager|Slurm]]** — suitable for organizations with internal HPC administrators and platform-engineering capacity.
- **AWS ParallelCluster** — a cloud-native option for teams that want to provision and manage Slurm clusters directly on AWS.
- **AWS Batch** — appropriate for batch-oriented workloads that do not require Slurm compatibility.
- **[[Tooling/Software Development/Developer Experience/DevOps/Kubernetes|Kubernetes]]-based research platforms** — suitable for teams standardizing on containers and Kubernetes rather than HPC schedulers.
- **Specialist HPC cloud providers** — relevant for teams that prefer rented infrastructure and provider-managed operations over a bring-your-own-cloud model.
