## Value Proposition & Features

Nebius is an Amsterdam-based AI-cloud provider offering full-stack infrastructure for training, deploying, and running machine-learning and generative-AI workloads. [^E1] Its platform targets high-performance AI computing through dedicated GPU capacity, cloud infrastructure, and developer-facing services.

- **GPU and CPU compute:** On-demand infrastructure for AI training and inference, with support for current Nvidia GPU generations and AMD/Intel CPU offerings.[9]
- **Flexible capacity:** Customers can reserve large clusters for multiple months and receive commitment discounts.[10]
- **Spot pricing:** Preemptible VM pricing dynamically follows demand and can be compared with up to 30 days of price history.[1]
- **Private connectivity:** Cloud Interconnect provides a dedicated, MACsec-encrypted connection between Nebius and customer networks.[2]
- **Developer access:** Cloud Shell offers a browser-based authenticated terminal, while REST APIs expose public services through IAM credentials.[1]
- **Enterprise cost controls:** Cost-allocation labels, downloadable monthly usage reports, and project limits support governance across teams.[1]
- **AI-focused positioning:** Nebius describes its platform as a secure, high-performance, cost-optimized environment for AI developers.[^E1]

## Competitive Landscape

### Who it's for, who it's not for

Nebius is aimed at AI labs, hyperscalers, enterprises, startups, and developers that need substantial GPU capacity for model training, inference, or generative-AI applications.[3] It is especially suited to customers needing dedicated or reserved clusters, private networking, and infrastructure-level cost controls.

It is not an obvious fit for small workloads needing only basic general-purpose cloud services, or for buyers prioritizing the broadest mature hyperscaler service catalog over specialized AI compute.

### Viable Alternatives

- **[[Tooling/AI-Toolkit/AI Infrastructure/CoreWeave|CoreWeave]]:** A direct neocloud alternative specializing in AI GPU infrastructure and competing for large AI-compute contracts.[3]
- **IREN:** An AI-infrastructure provider operating in the same specialized cloud-capacity market.[3]
- **[[concepts/Market-Categories/Hyperscale Cloud Providers|Hyperscale Cloud Providers]]:** Larger general-purpose clouds may offer broader services, though Nebius is more narrowly focused on AI infrastructure.
- **[[Tooling/AI-Toolkit/AI Infrastructure/Lambda Labs|Lambda]]:** A comparable GPU-cloud provider for AI training and inference workloads, as reflected in the competitive set for dedicated GPU infrastructure.
