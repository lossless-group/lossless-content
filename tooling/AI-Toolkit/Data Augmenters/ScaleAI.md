## Value Proposition & Features

Scale AI provides data, evaluation, and AI-agent infrastructure for organizations developing advanced AI systems, including the Scale Generative AI Platform, Scale Data Engine, and Scale Labs. [^E1] Its offering extends from human-generated training data and model evaluation to enterprise control and deployment workflows. [6]

- **Scale Data Engine:** Collects, curates, and annotates datasets for AI applications. [^E1]
- **Generative AI Platform:** Supports building, evaluating, and controlling AI agents and applications. [^E1]
- **Human-feedback data:** Provides labeled data, preference data, red-teaming, and chatbot testing for model developers. [6]
- **Model evaluation:** Scale Labs develops benchmarks and evaluations for measuring model performance. [^E1] [10]
- **Physical-AI data:** Supports robotics and other embodied-AI applications through sensor and operational data. [1]
- **Government and defense applications:** Serves government customers, including U.S. military organizations. [6]
- **Managed human-in-the-loop operations:** Combines automated processing with human review for complex data workflows. [^E1] [[Tooling/AI-Toolkit/Data Augmenters/ScaleAI|ScaleAI]]

## History and Origin Story

Scale AI was founded in 2016 and is headquartered in San Francisco. [^E1] It began with data labeling for autonomous-vehicle applications and expanded into language-model data, evaluation, enterprise AI software, and physical AI. [4] Founder Alexandr Wang later joined Meta after Meta’s 2025 investment in Scale. [6]

# Competitive Landscape

## Who it's for, who it's not for

Scale AI is aimed at enterprises, AI laboratories, robotics developers, autonomous-vehicle companies, and government organizations that need large-scale training data, evaluation, human feedback, or AI-agent infrastructure. [^E1] [6]

It is not a typical self-serve labeling tool for small projects: the returned sources describe enterprise platforms, managed operations, and government-scale engagements rather than transparent, fixed-price plans. [^E1] [6]

## Viable Alternatives

- **Surge AI:** Competes in human-generated AI training and evaluation data. [4]
