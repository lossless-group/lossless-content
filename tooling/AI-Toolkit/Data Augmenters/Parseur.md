Parseur is an AI data-extraction software company that automates extraction from PDFs, emails, spreadsheets, and attachments, then sends structured data to downstream applications in real time via integrations or webhooks. [^E1]

## Value Proposition & Features

Parseur is positioned as a managed **intelligent document processing** platform: it handles document intake, extraction, validation, and delivery rather than returning only raw OCR text. [^1959sp] It is intended to let operations and technical teams automate document workflows without building and maintaining a complete extraction pipeline. [^sp7tzw]

Key features, in priority order:

- **AI document extraction:** AI Vision reads PDFs, scans, images, tables, handwriting, checkboxes, stamps, and signatures, while AI Text handles text-based documents such as emails and spreadsheet exports. [^uwhv1n] [^i5yn4o]
- **Template-free extraction:** Users describe desired fields in plain language, and the AI engine identifies them across varying layouts without requiring a separate positional template for each sender. [^uwhv1n]
- **Multi-channel intake:** Documents can arrive through email mailboxes, manual upload, or API submission. [^uwhv1n]
- **Structured output:** Parseur returns extracted fields and tables as structured data, including JSON delivery through webhooks. [^1959sp]
- **Validation and review:** The workflow supports field validation, exception review, and inspection of extracted values alongside the source document. [^1959sp] [^uwhv1n]
- **Integrations:** Parseur connects with Google Sheets, Excel, Zapier, Make, Microsoft Power Automate, n8n, accounting platforms, and other downstream applications. [^1959sp] [^uwhv1n] [^9ca01x]
- **API and webhooks:** Developers can submit documents through an extraction API and deliver results to their own systems through APIs or webhooks, with retry and delivery-log capabilities. [^1959sp]
- **Data controls:** Parseur supports configurable mailbox retention, including a “Process Then Delete” option, and states that customer data is processed and stored in the European Union with AES-256 encryption at rest. [^68j3dq]

# Competitive Landscape

## Who it's for, who it's not for

Parseur is aimed at operations, finance, logistics, accounting, and technical teams that receive recurring PDFs, emails, spreadsheets, invoices, statements, or other semi-structured documents and need to turn them into validated data for downstream systems. [^1959sp] [^uwhv1n] [^9ca01x]

It is less suited to buyers seeking only raw OCR text, direct custodian or brokerage APIs, or a fully bespoke enterprise implementation; Parseur is presented as a managed document-processing layer, and it does not directly connect to brokerage or custodian APIs. [^uwhv1n] [^sp7tzw]

## Viable Alternatives

- **OCR APIs:** Suitable when engineering teams want raw text, page positions, or coordinates and will build their own extraction and delivery logic. [^1959sp]
- **Multimodal LLM APIs:** Flexible for teams that want to construct a custom document-understanding pipeline, but they require the buyer to implement surrounding validation and delivery workflows. [^1959sp]
- **Regex and rule-based extraction:** Appropriate for highly predictable document formats where deterministic rules are preferred. [^1959sp]
- **Enterprise IDP platforms:** Relevant to organizations seeking broader implementation services or heavyweight enterprise document-processing deployments. [^sp7tzw]
- **Custom extraction pipelines:** Suitable for teams that need complete control over ingestion, processing, storage, validation, and delivery. [^1959sp]
