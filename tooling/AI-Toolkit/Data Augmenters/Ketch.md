Ketch is enterprise privacy-management software focused on consent, data-subject requests, data mapping, assessments, and privacy-safe data activation. Its positioning centers on making privacy choices actionable across websites, apps, connected TV, identity systems, and downstream data platforms. [^E1] [^d8zuzr]

## Value Proposition & Features

Ketch is designed for organizations that need centralized privacy controls across multiple brands, jurisdictions, devices, and data systems. Its platform treats consent and preference decisions as operational signals that can be propagated to connected systems rather than retaining them only in a cookie banner. [^E1] [^j1167a]

Priority capabilities:

- **Consent and preference management:** Collects consent, manages preference centers, supports privacy-law templates, and maintains consent records. [^E1] [^owqh28]
- **Identity-aware enforcement:** Resolves identifiers across devices, browsers, applications, and platforms so privacy choices can follow a recognized individual. [^d8zuzr]
- **Data-subject request automation:** Supports configurable workflows for access, deletion, and other rights requests. [^E1] [^d8zuzr]
- **Data mapping and discovery:** Uses AI-assisted discovery, classification, data maps, and records-of-processing capabilities. [^E1] [^owqh28]
- **Tracker and tag governance:** Discovers and classifies trackers and orchestrates tags according to applicable permissions. [^d8zuzr] [^b49n6y]
- **Downstream signal orchestration:** Sends consent and opt-out decisions to marketing, advertising, warehouse, and other connected systems. [^d8zuzr] [^j1167a]
- **AI privacy controls:** Ketch markets AI-oriented privacy and data-governance capabilities, including AI Sentry for detecting sensitive information in prompts and preventing unauthorized data use. [^91jkmw]
- **Regulatory coverage:** Supports [[projects/Emergent-Innovation/Policy-&-Regulation/General Data Protection Regulation|GDPR]], CCPA, and U.S. state privacy-law programs. [^E1]

## Competitive Landscape

### Who it's for, who it's not for

Ketch is suited to mid-market and enterprise organizations operating across multiple jurisdictions, brands, digital properties, and downstream data systems that need centralized consent, rights-request, tracker, identity, and data-governance controls. [^E1] [^d8zuzr]

It is less suited to small organizations seeking only a basic cookie banner, or buyers that require fully transparent enterprise pricing for advanced DSR and data-mapping functionality, because those capabilities may require add-ons or the custom Pro tier. [^owqh28]

### Viable Alternatives

- **OneTrust:** Broad enterprise privacy, governance, risk, and compliance suite with extensive regulatory and workflow coverage. [^91jkmw]
- **Transcend:** Strong alternative for privacy-rights automation and enterprise data orchestration. [^91jkmw]
- **Usercentrics:** Consent-management platform with emphasis on consent evidence, CMP deployment, and regulatory configuration. [^91jkmw]
- **Osano:** Privacy-management and consent alternative aimed at simplifying compliance operations. [^91jkmw]
