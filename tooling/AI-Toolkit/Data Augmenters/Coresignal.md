# Value Proposition & Features

Coresignal provides structured public-web data on companies, professionals, and job postings for AI agents, enterprise applications, and business workflows. [^E1] Its value proposition is combining continuously refreshed records with [[Vocabulary/Application Programming Interface|APIs]], bulk datasets, and self-service workflows for operational data use cases. [^E1]

Core product features include APIs for on-demand enrichment and workflow integration, bulk datasets for large-scale analysis, and webhooks for monitoring changes over time. [^mqkpo4] Coresignal also offers agent-oriented access, including a natural-language search API and MCP server, allowing AI workflows to query company, employee, and job data. [^mqkpo4]

- **Company data:** firmographic, organizational, technology, funding, location, and growth information delivered through APIs and datasets. [^juc672]
- **Employee and professional data:** structured profiles and enrichment records for talent, sales, recruiting, and market-intelligence workflows. [^E1]
- **Job-posting data:** hiring activity and job records suitable for workforce and demand-signal analysis. [^mqkpo4]
- **Data Search:** self-service search for building targeted company, employee, and job lists; plans start at $49 per month according to Coresignal’s announcement. [^h6xzok]
- **Bulk delivery:** JSONL, [[projects/Emergent-Innovation/Standards/Parquet File Format|Parquet]], and [[Vocabulary/Comma-Separated Values|CSV]] files, including delivery to cloud-storage environments used by Snowflake. [^juc672]
- **APIs and integrations:** [[Vocabulary/REST API|REST API]]-style access and integrations with tools such as Clay, [[Tooling/Software Development/Developer Experience/DevOps/Zapier|Zapier]], and [[projects/Context-Vigilance/UseCases/n8n|n8n]]. [^1765cz] [^spc5ut]
- **[[Webhooks]] and monitoring:** ongoing change detection for revenue-intelligence and other signal-monitoring workflows. [^mqkpo4]
- **AI-agent access:** natural-language search and an [[concepts/Explainers for AI/MCP Servers|MCP Servers]] for agentic querying. [^mqkpo4]

Coresignal is a stronger fit for teams seeking curated business datasets than for users wanting only a simple URL-in/content-out scraping API, a distinction reflected in the vault’s comparison with [[Tooling/AI-Toolkit/Data Augmenters/Bright Data|Bright Data]].

# Competitive Landscape

## Who it's for, who it's not for

Coresignal is intended for data teams, AI-product builders, sales and recruiting platforms, investment and market-intelligence teams, and enterprises that need continuously refreshed company, professional, and job data through APIs or bulk delivery. [^E1] [^mqkpo4] It is particularly suited to organizations building their own enrichment, monitoring, analytics, or agentic workflows.

It is less suitable for users seeking only lightweight webpage extraction, because its positioning emphasizes curated business datasets, structured records, enrichment, and delivery infrastructure rather than general-purpose scraping alone. [^ai376u]

## Viable Alternatives

- **People Data Labs:** a comparable provider of professional and company data through APIs and enrichment workflows. [^u7uul0]
- **Crustdata:** an alternative for company and people datasets delivered as flat files alongside API access. [^u7uul0]
- **[[Tooling/Enterprise Jobs-to-be-Done/Apollo|Apollo]]:** a sales-intelligence and contact-data alternative for prospecting and enrichment workflows. [^r1gq7f]
- **Clearbit / HubSpot Breeze:** an alternative ecosystem for business-data enrichment, though the retrieved comparison frames Coresignal as offering a broader combination of real-time APIs and bulk datasets. [^96cc2b]
- **[[Tooling/AI-Toolkit/Data Augmenters/Bright Data|Bright Data]]:** a stronger alternative for general web-data collection and scraping infrastructure, while Coresignal is more focused on curated business datasets.
