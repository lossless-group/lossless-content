## Value Proposition & Features

Decart is a frontier AI lab founded in 2023 that builds real-time world models and infrastructure for running generative AI with very low latency. [^E1] Its stated product family includes Oasis, Lucy, and the Decart Optimization Stack (DOS). [^E1]

- **Real-time world models:** Generates responsive interactive environments for applications such as robotics, autonomous-driving development, and digital-world simulation. [^i7scjj]
- **Lucy:** Real-time video generation and transformation, including live video editing. [^i7scjj]
- **Oasis:** Simulated environments intended for training and testing robotics and autonomous-driving systems. [^i7scjj]
- **Decart Optimization Stack (DOS):** Infrastructure for optimizing AI workloads across hardware and inference systems. [^hp6bls]
- **Hardware portability:** DOS is described as supporting NVIDIA, AWS Trainium, and Google TPU platforms. [^hp6bls]
- **Low-latency inference:** Decart positions its infrastructure around faster, more efficient model execution and millisecond-latency experiences. [^E1]
- **Generative interactive models:** Combines model development with infrastructure for responsive visual and physical-world applications. [^E1]

# History and Origin Story

Decart was founded in 2023 by Dean Leitersdorf, Orian Leitersdorf, and Moshe Shalev, according to multiple reports. [^qy3tdp] [^6rtzw8] The company developed from real-time generative video and world-model research into a broader platform spanning interactive models and AI optimization infrastructure. # Competitive Landscape

## Who it's for, who it's not for

Decart is aimed at AI developers, robotics and autonomous-driving teams, model operators, and organizations that need responsive generative environments or efficient inference across heterogeneous hardware. [^hp6bls] [^i7scjj]

It is not positioned as a general-purpose consumer chatbot, conventional video-editing suite, or ordinary cloud infrastructure provider; its products focus on model execution, real-time visual generation, and interactive-world simulation. [^E1] [^4ejmql]

## Viable Alternatives

- **NVIDIA Omniverse:** A plausible alternative for teams prioritizing physically based 3D simulation and robotics workflows.
- **World Labs:** An alternative for generative 3D-world construction and spatial intelligence.
- **Runway:** An alternative for generative video creation and transformation, though not necessarily equivalent in world-model infrastructure.
- **Google [[organizations/DeepMind|DeepMind]]:** A potential alternative for organizations seeking frontier world-model and robotics research.
- **AWS AI infrastructure:** A potential alternative for model-serving and hardware-optimization requirements, especially where cloud-native deployment is the priority.
