## Value Proposition & Features

xAI develops frontier AI models under the Grok brand for reasoning, coding, knowledge work, agentic tasks, voice, image generation, and video generation. [^E1] Its products serve both end users through Grok and developers through the Grok API. [^E1] Grok’s positioning emphasizes broad multimodal capability, configurable reasoning, real-time tool use, and integration with X’s information ecosystem.[^om62em]

- **Grok foundation models:** Models for text, coding, reasoning, and agentic workflows, including a 500,000-token context window in Grok 4.7.[^qqyqe1]
- **Developer API:** Programmatic access to models across text, code, voice, image, and video.[^om62em]
- **Agentic tool calling:** APIs support models that can call tools and operate inside developer-built workflows.[^qqyqe1]
- **Multimodal input:** Grok 4.7 accepts text and images and produces text output.[^qqyqe1]
- **Voice:** Grok Voice 3.0 supports speech-to-speech interaction through an API priced per minute.[^pulih5]
- **Image generation and editing:** Grok Imagine supports image generation, edits using up to five reference images, and image-to-image workflows.[^25s33b]
- **Video generation:** Grok Imagine supports text-to-video, still-image-to-video, and video editing through asynchronous API requests.[^91pgv1]
- **Grok Build:** The Responses API supports agentic coding workflows such as refactoring and debugging.[^om62em]

## Product Roadmap / Announcements

As of November 2, 2026,

- **October 27, 2026:** Grok 4.7 became available through the xAI API for coding, agentic tasks, and knowledge work.[^qqyqe1]
- **October 14, 2026:** xAI announced Grok Voice 3.0 for its speech-to-speech API.[^pulih5]
- **October 8, 2026:** xAI documented the Grok Imagine Video API for asynchronous video-generation requests.[^91pgv1]
- **October 8, 2026:** xAI documented the Imagine API for image generation, image editing, and video creation.[^25s33b]

## Recent Developments

xAI announced a $20 billion Series E round on January 6, 2026, at a reported $230 billion valuation, with participation from Nvidia, Cisco Investments, Fidelity, Qatar Investment Authority, MGX, and other investors. [^F1] The company had previously announced a $6 billion Series C round in December 2024 at a $45 billion valuation. [^F2]

# History and Origin Story

xAI was founded in 2023 to build frontier AI models, with Elon Musk as its founder and a founding team that included researchers such as Igor Babuschkin, Christian Szegedy, Jimmy Ba, and Greg Yang.[^75e2bz] Its principal product became Grok, which expanded from a chatbot into an API platform covering text, code, voice, image, and video.[^om62em] xAI’s own materials describe the company as building “frontier AI models for reasoning, voice, image generation, and more.” [^E1]

## Competitive Landscape

### Who it's for, who it's not for

xAI is aimed at developers building applications that need frontier language models, long-context processing, agentic tool use, coding assistance, voice interfaces, or generative image and video features.[^qqyqe1][^om62em][^25s33b] It also targets end users seeking a general-purpose conversational AI product through [[Tooling/AI-Toolkit/Models/Grok|Grok]]. [^E1]

It is not a strong fit for buyers requiring transparent public financials, fully open model weights, or a published enterprise subscription matrix; the available materials primarily expose usage-based API pricing and product documentation.[^qqyqe1][^om62em]

### Viable Alternatives

- **[[Tooling/AI-Toolkit/Model Producers/OpenAI|OpenAI]]:** A broad alternative for general-purpose models, multimodal APIs, agents, and media generation.
- **[[Tooling/AI-Toolkit/Model Producers/Anthropic|Anthropic]]:** A strong alternative for enterprise language-model and coding workloads.
- **Google [[Tooling/AI-Toolkit/Models/Gemini|Gemini]]:** An alternative for multimodal models and ecosystem-integrated AI services.
- **Meta [[Tooling/AI-Toolkit/Models/LLaMA|LLaMA]]:** An alternative for organizations prioritizing downloadable or self-hosted model options.
- **[[Mistral AI]]:** An alternative for API-accessible and more deployment-flexible language models.
