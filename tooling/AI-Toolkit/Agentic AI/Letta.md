## Value Proposition & Features

Letta is a San Francisco AI research lab and software ecosystem for building **stateful agents**—agents designed to retain memory, manage context, and improve through experience. [^ea1s37] [^1z7wea] Its work originated with the creators of MemGPT at [[organizations/UC Berkeley|UC Berkeley]]’s Sky Computing Lab. [^ea1s37]

- **Stateful agent runtime:** Provides the umbrella ecosystem for hosted and open-source agent products. [^1z7wea]
- **Persistent memory:** Enables agents to retain information across interactions and use memory as part of ongoing learning. [^ea1s37]
- **[[concepts/Explainers for AI/Context Management]]:** Focuses on programmatic context management and “context repositories” with Git-based versioning. [^ea1s37]
- **Letta Code:** Offers an installable AI coding agent whose memory, identity, and capabilities evolve with experience. [^ea1s37]
- **Memory research:** Develops memory models and benchmarks for measuring continual agent learning. [^ea1s37]
- **Agent evaluation:** Letta [[concepts/Explainers for AI/Agent Evals]] supports configurations for creating, accessing, and testing agents or systems. [^3kl0q6]
- **Developer [[Vocabulary/Application Programming Interface|APIs]]:** Letta’s documentation includes APIs for querying agent execution steps. [^jut46k]

## Recent Developments

Letta’s public positioning has expanded from agent memory infrastructure toward AI systems that learn continuously through memory, context management, and evolving capabilities. [^ea1s37] Its current product presentation centers on Letta Code, while its research agenda includes memory models, continual-learning benchmarks, and context repositories. [^ea1s37]

## History and Origin Story

Letta was founded by the creators of MemGPT from UC Berkeley’s Sky Computing Lab, the research environment associated with projects including Spark and Ray. [^ea1s37] The company describes itself as an AI research lab building “machines that learn” and lists Ion Stoica and Joey Gonzalez as advisers. [^ea1s37]

## Competitive Landscape

### Who it's for, who it's not for

Letta is aimed at developers and AI research teams building agents that must preserve state, manage context across sessions, evaluate behavior, or improve through accumulated experience. [^ea1s37] [^1z7wea] [^3kl0q6] It is less suited to teams seeking only a stateless chatbot wrapper or a conventional one-shot completion API.

### Viable Alternatives

- **[[MemGPT]]:** A closely related alternative for memory-managed agents, given that Letta originated with MemGPT’s creators. [^ea1s37]
- **LangGraph:** A potential alternative for developers prioritizing explicit agent workflows and state orchestration.
- **[[Tooling/AI-Toolkit/Agentic AI/Mem0|Mem0]]:** A potential alternative focused primarily on adding persistent memory to AI applications.
- **[[Tooling/AI-Toolkit/Agentic AI/Zep|Zep]]:** A potential alternative for managed user and conversational memory.
- **[[Microsoft Semantic Kernel]]:** A broader agent and orchestration framework that can serve teams wanting an enterprise-oriented ecosystem.
