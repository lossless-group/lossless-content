# Value Proposition & Features

Dove is an AI email client designed to reduce inbox noise by sorting incoming mail into **Focus, Feed, and Noise**, while its **Today** view highlights messages requiring attention, schedule items, and pending follow-ups. [^5hp5h3] It supports multiple providers and devices, positioning itself for people managing several busy inboxes. [^5hp5h3]

- **AI inbox sorting:** Classifies new messages into Focus, Feed, and Noise. [^5hp5h3]
- **Today view:** Surfaces actionable mail, calendar information, and items users are waiting on. [^5hp5h3]
- **AI drafting and summaries:** Drafts replies and summarizes email threads. [^mmron4]
- **AI email questions and search:** Supports questions about mail; free-plan AI actions include drafts, summaries, searches, and questions. [^rp6z03]
- **Multi-provider support:** Works with Gmail, Google Workspace, Outlook.com, Microsoft 365, iCloud, Yahoo, and IMAP accounts. [^mmron4]
- **Cross-platform access:** Available on Mac, Windows, iPhone, iPad, Android, and the web. [^0fl4jx]
- **One-tap unsubscribe:** Provides unsubscribe actions from the email menu or as a swipe action. [^chi3z9]
- **Privacy positioning:** Dove says it does not use email content to train AI models; mail is encrypted in transit and at rest. [^mmron4]

# Competitive Landscape

## Who it's for, who it's not for

Dove is aimed at individuals handling several accounts across Gmail, Outlook, iCloud, Yahoo, or IMAP who want AI to prioritize mail, reduce newsletter and promotional clutter, and help draft responses. [^5hp5h3] [^mmron4] It is also suited to users who want one client across desktop, mobile, and web platforms. [^0fl4jx]

It is less suitable for users seeking a free, rules-first workflow, end-to-end encryption, or team-oriented shared-inbox functionality; the available material describes subscription pricing, AI-based rather than user-defined sorting, and encryption in transit and at rest rather than PGP or end-to-end encryption. [^mmron4] [^7mgi94]

## Viable Alternatives

- **Spark Mail:** A multi-platform email client with comparable cross-provider use; Dove differentiates itself through automatic Focus, Feed, and Noise sorting. [^rp6z03]
- **HEY:** A sender-screening-oriented email service; Dove instead keeps existing provider accounts and applies AI sorting. [^7mgi94]
- **Mailbird:** A multi-account desktop email client; Dove adds AI sorting, Today, and AI drafting. [^x13opr]
- **Missive:** A stronger alternative for shared inboxes, team chat, and collaborative drafts. [^5hp5h3]
- **Gmail:** A native option for Gmail users who prefer Google’s ecosystem and labels; Dove supports Gmail while unifying additional providers. [^0fl4jx]
