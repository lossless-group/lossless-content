## Value Proposition & Features

Sintra is an **AI employees platform** that gives small business owners a pre-built team of role-based digital workers to run socials, inbox, website, content, customer support, business operations, and sales 24/7 so “work gets done consistently without constant supervision.” [^yn87gr] [^joz4dy] [^E1] It positions itself as an all‑in‑one **AI workforce platform** where each “AI employee” has a name, role, and skill set, coordinated through a shared **Brain AI** knowledge hub that learns the brand voice and stores documents. [^41zsvu] [^6njrwl] [^l1pxie] Sintra targets founders and small businesses that want an autonomous, always‑on operational layer rather than building custom agents or learning complex prompting, a fit that aligns naturally with tools mapped in [[lost-in-public/market-maps/Agentic AI in Fintech|Agentic AI in Fintech]].

**Core product concepts**

Sintra sells a roster of **12 specialized AI employees / helpers**—for social, copy, SEO, sales, support, ecommerce, email, recruiting, data, admin, personal growth, and executive assistance—each with an avatar, job title, and one‑click tasks. [^3r2ire] [^w5kuex] [^w4az4p] Brain AI acts as a central knowledge base where users load brand guidelines, tone, documents, and business context so every helper produces aligned, on‑brand output across channels. [^41zsvu] [^6njrwl] [^w4az4p] The system runs on multiple advanced models such as GPT‑4.1, Claude 4 Sonnet, Gemini 2.5, and Flux for text, image, and video content, orchestrated inside a chat‑based workspace and web/mobile apps. [^jdow8d] [^du6quz] [^tkw79y] [^6gtxnr]

**Key feature sets (2–3 sentences each)**

- **AI Employees / Helpers**  
  Sintra’s “AI employees” are role‑based digital workers that operate inside the user’s tools, follow workflows, learn the business over time, and take ownership of specific functions like marketing, support, or operations. [^joz4dy] [^yn87gr] They are sold as named personas (e.g., Soshie for social, Penn for copy, Seomi for SEO, Cassie for support, Buddy for business development, Dexter for data, Gigi for growth coach, Vizzy for executive assistant, Scouty for HR, Commet for ecommerce, etc.) with pre‑configured tasks and workflows. [^3r2ire] [^w5kuex] [^w4az4p]

- **Brain AI (Knowledge Hub)**  
  Brain AI is a shared knowledge hub that stores documents, brand voice, guidelines, and other business context, and is used by all helpers to keep output consistent. [^41zsvu] [^6njrwl] [^w4az4p] Users create profiles, add information, and attach those profiles to helpers so each AI employee writes and acts in the company’s voice across emails, social posts, support replies, and more. [^6njrwl] [^b7uy3s] [^o84goj]

- **AI Assistants, Copilots, Bots, Operators, Chatbots, Agents**  
  Sintra exposes its workers through multiple solution wrappers—AI assistants, copilots, bots, operators, chatbots, conversational agents, generative agents, “AI workforce,” and an AI agent orchestrator—aimed at automating daily tasks, content creation, lead handling, customer replies, and multi‑step workflows. [^c1r5ru] [^jdow8d] [^7vtw8f] [^fpr4s3] [^2snl6m] [^iua5uy] [^g48fq1] [^chjkg4] [^unj08y] These helpers can create blog posts, manage emails, track leads, automate follow‑ups, draft reports, and run orchestrated workflows powered by Brain AI and integrated tools. [^2snl6m] [^fpr4s3] [^1gtipt] [^chjkg4]

- **Vertical “AI for X” templates**  
  Sintra offers verticalized templates such as **AI for ecommerce**, **automotive**, **UI design studios**, and more, where Brain AI is pre‑shaped around use cases like running dealership lead intake, ecommerce store operations, or design‑studio marketing. [^cxulb5] [^otqlv6] [^y4iye8] [^o84goj] These industry pages guide users to fill Brain AI with catalogs, past content, and client context, then let AI employees handle leads, follow‑ups, social content, and routine communications. [^otqlv6] [^b7uy3s] [^o84goj]

- **Workspace & Apps**  
  The platform is delivered via a **workspace‑based web app** and mobile apps (including “Sintra: AI Employees” and “AI Copilots: Your Pre‑Built Team for Business”), where users chat with helpers, manage Brain AI, and configure workflows. [^6gtxnr] [^l1pxie] [^ukrmy0] Login is via email+code, Google, or Apple, with subscription‑gated access and support for password setup and troubleshooting from the Help Center. [^7lav47] [^f815z3]

**Top features (5–8 bullets, priority order)**

- **12 named AI employees/helpers covering socials, copywriting, SEO, sales, support, ecommerce, email, recruiting, data analysis, admin, growth coaching, and executive assistance. [^3r2ire] [^w5kuex] [^w4az4p]**
- **Brain AI shared knowledge hub that stores brand voice, documents, and business context for all helpers. [^41zsvu] [^6njrwl] [^w4az4p]**
- **Pre‑built AI assistants, copilots, bots, operators, chatbots, and agents for automated content, inbox management, lead tracking, and customer support. [^c1r5ru] [^jdow8d] [^2snl6m] [^fpr4s3] [^iua5uy] [^g48fq1]**
- **AI agent orchestrator to coordinate multiple agents and helpers into larger, multi‑step workflows. [^1gtipt] [^chjkg4] [^unj08y]**
- **Vertical “AI for X” packages (ecommerce, automotive, UI design studios, etc.) tuned for specific small‑business industries. [^cxulb5] [^otqlv6] [^y4iye8] [^o84goj]**
- **Workspace‑based web and mobile apps with chat interface and centralized settings for account, language, subscription, and experimental features. [^6gtxnr] [^l1pxie] [^7lav47] [^f815z3]**
- **Multi‑model backend using GPT‑4.1, Claude 4 Sonnet, Gemini 2.5, Flux and similar models for text, image, and video automation. [^jdow8d] [^du6quz] [^tkw79y]**
- **Integrations across 15+ tools to connect marketing, sales, support, and operations workflows (not all tools are listed, but integrations are referenced in reviews and solution pages). [^3r2ire] [^fs08ax] [^du6quz]**

## History and Origin Story

Sintra was founded in **2023** in **Vilnius, Vilniaus Apskritis, Lithuania** by **Chris Sidlauskas**, **Rokas Judickas**, and **Vasaris Kaveckas**. [^7xjm05] [^E1] From inception it focused on building AI‑powered digital teams for small businesses, marketed as “AI employees that never sleep” and aimed at automating routine work across marketing, sales, customer support, and operations. [^yn87gr] [^ak3wf3] [^hl0fpb] By mid‑2025, the company had reportedly reached **over 40,000 paying customers** and **€10.5M ARR** at the time of its seed round, scaling to “50,000+ founders” by 2026 according to product pages and virtual‑assistant copy. [^i9ky20] [^gpkrh3] [^y4iye8]

## Competitive Landscape

### Who it’s for, who it’s not for

Sintra is designed for **solo founders, small business owners, and small marketing/support teams** who need repeatable help with content, social media, inbox management, customer support, light sales, and admin, and prefer pre‑built role personas over building their own agents. [^w4az4p] [^yn87gr] [^3r2ire] [^yyzw9q] It suits SMBs that want a “24/7 digital team” handling daily operations without adding headcount, especially in ecommerce, local services, creative studios, and similar sectors where routine communication and marketing work dominate. [^otqlv6] [^y4iye8] [^o84goj] [^hl0fpb]

It is **not** aimed at technical teams needing deep custom agent development, enterprise IT departments requiring heavy governance, or founders who want AI to design and run the entire product stack; reviewers stress that Sintra is a “task” product focused on content/ops, not a platform to build and operate complex software or end‑to‑end venture workflows. [^w4az4p] [^hl0fpb] [^503o0y] For organizations standardized on major ecosystems (Microsoft 365, Salesforce, etc.) or needing governed brand content at scale, tools like Microsoft Copilot Studio, Salesforce Agentforce, Jasper, or HubSpot Agent Hub may be better fits than Sintra’s SMB‑oriented templates. [^t28cbn] [^u54hej] [^503o0y]

### Viable Alternatives

- **Lindy** – A general‑purpose AI teammate that works across meetings, inboxes, files, Slack, browser tasks, and thousands of integrations; better for teams willing to design workflows rather than use fixed personas. [^t28cbn] [^yyzw9q] [^u54hej]
- **NoimosAI** – Marketing‑specialized agent platform focused on connected strategy and execution (research, SEO, GEO, social, outreach, analytics); stronger for comprehensive marketing orchestration than Sintra’s task‑level helpers. [^t28cbn]
- **Relevance AI** – Low‑/no‑code platform for multi‑agent workflows and automation, with free and Pro tiers; better for custom multi‑agent coordination and API chaining. [^t28cbn] [^wu4y5h] [^u54hej]
- **Zapier Agents** – Agentic layer on top of Zapier’s 9,000+ integrations, ideal for cross‑app automation rather than Sintra’s content‑centric tasks. [^t28cbn] [^u54hej] [^h2p0kl]
- **Taskade** – Budget multi‑agent workspace combining projects, knowledge, agents, automations, and app building; good for teams needing flexible collab+automation rather than fixed role helpers. [^t28cbn] [^u54hej] [^ebd4wx]
