# The Prompting Company
https://promptingcompany.com/

The Prompting Company helps products get mentioned in ChatGPT and provides AI/LLM-driven prompting and related services for marketing, discovery, and search optimization within AI conversations. [^E1]

## Value Proposition & Features

The company’s stated value proposition is to track how AI assistants describe a product and turn those insights into product information that agents can find, evaluate, and use. [^E1]

Its apparent core offering is **generative-engine optimization**: improving a product’s visibility and representation in conversational AI systems such as ChatGPT. [^E1]

Priority features described by the available source:

- Monitoring how AI assistants mention a product. [^E1]
- Product discovery optimization within AI conversations. [^E1]
- Prompting and LLM-driven analysis. [^E1]
- Marketing support for AI-mediated discovery. [^E1]
- Lead-generation support. [^E1]
- Agentic-AI-oriented product positioning. [^E1]
