Azoma is an Agentic Commerce Optimization platform for consumer brands that measures and improves product visibility across AI shopping agents, answer engines, and retailer assistants. Its stated goal is to help products become discoverable, understandable, and recommendable in systems such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Alexa for Shopping, Walmart Sparky, and Meta Muse. [1][2]

## Value Proposition & Features

Azoma combines measurement, optimization, and execution across product data, brand content, and third-party sources that influence AI recommendations. [2] Its platform is aimed at turning AI visibility insights into changes to listings, websites, and external content. [2]

- **Shopping-agent visibility measurement:** Tracks how agents see, rank, mention, cite, and recommend products. [2][5]
- **Multi-agent monitoring:** Covers 10 agents, including ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, Claude, Alexa for Shopping, Walmart Sparky, Instacart Clementine, Lazada Lazzie, and Target Shopping Assistant. [1][9]
- **Agentic modules:** Generate, approve, and publish listing, website, and third-party content used by shopping agents. [2]
- **Product-data optimization:** Improves item names, features, descriptions, attributes, variants, rich media, and Q&A. [8]
- **Prompt and persona tracking:** Measures visibility across selected markets, agents, shopper prompts, and personas. [9]
- **Digital Twin technology:** Simulates AI shopper personas to show how different buyer types encounter a brand’s products. [3]
- **Platform integrations:** Connects marketplaces, product-data systems, and analytics supporting a catalog. [2]
- **Conversational Briefs:** Captures product use cases, buying contexts, and combinations to help agents understand products. [4]

# History and Origin Story

Azoma was founded in 2022 by Max Sinclair, a former Amazon executive, and Timur Luguev, a machine-learning PhD and postdoctoral researcher. [3] The company developed around measuring and optimizing how AI systems discover, interpret, rank, and recommend consumer products, later extending its scope from answer engines and retailer assistants toward personal AI agents. [3][4]

## Competitive Landscape

## Who it's for, who it's not for

Azoma is designed for enterprise consumer brands and agencies managing AI visibility across multiple brands, catalogs, markets, and shopping agents. [1][8] The company’s cited customers include L’Oréal, Unilever, Mars, Beiersdorf, and Reckitt. [3]

It is less suited to small merchants seeking a simple storefront, basic SEO monitoring, or a standalone product-feed editor, because the platform focuses on multi-agent measurement, enterprise product data, content execution, and third-party source optimization. [2][8]

## Viable Alternatives

- **Traditional enterprise SEO and GEO platforms:** Suitable for brands prioritizing web-search and answer-engine visibility rather than retailer-agent execution.
- **Product-information-management platforms:** Suitable for centralizing catalog data, but generally narrower than Azoma’s agent measurement and recommendation analysis.
- **Retail marketplace-optimization tools:** Suitable for optimizing listings on individual marketplaces rather than monitoring multiple AI agents.
- **In-house analytics and content operations:** Suitable for large brands with engineering, commerce, and digital-marketing teams capable of building multi-agent monitoring internally.
