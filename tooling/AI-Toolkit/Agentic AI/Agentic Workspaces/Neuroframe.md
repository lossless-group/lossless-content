## Value Proposition & Features

Neuroframe is the company behind **Super**, a shared AI agent for teams that preserves context across conversations and projects and connects to company knowledge and tools such as OpenCode. Its stated value proposition is to help teams recover context instantly, stay aligned, and move faster. [^E1]

- **Persistent company context:** Remembers clients, workflows, decisions, and team context. [^E1]
- **Shared team agent:** Designed for use by a team rather than as an isolated personal assistant. [^E1]
- **Cross-project continuity:** Keeps context across conversations and projects. [^E1]
- **Company knowledge access:** Works with an organization’s existing knowledge. [^E1]
- **Tool integrations:** Uses existing tools, including [[Tooling/AI-Toolkit/Agentic AI/OpenCode|OpenCode]]. [^E1]
- **Context recovery:** Helps users recover prior decisions and operational context quickly. [^E1]
- **Workflow alignment:** Supports coordination across team members and projects. [^E1]
