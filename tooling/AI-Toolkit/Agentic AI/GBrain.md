## Value Proposition & Features

GBrain is an [[concepts/Explainers for AI/Memory Layers|Memory Layer]] and knowledge layer intended to give the AIs a user already uses a shared understanding of people, companies, and working context. [^60eeis] Its Personal plan combines memory, skills, and connected applications while leaving the underlying reasoning to the user’s existing Claude or ChatGPT account. [^60eeis]

- **Shared memory:** Maintains one memory across Claude, ChatGPT, Claude Code, Codex, Capy, Cursor, and other MCP-compatible AIs. [^60eeis]
- **Relationship records:** Provides pages for people and companies a user works with. [^60eeis]
- **Portable knowledge:** Stores information in plain files that users can read, correct, and take with them. [^60eeis]
- **Connected context:** Connects Gmail, calendar, and meeting notes once for reuse across supported AIs. [^60eeis]
- **Application access:** Exposes connected applications through AI interfaces and a command-line experience. [^lih6nx] [^lcj484]
- **[[concepts/Explainers for AI/Model Context Protocol|MCP]] connectivity:** Uses MCP-compatible connections to support additional AI clients. [^60eeis]
- **Connector catalog:** Offers application-specific interfaces for tools such as Kajabi, Attio, Recraft, AWS Marketplace, Gem, TwinMind, Ninout, Profound, and Attentive. [^lih6nx] [^lcj484] [^1s89kx] [^3r0dtk] [^jnxu0f] [^aqlx1z] [^qf163x] [^8ux74f] [^8c3ve5]

# Competitive Landscape

## Who it's for, who it's not for

GBrain is aimed at individuals or small teams that use several AI clients and want shared context, portable records, and access to business applications without reconnecting each tool separately. [^60eeis] It is particularly relevant to users working across Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, and MCP-compatible systems. [^60eeis]

It is less suitable for organizations seeking publicly documented enterprise pricing, audited security details, or a mature procurement and governance program, because those details were not established in the available results.

## Viable Alternatives

- **Native MCP integrations:** A lower-cost approach for users who only need direct AI-to-application connections, without a shared memory layer.
- **AI workspace platforms:** Suitable for teams that prefer one centralized environment rather than keeping their existing AI clients.
- **CRM and knowledge-base systems:** Better for organizations whose primary requirement is structured records or documentation rather than cross-agent memory.
- **Custom internal tooling:** Appropriate for teams needing bespoke permissions, workflows, or deployment controls.
