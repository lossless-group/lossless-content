### Kin AI — 
https://mykin.ai/ [^E1]

Kin AI is described as a software-development company offering Kin, a personal AI advisory board made from specialized AI characters and a memory system. It is headquartered in Copenhagen, was founded in 2022, and reports six employees. [^E1]

## Value Proposition & Features

Kin positions itself as a private personal AI advisory board for decisions involving leadership, relationships, mindset, and wellbeing. Its stated tagline is: “It sees what you can’t. It remembers what you won’t. And it’s there when it matters.” [^E1]

Core features include specialized AI characters that provide different perspectives, plus a memory system intended to preserve relevant context across conversations. [^E1] Kin emphasizes a private, local-first memory engine designed to keep user data under the user’s control. [^E1]

Priority feature set:

- Specialized AI advisor characters [^E1]
- Personal advisory-board format [^E1]
- Persistent memory system [^E1]
- Local-first data architecture [^E1]
- Privacy-oriented user data handling [^E1]
- Guidance for leadership and work [^E1]
- Relationship and mindset guidance [^E1]
- Wellbeing support [^E1]

## Competitive Landscape

### Who it's for, who it's not for

Kin is aimed at individuals seeking ongoing, personalized guidance across work, relationships, mindset, and wellbeing, especially users who value persistent context and privacy. [^E1]

It is not clearly positioned for enterprise workflow automation, general-purpose search, or regulated professional advice; no source establishes those use cases.
