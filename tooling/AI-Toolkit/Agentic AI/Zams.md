In the broader field, an AI agent for business operations is typically defined as software that can **own bounded tasks inside an enterprise system that already holds the source of truth**, taking a goal, deciding its own sequence of steps, invoking tools, and looping until it either finishes or escalates.[1][3][4] Such agents are distinguished from traditional automation by the fact that **no one has hard-coded the exact sequence of actions**; instead, the underlying model chooses the sequence at run time, providing both flexibility and new risk surfaces.[1][4] Platforms that claim to “build AI agents for nuanced business operations” therefore tend to focus on giving teams a controlled way to harness that flexibility—through scoped tools, guardrails, observability, and human-in-the-loop review—while preserving the reliability and governance standards expected of enterprise workflows.[1][2][3][4][5][8][10]
