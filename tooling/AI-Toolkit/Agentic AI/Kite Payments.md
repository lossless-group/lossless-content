Kite Payments is the agentic-payment infrastructure associated with **Kite AI**, focused on giving autonomous software agents identity, controlled spending authority, and stablecoin settlement. Its public materials describe an EVM-compatible chain and payment layer with Agent Passport, programmable limits, and support for agent-to-service payments. [^8g5hmy]

## Value Proposition & Features

Kite targets the problem of allowing [[Vocabulary/Agentic AI|AI Agents]] to transact without handing them unrestricted access to a user’s private keys or funds. Its model combines identity, spending permissions, session-based authorization, auditability, and stablecoin payments. [^yuej2t] [^h8frde]

- **Agent Passport:** Provides an identity and payment-control layer for autonomous agents. [^8g5hmy]
- **Programmable spending limits:** Users can define budgets and conditions before an agent acts. [^yuej2t] [^h8frde]
- **Session-based payments:** Agents can make approved payments through temporary authorization rather than directly controlling a user’s private keys. [^h8frde]
- **[[Vocabulary/Stablecoin|Stablecoin]] settlement:** Agent payments can use stablecoins, while the network also has a native KITE token. [^yuej2t]
- **Service discovery:** Agents can discover paid services through the Kite ecosystem. [^1ixxm5]
- **Micropayments:** The architecture is designed for frequent, low-value payments requiring fast settlement and low fees. [^h8frde]
- **Merchant acceptance:** Passport supports agent-facing payment rails including x402 and [[Sources/Standards-and-Specs/Machine Payments Protocol]], with [[organizations/Stripe|Stripe]] also referenced in coverage. [^yuej2t]
- **On-chain governance and auditability:** Spending permissions and activity are presented as reviewable controls, with the KITE token used for staking and governance. [^yuej2t] [^vy62p1]

# Competitive Landscape

## Who it's for, who it's not for

Kite is aimed at developers and businesses building autonomous agents, paid APIs, digital services, travel workflows, and DeFi automation that need identity, permissioned spending, and stablecoin settlement. [^1ixxm5] [^yuej2t] [^h8frde]

It is not clearly positioned as a conventional consumer checkout product, general-purpose payment processor, or standard enterprise billing platform; its documented focus is agent-controlled transactions and machine-to-machine payments. [^8g5hmy] [^yuej2t]

## Viable Alternatives

- **x402:** An agent-facing payment protocol supported by Kite for service-provider payments. [^1ixxm5] [^yuej2t]
- **Machine Payments Protocol:** Another agent-payment rail supported in Kite’s service-provider documentation. [^1ixxm5] [^yuej2t]
- **Stripe:** A conventional payment platform referenced as part of the broader agent-payment ecosystem, but not equivalent to Kite’s chain-native identity and permission model. [^yuej2t]
- **Tempo:** Referenced alongside Machine Payments Protocol as part of the emerging machine-payment landscape. [^yuej2t]
