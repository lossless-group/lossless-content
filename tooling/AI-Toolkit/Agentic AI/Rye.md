## Value Proposition & Features

Rye is an agentic-commerce infrastructure company founded in 2022 and headquartered in San Francisco. [^E1] Its product enables native in-app shopping by retrieving product price, tax, and shipping information and supporting checkout inside an AI experience. [^E1] This makes Rye relevant to [[concepts/Explainers for AI/Agentic Commerce|Agentic Commerce]] use cases involving universal checkout across fragmented merchants.

- Product, price, tax, and shipping retrieval for arbitrary products. [^E1]
- Native in-app commerce infrastructure. [^E1]
- Checkout without redirecting users away from the host AI experience. [^E1]
- Support for AI agents that discover products and facilitate purchases. [^E1]

# Competitive Landscape

## Who it's for, who it's not for

Rye is aimed at AI-product developers and platforms that want to add product discovery, real-time commerce data, and checkout without sending users to separate merchant websites. [^E1]

It is not evidently intended as a conventional storefront builder, consumer shopping destination, or merchant-only e-commerce platform; no source supplied evidence that it targets those segments.
