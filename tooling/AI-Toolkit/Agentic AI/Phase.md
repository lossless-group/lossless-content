## Value Proposition & Features

Phase is positioned as an open-source secrets-management platform that centralizes application credentials while supporting both human development workflows and AI-agent access. [^E1]

- Secure access to application secrets across development and production. [^E1]
- Centralized management of environment variables and credentials. [^E1]
- Deployment-oriented secret delivery for applications. [^E1]
- Open-source deployment model. [^E1]
- Support for teams and AI agents as secret consumers. [^E1]

### Who it's for, who it's not for

Phase is aimed at software teams that need centralized, secure handling of application secrets across development and production, including workflows in which AI agents require controlled access to credentials. [^E1]

### Viable Alternatives

- **HashiCorp Vault** — A mature, widely adopted secrets-management alternative for organizations needing extensive infrastructure integrations.
- **AWS Secrets Manager** — A natural option for teams already operating primarily within AWS.
