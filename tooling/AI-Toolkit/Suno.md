## Value Proposition & Features

Suno is an AI music-generation service that turns prompts and other inputs into original songs, allowing users to create, share, and discover music. Its stated positioning emphasizes fast creation and accessibility for people with little or no formal music training.[15] [^E1]

- **Text-to-music generation:** Creates complete songs from written descriptions, including lyrics, genre, and style.[15]
- **Multimodal creation:** v6 accepts text, audio, images, and video as starting material.[7]
- **Song editing:** Users can modify a section of an existing song with plain-language instructions.[7]
- **Mashups:** Users can combine elements from multiple sources in one request.[7]
- **Sampling and beat creation:** Users can isolate a sample and build a new beat in one workflow.[7]
- **Exploration model:** v6-wild is designed for more varied and unpredictable musical ideas.[7]
- **Free generation:** v6-mini is available to all users, while v6 and v6-wild are reserved for paid subscribers.[7]
- **Commercial rights:** Paid plans include commercial rights for generated music, according to reporting on Suno’s upgraded plans.[15]

# Competitive Landscape

## Who it's for, who it's not for

Suno is designed for creators who want to turn text, recordings, images, or video into songs, including users without formal musical training.[7] [15] It is also aimed at paid creators seeking editing tools, commercial rights, and more controlled production workflows.[15]

It is less suitable for users who require conventional human-performed recordings, full ownership of training provenance across all historical models, or production workflows outside Suno’s supported generation and editing environment.

## Viable Alternatives

- **Udio:** A direct text-to-music alternative for generating complete songs with AI.
- **Stable Audio:** An AI audio-generation alternative oriented toward producing music and sound from prompts.
- **ElevenLabs Music:** An adjacent generative-audio competitor; reporting places it at a substantially higher valuation than Suno in 2026.[5]
- **Traditional digital audio workstations:** Tools such as Ableton Live, Logic Pro, and FL Studio offer more conventional timeline-based composition, recording, editing, and mixing workflows but do not primarily automate complete song generation from prompts.
