# Value Proposition & Features

Mem.ai is an AI note-taking and workspace product positioned as a “self-organizing workspace builder” for enterprise and small-business users. [^7zkqep] Public metadata describes **Mem Agent** as an AI chief of staff that maintains a living picture of tasks, projects, and goals, then intervenes at useful moments.

- AI-assisted note organization and retrieval.
- Workspace building around tasks, projects, and goals.
- Agent-like assistance for ongoing work.
- Search and contextual recall across workspace content.
- Support for enterprise and small-business customers. [^7zkqep]
