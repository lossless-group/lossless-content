# Value Proposition & Features

Fin is an **AI customer agent platform** (formerly Intercom) that autonomously resolves customer service, sales, and ecommerce queries end-to-end across channels like chat, email, SMS, WhatsApp, social, and voice. [^6pwqxr] [^ko06wq] [^b0l8p3] [^2lnega] It is positioned as the **highest‑performing AI agent in customer experience**, claiming an average **76% end‑to‑end resolution rate** across 12,000–8,000+ businesses and more than 30,000 companies in its broader customer base. [^6pwqxr] [^wjf6g2] [^3g6gwh] [^ab5h2d] [^2lnega] [^9nacuu] [^hbz6s0] Fin uses an outcome‑based pricing model (usually **$0.99 per outcome**) aligned to resolutions and other completed actions rather than seats, and is now owned by Salesforce following a **$3.6B acquisition**. [^7odddx] [^6vxob8] [^d255hq] [^lkb7ci] [^zr0bax] [^b0l8c6] [^7xgs6h] [^wjf6g2] [^3g6gwh] [^yslo2c] [^ab5h2d] [^01oo84] [^zw8kml] [^5xnumi] [^hbz6s0] [^udfy49]

The core product is the **Fin AI Agent**, which answers customer questions, resolves conversations, executes multi‑step procedures, qualifies leads, and hands off to humans with full context across channels. [^7odddx] [^6pwqxr] [^2ede1c] [^b0l8p3] [^i28kxd] [^wjf6g2] [^2lnega] [^3fx9g7] Around this agent, Fin provides a **Fin AI Engine** with tools to Analyze, Train, Test, and Deploy agents, plus performance dashboards, CX scoring, topics analysis, incident detection, and evaluation pipelines adapted from AI research. [^6pwqxr] [^b0l8p3] [^i6awtz] [^g7z06w] [^zkz5tt] [^kk6kyj] The platform also includes **Operator**, an AI operations agent that monitors support interactions and surfaces thousands of improvement opportunities, and **Fin Voice**, a voice-first agent that handles phone calls via interactive demos and production deployments. [^u25krg] [^rt2d6r] [^6jmz36] [^sue6h9] [^zkz5tt] [^0swx8t] [^p6titk] [^5tt5bq] [^272ah5] [^7283n0]

### Key Features (5–8, in priority order)

1. **Autonomous multichannel customer agent** – Fin resolves customer conversations end-to-end over **live chat, email, SMS, WhatsApp, social channels (Facebook, Instagram), Slack, and voice**, positioning itself as “more than a chatbot” and a full customer agent. [^7odddx] [^6pwqxr] [^kehe8c] [^b0l8p3] [^i28kxd] [^wjf6g2] [^3g6gwh] [^ab5h2d] [^2lnega] [^r5cbhh] [^3fx9g7]

2. **Outcome‑based AI pricing and usage** – Usage is metered at **$0.99 per outcome** for resolutions, procedure handoffs, and disqualifications, and **$9.99 per lead qualification**, typically with a **50‑outcome monthly minimum** when running on external helpdesks. [^7odddx] [^6vxob8] [^d255hq] [^lkb7ci] [^zr0bax] [^b0l8c6] [^7xgs6h] [^on7ino] [^8fpsm8] [^ng6y7x] [^xkjs6b] [^83z1i8] [^tj49lg] Only one outcome is billed per conversation, even if multiple actions occur. [^lkb7ci] [^zr0bax] [^7xgs6h] [^on7ino] [^ng6y7x] [^7ca9co] [^tj49lg]

3. **Fin AI Engine (Analyze, Train, Test, Deploy)** – The Fin AI Engine lets teams **Analyze** performance (CX Score, resolution/involvement rates, topics), **Train** behavior and tone using multi-source knowledge and tasks, **Test** answers via batch tests and previews, and **Deploy** across web, phone, and other channels. [^6pwqxr] [^gawb74] [^b0l8p3] [^g7z06w] [^zkz5tt] [^z5dg19] [^tdj6r2] [^kk6kyj]

4. **Customer Experience (CX) Score & performance dashboards** – Fin introduces a **CX Score** with “5x more coverage than CSAT,” measuring the quality of every conversation to understand how customers feel, paired with dashboards that unify resolution rate, involvement rate, and CX score. [^6pwqxr] [^gawb74] [^b0l8p3] [^g7z06w] [^kk6kyj]

5. **Topics Explorer & conversation analytics** – Using AI, Topics Explorer clusters conversations into topics and subtopics to reveal trends, high‑effort issues, and drivers of support volume, reducing manual tagging and analysis time. [^6pwqxr] [^gawb74] [^b0l8p3]

6. **Operator AI agent for operations** – **Operator** is an AI agent that continuously monitors AI‑handled and human‑handled support conversations, identifies recurring issues, and recommends operational fixes, with about **20,000 improvements** reportedly generated. [^u25krg] [^rt2d6r] [^zkz5tt] [^0swx8t] [^p6titk] [^kk6kyj]

7. **Fin Voice (AI phone agent)** – Fin Voice allows teams to assign a phone number and have Fin answer inbound calls, simulate voice calls via a web preview panel, choose voices, configure greetings, and monitor voice conversations with transcripts and metrics. [^uw2m4y] [^rt2d6r] [^6jmz36] [^sue6h9] [^5tt5bq] [^z7hth6] [^4d2iag] [^272ah5] [^7283n0]

8. **Startup Pack and ecosystem integrations** – The **Fin Startup Pack** bundles helpdesk, Fin AI Agent, Operator credits, and 55+ partner deals worth over $500K for startups under 50 employees, while Fin also deploys as an AI layer on top of helpdesks like Salesforce, HubSpot, Zendesk, Freshworks, and Zoho. [^74yoz9] [^vg1440] [^7odddx] [^ko06wq] [^da00g2] [^7hkpsi] [^zkz5tt] [^wjf6g2] [^65g8ae] [^z5dg19] [^tdj6r2]

# History and Origin Story

Fin began as **Intercom**, a customer messaging and helpdesk company founded in 2011 by Eoghan McCabe, Des Traynor, Ciaran Lee, and David Barrett in Dublin and San Francisco. [^k23p57] [^brval3] [^tq1m4j] [^96r3tl] [^ko06wq] [^lktei5] Intercom launched the Fin AI Agent product in 2023 as an autonomous support agent resolving customer conversations across digital channels, and by 2026 the company **rebranded itself to Fin** on May 12, 2026 as it bet the business on AI agents rather than traditional helpdesk software. [^tq1m4j] [^96r3tl] [^ab5h2d] [^h33b0u] [^0x3qtc] [^ebip89] [^5xnumi] [^hbz6s0] On June 15, 2026 Salesforce agreed to acquire Fin for about **$3.6B**, citing Fin’s Apex model suite, 76% resolution rate, and >30,000 customers, with the deal closing on September 10, 2026 and Fin continuing to operate as a customer agent platform within Salesforce AI Labs. # Competitive Landscape

## Who it’s for, who it’s not for

Fin is built for **digital‑first support, sales, and ecommerce teams** that want a high‑performance autonomous agent across multiple channels, particularly SaaS and online businesses already running or willing to adopt Intercom or an existing helpdesk that Fin can sit on top of. [^7odddx] [^ko06wq] [^d255hq] [^z1tq9c] [^wpdgq5] [^d45xjs] [^8phve3] [^tzhos5] [^qz7qvi] [^94ztc0] [^j5dckd] [^wjf6g2] [^tj49lg] Its outcome‑based pricing and strong analytics tools fit teams that measure customer experience rigorously and value the ability to deflect a large share of conversations (roughly 75–76%) while still orchestrating procedures and handoffs. [^7odddx] [^6vxob8] [^d255hq] [^z1tq9c] [^j5dckd] [^ekge5c] [^c7dgzn] [^wjf6g2] [^0x3qtc] [^ebip89] [^5xnumi] [^hbz6s0]

It is less suited to **very small, budget‑constrained teams** needing ultra‑low‑cost or free chatbots, organizations locked into other ecosystems with their own AI agents (e.g., deeply standardized on Zendesk or Salesforce Agentforce without appetite for a separate agent), or companies whose primary need is custom, quote‑only voice or niche channels rather than a general multichannel agent. [^955bix] [^qhfpm5] [^z1tq9c] [^d45xjs] [^8phve3] [^tzhos5] [^qz7qvi] [^94ztc0] [^j5dckd] [^n1sr0i] [^da00g2] [^hwfv9t] Teams uncomfortable with per‑resolution billing or who require very low, predictable monthly AI costs may prefer flat‑fee or credit‑based alternatives. [^955bix] [^z1tq9c] [^d45xjs] [^8phve3] [^tzhos5] [^j5dckd] [^hwfv9t] [^tj49lg]

## Viable Alternatives

- **Zendesk AI Agents** – Native AI agents layered onto Zendesk’s ticketing suite, best for enterprises already standardized on Zendesk and wanting AI inside their existing workflows rather than a separate agent layer. [^qhfpm5] [^z1tq9c] [^fegs0m] [^d45xjs] [^8phve3] [^tzhos5] [^qz7qvi] [^94ztc0] [^j5dckd] [^m0s656] [^tj49lg]  
- **Salesforce Agentforce** – Salesforce’s own agent platform built on live Salesforce data, suitable for enterprises deeply invested in Salesforce CRM that want tight native integration with their sales and service stacks. [^qhfpm5] [^z1tq9c] [^tzhos5] [^qz7qvi] [^94ztc0] [^j5dckd] [^n1sr0i]  
- **Ada** – Enterprise multichannel AI customer service platform emphasizing formal AI assurance and operating across chat, voice, email, and social, serving large Zendesk and Salesforce deployments. [^955bix] [^z1tq9c] [^fegs0m] [^tzhos5] [^n1sr0i] [^da00g2]  
- **Freshworks Freddy AI / Freshdesk AI** – AI layered on Freshdesk ticketing for mid‑market support teams seeking lower entry prices and tight integration with Freshdesk rather than a separate AI vendor. [^qhfpm5] [^z1tq9c] [^d45xjs] [^tzhos5] [^j5dckd] [^83z1i8]  
- **eesel AI / Aissist.io** – Agentic or agentic‑lite AI layers that sit on top of existing stacks, often offering lower per‑ticket rates and strong simulation tools for proving resolution rates before deployment. [^955bix] [^jidez3] [^j5dckd]
