## Value Proposition & Features

Sanctum AI is a private, on-device AI assistant founded in 2023 and based in Brooklyn, United States. It runs full-featured large language models locally, with encrypted interactions stored on the user’s device and support for operation without an internet connection. [^E1]

Its core positioning fits the **local-first AI workspace** category: desktop AI that prioritizes privacy, local storage, and device-side inference over cloud processing. This makes it relevant to users evaluating [[concepts/Explainers for AI/AI Interfaces|AI Interfaces]] that keep sensitive conversations and data under local control. [^E1]

- On-device execution of LLMs. [^E1]
- Encrypted interactions. [^E1]
- Local storage of user data. [^E1]
- Offline operation. [^E1]
- Desktop-oriented AI workspace. [^E1]
# Competitive Landscape

## Who it's for, who it's not for

Sanctum is suited to privacy-conscious individuals and small teams that want desktop AI assistance while keeping model interactions and stored data on their own devices. [^E1]

It is less suited to organizations that require centralized cloud administration, shared enterprise workspaces, or access to cloud-only models and services; no reliable source confirms those capabilities.

## Viable Alternatives

- **AnythingLLM** — A comparable local-first AI workspace for users who want to work with local models and private documents.
- **Jan.ai** — A desktop-oriented local-LLM assistant with a similar privacy and on-device emphasis.
- **Ollama** — A local model runtime that can serve as an underlying alternative for users prioritizing local inference over a packaged workspace.
- **LM Studio** — A desktop application for downloading and running language models locally.
- **GPT4All** — A local AI application focused on running language models on personal hardware.
