ChatGPT is OpenAI’s conversational AI platform for answering questions, writing, image creation, work completion, and coding. [^E1]

## Value Proposition & Features

ChatGPT combines conversational interaction with general-purpose productivity tools, positioning it as an AI interface for both individual and organizational work. [^E1]

- **Conversation and question answering:** Users can interact with the system conversationally to obtain explanations, drafts, and assistance. [^E1]
- **Writing and editing:** The platform supports creating and revising written content. [^E1]
- **Image generation:** Users can create images within ChatGPT. [^E1]
- **Coding:** ChatGPT supports software-development tasks and code generation. [^E1]
- **Work assistance:** The product is designed to help users complete work, not only exchange messages. [^E1]
- **Custom GPTs:** Paid plans provide access to custom GPT capabilities. [^hha50j]
- **Deep research and tools:** Higher tiers provide expanded access to deep research, uploads, memory, context, and tool-enabled messages. [^hha50j]
- **Business and enterprise use:** ChatGPT offers dedicated Business and Enterprise plans for organizational deployment. [^g8pj60]

## Recent Developments

The available results show a product direction centered on broader tool access, expanded memory and context, advanced reasoning models, image generation, deep research, and Codex access across paid tiers. [^hha50j] A separate ChatGPT feature page describes “dots,” always-on agents available to eligible Pro, Business Premium, and Enterprise users in selected markets. [^ugzko9]

# Competitive Landscape

## Who it's for, who it's not for

ChatGPT is aimed at individuals and organizations seeking conversational assistance for questions, writing, image creation, work, coding, research, and related productivity tasks. [^E1] [^g8pj60]

## Viable Alternatives

- **Claude:** A competing general-purpose conversational AI assistant for writing, analysis, and coding.
- **Google Gemini:** A competing multimodal AI assistant integrated with Google’s ecosystem.
- **Microsoft Copilot:** A competing AI assistant focused on Microsoft productivity and enterprise workflows.
- **Meta AI:** A consumer-oriented conversational AI alternative integrated into Meta products.
- **Perplexity:** An answer-engine alternative emphasizing web-grounded responses and citations.
