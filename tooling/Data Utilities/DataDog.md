## Value Proposition & Features

Datadog provides a [[Unified Observability]] and security platform for monitoring infrastructure, applications, logs, databases, networks, cloud services, and user experience in one environment. [^E1] It is positioned to reduce tool fragmentation, accelerate incident investigation, and give engineering, operations, security, and business teams a shared view of system health. [^md27ox]

Core capabilities include infrastructure monitoring, application-performance monitoring, log management, real-user and synthetic monitoring, database monitoring, cloud security, incident management, software-delivery monitoring, and cloud-cost management. [^md27ox]

- **Unified observability:** Correlates telemetry from servers, containers, databases, applications, networks, and third-party services. [^E1] [^md27ox]
- **Infrastructure monitoring:** Tracks hosts, containers, cloud resources, and infrastructure health. [^md27ox]
- **Application performance monitoring:** Helps trace requests and diagnose application bottlenecks. [^md27ox]
- **Log management:** Collects, searches, routes, and analyzes logs at scale. [^md27ox]
- **AI-agent observability:** Monitors the performance, quality, security, and cost of AI agents and LLM applications. [^md27ox]
- **Security operations:** Bits Investigation can investigate alerts, identify root causes, and recommend or take action. [^md27ox] Culminate’s technology supports automated alert investigation and response. [^E3]
- **Telemetry control:** Observability Pipelines provides OpenTelemetry support and controls how telemetry is collected, routed, and retained. [^md27ox]
- **Developer and incident workflows:** Connects monitoring data to alerting, collaboration, incident response, and software-delivery workflows. [^md27ox]

## Competitive Landscape

### Who it’s for, who it’s not for

Datadog is suited to organizations operating distributed cloud, hybrid, containerized, or microservices environments that need infrastructure, application, log, security, user-experience, and AI-workload telemetry in a shared platform. [^E1] [^md27ox] It is particularly relevant to engineering, platform, DevOps, security, and operations teams managing large-scale systems.

It is less suited to small deployments needing only basic host metrics, teams seeking a predominantly self-hosted open-source stack, or buyers whose primary requirement is a single narrowly focused monitoring function rather than an integrated platform.

### Viable Alternatives

- **[[Tooling/AI-Toolkit/AI Infrastructure/ScienceLogic|ScienceLogic]]:** Enterprise IT operations and event-correlation alternative for organizations prioritizing broad infrastructure context.
- **[[content-areas/AI-Factories-Datacenters/Organizations/Virtana|Virtana]]:** Enterprise observability alternative emphasizing broad system context and complex deployment environments.
- **[[Tooling/Software Development/Developer Experience/Logfire|Logfire]]:** More focused observability option for teams emphasizing application and AI-agent telemetry.
- **Grafana Labs:** Open-source-oriented observability alternative built around metrics, logs, traces, dashboards, and ecosystem integrations.
- **Dynatrace:** Enterprise observability and application-performance alternative competing across infrastructure, application, and security monitoring. [^zlqvg2]
