# Framework

## Value Proposition & Features

Framework makes modular, repairable, and upgradeable laptops and desktops, positioning user-serviceability and component reuse as alternatives to sealed consumer computers. The product range includes Framework Laptop models 13, 16, and 12, plus Framework Desktop and a Marketplace for parts and ecosystem components. [^E1]

- **Framework Desktop:** A compact 4.5-liter Mini-ITX desktop powered by AMD Ryzen AI Max processors.
- **Modularity:** The Desktop’s Mainboard uses the standard Mini-ITX form factor and can be installed in the Framework case, a half-width rack tray, or another ATX-compatible case. [^tc6skk]
- **Local AI capability:** The 192GB configuration uses AMD Ryzen AI Max PRO 495+, with 16 Zen 5 CPU cores and 40-core Radeon 8065S graphics. [^tc6skk]
- **Memory bandwidth:** The 192GB model uses a 256-bit memory bus delivering 273GB/s bandwidth. [^tc6skk]
- **Expansion:** The system includes two M.2 SSD slots, a PCIe x4 slot, USB4, USB-A, DisplayPort 2.1, HDMI 2.1, Ethernet, Wi‑Fi 7, and front Expansion Card slots. [^tc6skk]
- **Repairability and reuse:** Framework’s computers are designed around replaceable modules, repair guidance, and component reuse across generations. [^E1]
- **Operating-system choice:** Framework Desktop is offered as a DIY Edition, a pre-built Fedora version, or as the Mainboard alone. [^tc6skk]

# History and Origin Story

Framework was founded in 2020 and focuses on modular laptops and PCs designed for performance, repairability, and upgradability. Its product portfolio has expanded from the Framework Laptop line to include the Framework Desktop and a Marketplace for replacement parts and ecosystem components. [^E1]

# Competitive Landscape

## Who it's for, who it's not for

Framework is aimed at buyers who value repairability, upgradeability, modular expansion, component reuse, and local AI computing in a compact desktop. The 192GB configuration is particularly relevant to users running larger AI models locally. [^tc6skk]

It is less suited to buyers seeking a conventional sealed desktop, a fully documented lowest-price configuration, or a system optimized primarily for conventional discrete-GPU gaming; no source in the available results establishes Framework Desktop as a gaming-first product.

## Viable Alternatives

- **Standard Mini-ITX desktops:** Alternative buyers can assemble a compact system around conventional Mini-ITX parts, but they may not offer Framework’s integrated modular ecosystem.
- **Conventional prebuilt mini PCs:** These can provide simpler purchasing and broader vendor choice, but the available sources do not establish equivalent repairability or upgradeability.
- **Rack-mounted Mini-ITX systems:** Framework’s Mainboard can be installed in a half-width rack tray, making rack-oriented builds a direct alternative use case. [^tc6skk]
- **Other local-AI desktops:** Systems with higher memory capacity or discrete GPUs may be alternatives for users prioritizing AI throughput, although no comparative benchmark source was found.
