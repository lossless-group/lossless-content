## Value Proposition & Features

Peak Power Inc. provides end-to-end battery-energy-storage development and energy-optimization solutions for large energy users. Its stated outcomes are lower electricity costs, additional energy-market revenue, and progress toward sustainability goals. [^E1]

- **Battery storage development:** Project development for battery-energy-storage systems, including end-to-end delivery. [^E1]
- **Energy optimization:** Software and services optimize energy use and storage operations. [^E1]
- **Peak forecasting:** Forecasting identifies periods of grid or electricity-demand peaks. [^E1]
- **Energy-market intelligence:** Market data supports operational and revenue decisions. [^E1]
- **Energy management services:** Services help large energy users manage consumption and storage. [^E1]
- **Grid peak notifications:** Notifications alert users to likely peak-demand periods. [^E1]
- **Cost reduction:** Optimization is intended to reduce electricity costs. [^E1]
- **Revenue generation:** Storage can be used to pursue additional energy-market revenue. [^E1]
