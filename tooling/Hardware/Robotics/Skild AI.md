## Value Proposition & Features

Skild AI builds “general purpose [[content-areas/Physical AI/Robotic Intelligence]] software and solutions” intended to solve robotics problems across tasks and environments rather than for one narrowly defined robot application. [^E1] Its positioning fits the emerging **Physical AI** category, where reusable intelligence layers support multiple kinds of robots.

- General-purpose robotic intelligence software [^E1]
- Solutions aimed at robotics problems “in full generality” [^E1]
- Software-oriented approach rather than a publicly described robot-hardware product [^E1]
- Pittsburgh-based robotics development [^E1]
- Intended for broad deployment across robotic systems; detailed supported platforms were not available in the retrieved material

## History and Origin Story

Skild AI was founded in 2023 and is headquartered in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. [^E1] The company emerged from stealth in July 2024 with a $300 million Series A at a $1.5 billion valuation. [^F2] The available sources do not identify the founders or provide a fuller founding narrative.
