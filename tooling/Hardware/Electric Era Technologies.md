## Retrieved sources

The supplied retrieved sources identify Electric Era as a Seattle-based EV-charging infrastructure company founded in 2020. The company combines battery storage, intelligent software control, and deployment services for high-power charging sites. [^m0zzow] [^t5lypz]

# Value Proposition & Features

Electric Era targets retailers and charging networks that need reliable, economically viable DC fast charging where grid capacity is constrained or demand charges are high. Its system combines grid power, on-site batteries, and software that dynamically controls energy flows to reduce operating costs while maintaining charging performance. [^m0zzow] [^vkfo6w]

Core features:

- **Battery-backed DC fast charging:** On-site storage supplements grid power, allowing chargers to deliver high output without requiring equivalent grid capacity. [^m0zzow] [^vkfo6w]
- **Energy-management software:** The system controls how power is drawn from the grid and battery and distributed to vehicles. [^t5lypz]
- **Adaptive grid limits:** Software lowers grid draw during slow periods and flexes it upward during demand surges while maintaining hard limits on peak demand. [^vkfo6w]
- **Demand- and capacity-charge reduction:** Electric Era’s EMS predicted all five PJM 5CP events in a reported October 2026 deployment, reducing grid draw during coincident-peak periods. [^t5lypz]
- **Rapid deployment:** The company provides vertically integrated hardware, software, and deployment expertise for charging sites. [^m0zzow] [^t5lypz]
- **Reliability targets:** Electric Era reports a guarantee of more than 98% per-port uptime and more than 85% first-plug-in success for the UDF deployment. [^m0zzow]
- **Retail-site integration:** The company positions charging as a way for retailers to generate customer dwell time and support broader retail economics. [^jb3ynj]

# Competitive Landscape

## Who it's for, who it's not for

Electric Era is for retailers, convenience-store operators, travel stops, and charging networks deploying high-power EV charging where grid upgrades, demand charges, or capacity charges threaten site economics. [^m0zzow] [^vkfo6w] [^t5lypz]

It is not positioned as a consumer charging app, a residential charger, or a simple hardware-only supplier; its offering depends on commercial-site deployment, battery storage, and integrated energy-management software. [^m0zzow] [^t5lypz]

## Viable Alternatives

- **Tesla Supercharger:** A vertically integrated fast-charging network and hardware ecosystem; relevant where an operator prefers an established network model rather than a site-specific battery-backed deployment.
- **ChargePoint:** A commercial charging-infrastructure alternative for organizations seeking charging hardware, software, and network services.
- **ABB E-mobility:** A charging-hardware alternative for commercial and fleet deployments.
- **FreeWire:** A battery-integrated charging alternative for locations with limited grid capacity.
- **Electrify America:** A large public fast-charging network alternative for operators seeking network access rather than Electric Era’s integrated site-infrastructure model.
