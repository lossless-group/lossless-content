## Value Proposition & Features

Circuit is an Austin-based company founded in 2024 that provides purpose-built AI solutions for manufacturing and service teams. Its stated use cases include helping teams quote, troubleshoot, configure products, and support customers when scarce technical expertise cannot be present everywhere. [^E1]

- **Quoting:** Supports manufacturing and service workflows that require accurate technical and commercial responses. [^E1]
- **Guided product configuration:** Helps users configure products with structured guidance. [^E1]
- **Troubleshooting:** Applies organizational and technical knowledge to service and support problems. [^E1]
- **Technical enablement:** Makes expert knowledge available to broader teams. [^E1]
- **Field service support:** Targets field-service workflows across industrial and service settings. [^E1]
- **Industry specialization:** Focuses on automotive, trade, field service, and home and commercial services. [^E1]

# Competitive Landscape

## Who it's for, who it's not for

Circuit is aimed at manufacturing and service organizations that need to scale expert knowledge across quoting, configuration, troubleshooting, field service, and technical support. [^E1]

It is not clearly positioned for general-purpose consumer AI, generic office productivity, or organizations without technically complex products and service workflows.

## Viable Alternatives

- **[[Microsoft Dynamics]] 365 Field Service:** A broader enterprise platform for field-service management and technician workflows.
- **[[Tooling/Products/Salesforce|Salesforce]] Service Cloud:** A general customer-service platform that can support service operations and knowledge management.
- **[[Tooling/Enterprise Jobs-to-be-Done/ServiceNow|ServiceNow]] Customer Service Management:** An enterprise workflow platform for service processes and support operations.
