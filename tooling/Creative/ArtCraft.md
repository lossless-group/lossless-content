# Value Proposition & Features

ArtCraft is a suite of seventeen native, open-source creative and productivity applications built in pure Rust, designed to provide local, [[free alternatives to established creative software. [^a3vlbi] Its applications run on users’ own files without cloud round-trips, while **ArtCraft Studio** is described as its AI image-and-video application. [^a3vlbi]

- **PhotoCraft:** Native image editing with layers, masks, adjustment layers, filters, smart objects, layer styles, brushes, and PSD/PSB support. [^4dub5w]
- **FilmCraft:** Professional [[Vocabulary/Video Editing|Video Editing]] with source and program monitors, timeline editing, trimming modes, color workflows, effects, transitions, audio mixing, and native codecs. [^y6a5vo]
- **Vector Craft:** Open-source [[concepts/Vector Art Software|Vector Art Software]] illustration in pure [[Tooling/Software Development/Programming Languages/Rust|Rust]]. [^a3vlbi]
- **Light Craft:** Local photo-library management and RAW development. [^a3vlbi]
- **PDF Craft:** PDF reading, organization, combination, splitting, and security tools. [^a3vlbi]
- **DeckCraft:** Presentation authoring with slide masters, themes, vector shapes, transitions, animations, presenter view, PPTX support, and MCP/CLI automation. [^pvmxt2]
- **GridCraft:** Spreadsheet editing with XLSX compatibility, more than 500 calculation functions, charts, PivotTables, and agent-oriented automation. [^4iqqql]
- **Cross-platform delivery:** Several applications are listed for macOS, Windows, Linux, and the web, with free and open-source distribution. [^4dub5w] [^pvmxt2] [^4iqqql]

# Competitive Landscape

## Who it's for, who it's not for

ArtCraft is aimed at users seeking free, open-source, locally run alternatives for image editing, video, illustration, photography, documents, presentations, spreadsheets, audio, CAD, and related creative workflows. [^a3vlbi] Its platform breadth and native Rust implementation may appeal to technically inclined creators, open-source users, and people who prefer local files over cloud services. [^a3vlbi]

It is not an obvious fit for organizations that require a documented enterprise vendor, mature commercial support, or established production ecosystems; the retrieved official pages describe several applications as early builds or in development. [^a3vlbi] [^4dub5w]

## Viable Alternatives

- **Adobe Creative Cloud:** Commercial alternative for integrated image, illustration, video, layout, and motion workflows.
- **Affinity:** Commercial desktop alternative for image editing, vector design, and page layout.
- **DaVinci Resolve:** Established alternative for professional video editing, color, visual effects, and audio.
- **LibreOffice:** Open-source alternative for word processing, presentations, and spreadsheets.
- **Blender:** Open-source alternative for 3D creation, animation, rendering, and compositing.
