VEED is a London-founded, browser-based AI video creation and editing platform for creators, marketers, and small teams. Its product combines timeline editing with automated captions, translation, avatars, [[concepts/Explainers for AI/Text-to-Speech|Text-to-Speech]], dubbing, screen recording, templates, [[Vocabulary/Public Brand Kits|Public Brand Kits]], and AI video generation. [^E1] [^rq9mc7]

## Value Proposition & Features

VEED’s core value proposition is to let users create, edit, localize, and publish videos without installing desktop software. The platform is aimed at turning prompts, scripts, recordings, or existing footage into social, marketing, educational, and business videos. [^E1] [^w8pdjg]

- **Browser-based editor:** Timeline editing, templates, [[Stock Media]], screen recording, teleprompter, and export tools are available through the web. [^w8pdjg]
- **AI captions and translation:** Automatic subtitles, subtitle-file downloads, and multilingual translation support are included in paid plans. [^py0qt7]
- **AI generation:** Users can generate videos, voices, avatars, images, and text-to-video content from prompts. [^E1]
- **Avatars and lip sync:** AI presenters, speaking photos, voice cloning, and lip-sync workflows support presenter-led or “faceless” videos. [^rq9mc7] [^w8pdjg]
- **Audio and voice tools:** Text-to-speech, dubbing, voice generation, and audio cleanup are available as part of the AI toolkit. [^E1] [^w8pdjg]
- **Brand controls:** Brand kits store logos, colors, and fonts for repeatable branded production. [^w8pdjg]
- **Social-video workflows:** Templates, clips, captions, and platform-oriented editing support short-form content production. [^w8pdjg]
- **Developer capabilities:** VEED also offers APIs for capabilities such as lip sync, subtitles, and background removal. [^cpfs3x]

## History and Origin Story

Sabba Keynejad and Tim Mamedov founded VEED in 2018. [^als2jg] The company developed as a remote-first web-video platform and later expanded from browser editing into AI generation, avatars, translation, dubbing, and developer APIs. [^rq9mc7] VEED is headquartered in London, with the retrieved company data listing 152 employees and $35 million in total funding. [^E1]

## Competitive Landscape

### Who it's for, who it's not for

VEED is best suited to creators, social-media teams, marketers, educators, and small businesses that need fast, collaborative video production, captions, localization, and branded templates in a browser. [^E1] [^w8pdjg]

It is less suited to editors requiring advanced desktop-grade finishing, extensive offline workflows, or highly specialized post-production control; the available sources do not document VEED as a replacement for professional nonlinear editors.

### Viable Alternatives

- **[[Tooling/Creative/OpusClip|OpusClip]]** — Focuses on automatically turning long-form footage into short social clips.
- **[[Tooling/AI-Toolkit/Generative AI/CapCut|CapCut]]** — Consumer-oriented alternative for quick social-video editing, templates, captions, and platform-native creation.
- **Adobe Premiere Pro** — Desktop professional editor for deeper timeline, effects, color, and audio control.
- **[[Tooling/Creative/Descript|Descript]]** — Text-oriented editing for video and podcasts, with transcription-led workflows.
- **[[Tooling/Creative/Canva|Canva]]** — Template-driven design and lightweight video creation for marketing teams.
