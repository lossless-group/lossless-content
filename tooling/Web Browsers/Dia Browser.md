# Value Proposition & Features

Dia is an AI-native desktop browser from The Browser Company, positioned as “the browser for your best work.” [^v3spzi] It combines conventional browsing with contextual AI that can work from open pages, tabs, and connected work applications. [^v3spzi]

- **Contextual answers:** Ask Dia questions about the page currently open or bring multiple tabs into a conversation. [^zp5qq2] [^v3spzi]
- **Connected-work context:** Dia’s AI can use information from services including Gmail, Notion, Slack, Microsoft Teams, Google Workspace, and GitHub. [^v3spzi]
- **Browser organization:** The browser includes profiles, tab groups, split view, picture-in-picture, and cross-device synchronization. [^v3spzi]
- **Privacy and browsing controls:** Dia includes built-in ad and tracking blocking. [^v3spzi]
- **Arc migration:** Dia can import tabs, bookmarks, history, passwords, cookies, and selected extensions from Arc. [^zp5qq2]
- **AI-assisted research and output:** The product supports reports, decks, meeting preparation, and a “Morning Brief” workflow. [^zp5qq2]
- **Integrated search and tools:** Dia’s built-in tools are preloaded for faster Chat responses, and its browsing engine incorporates Chrome’s Perplexity search integration. [^drtd5p]

# Competitive Landscape

## Who it's for, who it's not for

Dia is aimed at knowledge workers who want browser-based AI grounded in current pages, tabs, and workplace applications. [^zp5qq2] [^v3spzi] It is especially relevant to Arc users seeking a successor that preserves profiles, tab organization, and imported browsing data. [^zp5qq2] [^7k2o9b]

It is less suitable for users who want a conventional browser without AI subscriptions, or who prefer to keep AI separate from browsing and work applications. [^zp5qq2] [^h7adyn]

## Viable Alternatives

- **Zen Browser:** A Gecko-based browser frequently recommended for users seeking an Arc-like experience without switching to Dia. [^7k2o9b]
- **Conventional Chromium browsers:** Suitable for users who prioritize broad compatibility and browser functionality over integrated AI workflows.

## Competitor Table

| Competitor | Description |
