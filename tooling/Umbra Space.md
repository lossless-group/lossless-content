## Value Proposition & Features

Umbra designs, builds, tests, and operates space-based sensing systems, centered on an agile Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) constellation that delivers high-quality imaging data and persistent customer access. Its target applications include defense, weather, environmental monitoring, and scientific research. [^E1]

Core features:

- **Commercial SAR imagery:** High-quality radar imaging intended for customers requiring observation independent of daylight and cloud cover. [^E1]
- **Agile constellation:** Space-based sensing infrastructure designed to support responsive collection and persistent access. [^E1]
- **Mission solutions:** Umbra combines spacecraft operations with data delivery and customer-specific sensing requirements. [^E1]
- **Defense and intelligence applications:** The company serves government customers including the National Reconnaissance Office. [^E1]
- **Weather and environmental monitoring:** SAR data supports weather-related, environmental, and scientific use cases. [^E1]
- **High-volume delivery:** Umbra positions its service around delivering SAR data at high volumes. [^E1]
- **SpaceTech infrastructure:** The company fits within [[concepts/Market-Categories/SpaceTech|SpaceTech]] through its combination of spacecraft, sensing hardware, and data services. [^E1]
